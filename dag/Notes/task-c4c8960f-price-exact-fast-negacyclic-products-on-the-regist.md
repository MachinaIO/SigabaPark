# Task summary: Price exact fast negacyclic products on the registered tapes for the shared-matrix CBD pad generator

Folding the public most-significant bits only when it reduces the centered l1 norm bounds every integer output coefficient by 32768. The transform and correction preserve the original product modulo 256. Five proof solutions and the accompanying replay concern this product. A complete garbling schedule, registered machine clocks for the full scheme and a reduction for general CircuitSAT remain open.
