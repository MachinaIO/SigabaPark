# Task summary: Compress higher-degree public constraints through their exact constant Boolean derivatives

Task `23f5f961-c6c6-4a4d-a20e-ffbe286c43f6` (design / expand), goal `e30dec5ceb4bf12756c8bfa37ac656e9185a83c224ed8b452446988fc9d1832b`, outcome `verified_partial`. Mathematical status comes from the records, not from this note.

The guarded pivot compiler uses identities on the common zero set of the other constraints to remove a coordinate and recover every accepting parent witness. A degree-four, 4096-gate example admits sixteen such eliminations (128 to 112 bits), although the derivative is not determined by the whole syndrome in the stated direction. Four verified proof solutions and the replay accompany the note. General registered frontend clocks, a universal fallback and the quantum WE reduction remain open.
