### The complete local polytope

Index a frequency by bits (A,X,Y,Z), least significant first. Homogeneous validity is XY=A(A+Z) over F2. The six invalid frequency integers are 1,3,5,6,14,15. They are the disjoint nonzero parts of the frequency planes generated by (1,14) and (3,5). In the dual mask coordinates U,V (the two evaluations on each generating pair), the six forbidden Fourier coefficients vanish exactly when both two-bit marginals are uniform. Thus four times the probability matrix is doubly stochastic. The rational polytope is precisely Birkhoff's order-four polytope with this scaling. Its 24 vertices have mass exactly 1/4 at the four pairs (u,σ(u)). Every permutation on two bits is affine: σ(u+u')=σ(u)+σ(u')+σ(0). Thus all vertices are translates of six affine-graph directions. Their Fourier supports are the six maximal allowed linear planes, with four possible sign translations per plane. Four directions see two unit-anchor legal NAND rows each; two see no such row. This includes every non-minimax vertex. The same linear equations and Birkhoff theorem apply over the reals. The Lean classification is over rationals.

### A joint law outside every correlated mixture of local planes

For g cells, draw independent fair x_i,r_i and a vector d uniformly conditioned on XOR_i d_i=F(x). Output U_i=(x_i,r_i), V_i=(x_i,r_i+d_i), with the first bit low, all sums binary. Given all other cells, the conditional law at cell i is a uniform permutation graph: x_i,r_i are fair and d_i is then determined by x_i and the fixed cells. Hence all joint Fourier coefficients with any forbidden local frequency vanish, without assuming independence. If g=2 and F(x)=x_1 x_2, a positive mixture of product permutation graphs would have to preserve the common low bit in every component of positive weight. Each hidden permutation would give d_i=h_i(x_i), forcing x_1 x_2=h_1(x_1)+h_2(x_2) on all four inputs. Their four-way XOR contradicts this. This is a classical jointly sampled law and uses no cryptographic assumption.

At local frequency pair (p_i,q_i), averaging r enforces equal high bits on p_i and q_i; averaging the d hyperplane enforces that these high bits are a common δ across cells. For δ=0 one gets coefficient one only when p_i=q_i lies in {0,1} everywhere. For δ=1 every p_i,q_i lies in {2,3}, and the coefficient equals the normalized Walsh coefficient of the sign of F at the vector of low-bit differences. These two global affine systems admit only legal local frequencies. Translating x changes only the Fourier signs. Changing decoding character based on the witness is already covered by the full spectrum.

For F=x_1 x_2 and g≥2 the coefficient at all-zero low differences is 1/2 independently of g. In the original coordinates rows 9 and 11 both have identical pairs p=q in {2,3}, so every word consisting exclusively of those legal rows has bias exactly 1/2: distinct cells can make different choices. This improves the independently multiplied phase for these witnesses. With a uniform wiring-perpendicular mask and an anchor-message shift, 593 independent packets and majority recover the anchor bit on those witnesses with error Σ_{j≤296} binom(593,j)3^j/4^593 <2^-128. At g=4096 this is 9,715,712 raw packet bits. A general NAND trace can use the missing rows.

### Why affine covers matter

For a homogeneous linear wiring space W, adding uniform noise from its perpendicular deletes frequencies outside W; translating by the payload anchor flips the signs of exactly the odd frequencies in W. For a mixture of product plane-coset laws, condition only on the plane flags and translations (or a sufficient seed leaving the uniform coset masks unsampled). If no odd element of W lies in the product of their legal support planes, the two conditional distributions coincide. Otherwise an odd candidate gives a valid circuit witness. Projected Gaussian elimination decides this linear feasibility; it also recovers a trace. Thus, with arbitrary efficiently samplable correlations of such flags, any payload acceptance advantage ε implies a randomized one-sided SAT procedure succeeding at least ε on that instance. False instances never pass. Sampling and elimination time must be charged. This is an algorithmic implication for those issuers, not a finite-cap impossibility for arbitrary SAT. The flagBound theorem gives the averaging step. The same conclusion applies to the single nonlocal parity box above: check its two explicitly allowed affine systems. They can be strict supersets of the nonzero spectrum, but every feasible odd vector is still a valid trace.

### A uniform all-circuit compiler with a quantifiable loss

Allow many two-cell boxes. Make two identical copies of each constrained cell by requiring equality of their *Fourier frequencies* in W. Relabel the right two-bit frequency by the invertible map old (0,1,2,3) to new (0,2,3,1), with the contragredient map on samples. At anchor one the two-cell box F=x_1 x_2 now allows exactly raw rows 7,13,11: their (p,q) pairs are (1,1),(2,2),(2,3). Raw row 9 gives (3,1) and is forbidden. The signed coefficients for the doubled rows are respectively 1,+1/2,−1/2. Their three physical triples (X,Y,Z) have weight two. Complementing all three bits by the common anchor gives an exact-one check; its distinguished unit weight row is the third slack bit.

For each original gate take four row indicators t00,t01,t10,t11 and slack s. Impose exact-one(t00,t01,s) and exact-one(t10,t11,A+s) using independent doubled boxes. Join the pins by X=t10+t11, Y=t01+t11, Z=t00+t01+t10. Homogenize constants by A, identify parent and child pins as in the actual circuit including the zero default, and impose final output A. Take W to be the image of these linear equations in physical frequencies, with all physical anchors identified. Emit the independent doubled boxes, add an independent uniform W-perpendicular word and an anchor shift times the message. Computing W from public wiring uses finite binary elimination; its registered Machine clock is not established. The issuer need not know any witness. At anchor one, the four selectors and slack have a unique consistent setting for any honest NAND row, and no setting for an invalid row. Exactly one of the two checks uses the distinguished row. Hence every complete witness produces an odd Fourier component of absolute value 2^-G, where G is the number of gates, and every surviving odd component arises from a witness. Unused witness bits can coalesce into the same physical frequency. False circuits have exactly equal full-packet distributions.

For a unique satisfying witness the total variation distance is exactly 2^-G. More generally write the inverse-transformed odd part, under uniform counting measure on physical words, as a sum of at most #SAT orthogonal signs with coefficients of magnitude 2^-G. Its mean absolute value is exactly total variation between the two shifts. Cauchy–Schwarz and Parseval bound that by sqrt(#SAT)·2^-G, including all unprojected packet components. The fullViewEnergy theorem certifies the bound given the energy identity. For 4096 gates and 128 inputs this is at most 2^-4032. One packet has 16G=65536 raw bits before framing or ancillary data. Reissuing T independent copies gives at most T times this distance. There is no general correct WE here. This distinguishes support on every witness from statistically useful bias even for an unbounded full-view decoder.

For multiple shared parity constraints H d=f(x), conditional uniform marginals require Δ_i f(x) in span of the i-th column h_i for all x (take a supported fiber). This is also sufficient. Every mixed second difference lies in both one-dimensional spans and vanishes when the columns are distinct nonzero binary vectors. A nonlinear ANF term therefore uses variables from only one identical nonzero-column class. Kernel averaging confines the high-bit pattern to rowspace(H), so rank k allows 2^k affine frequency sectors. Exponential-rank-sector search is not ruled out by the two-system bound. An all-circuit issuer with high advantageous mass in those sectors, or a release with computational NO hiding from the fixed registry, remains open. No new assumption is proposed.

### Reproducible finite verification

```python
from collections import Counter
from itertools import permutations, product
from fractions import Fraction

n = range(4)
parity = lambda u: u.bit_count() % 2
dot = lambda u, v: parity(u & v)
raw = lambda p,q: (0,1,14,15)[p] ^ (0,3,5,6)[q]
forbid = lambda a: (a >> 1 & 1)*(a >> 2 & 1) != (a & 1)*((a & 1)^(a >> 3 & 1))
legal = (9,13,11,7)
assert len({raw(a,b) for a,b in product(n,repeat=2)}) == 16
assert all(forbid(raw(a,b)) == ((not a) != (not b)) for a,b in product(n,repeat=2))
planes=Counter()
for perm in permutations(n):
    assert all(perm[a^b] == perm[a]^perm[b]^perm[0] for a,b in product(n,repeat=2))
    mu = {(u,perm[u]): Fraction(1,4) for u in n}
    spectrum = {raw(a,b): sum(w*(-1)**(dot(a,u)^dot(b,v)) for (u,v),w in mu.items())
                for a,b in product(n,repeat=2)}
    assert all(not v for k,v in spectrum.items() if forbid(k))
    support = tuple(sorted(k for k,v in spectrum.items() if v))
    assert len(support)==4
    planes[support]+=1
assert sorted(planes.values())==[4]*6
assert sorted(sum(k in legal for k in plane) for plane in planes)==[0,0,2,2,2,2]
def F(table, x, y): return table >> (x+2*y) & 1
for table in range(16):
    atoms=[]
    for x,y,r,t,u in product(range(2),repeat=5):
        v=F(table,x,y)^u
        atoms.append((x+2*r,x+2*(r^u),y+2*t,y+2*(t^v)))
    for who in (0,1):
        for other in {a[2-2*who:4-2*who] for a in atoms}:
            fixed=[a[2*who:2*who+2] for a in atoms if a[2-2*who:4-2*who]==other]
            assert sorted(x for x,y in fixed)==sorted(y for x,y in fixed)==list(n)
    for p,q,i,j in product(n,repeat=4):
        actual=sum((-1)**(dot(p,a)^dot(q,b)^dot(i,c)^dot(j,d)) for a,b,c,d in atoms)
        h={p>>1,q>>1,i>>1,j>>1}
        expected=0
        if h=={0}: expected=32*int(p==q and i==j)
        if h=={1}: expected=8*sum((-1)**(F(table,x,y)^((p^q)&x)^((i^j)&y)) for x,y in product(range(2),repeat=2))
        assert actual==expected
        if forbid(raw(p,q)) or forbid(raw(i,j)): assert actual==0
    separable = any(all((a>>x&1)^(b>>y&1)==F(table,x,y)
                          for x,y in product(range(2),repeat=2)) for a,b in product(n,repeat=2))
    assert separable==(table.bit_count()%2==0)
print("24 vertices, 6 directions; 16 boxes, 4096 joint Walsh checks, 8 non-product boxes")
from math import comb
assert sum(comb(593,j)*3**j for j in range(297))*2**128 < 4**593
cols=(0,2,3,1)
weights = {}
for p,q in product(n,repeat=2):
    j=cols[q]
    if p==j and p<2: weights[raw(p,q)]=Fraction(1)
    elif p>=2 and j>=2: weights[raw(p,q)]=Fraction((-1)**((p^j)&1),2)
assert [weights.get(t,0) for t in legal]==[0,Fraction(1,2),Fraction(-1,2),1]
for a,b,c in product(range(2),repeat=3):
    got=[]
    for u,v,w,z,s in product(range(2),repeat=5):
        if any((u+v+s!=1,w+z+1-s!=1,a!=(w^z),b!=(v^z),c!=(u^v^w))): continue
        keys=[1+2*(1-i)+4*(1-j)+8*(1-k) for i,j,k in [(u,v,s),(w,z,1-s)]]
        assert abs(weights[keys[0]]*weights[keys[1]])==Fraction(1,2)
        got.append((u,v,w,z,s))
    assert len(got)==int(c==1-(a*b))
print("Exact-one lift: all 256 trials and doubled-character products; 593-bit majority bound exact.")
```
