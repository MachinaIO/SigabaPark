For an ordered circuit with 128 inputs and 4096 NAND gates, a proposed gate value z has signed residual r=z+ab-1. On bit inputs r is -1,0 or 1. Its parity is the XOR of z and the honest NAND of its two parents; this identity is Lean-verified. Therefore for fixed input, a full fault bitmap r mod 2 uniquely determines all gate values and the signed residuals by forward replay. Pin the final output to one; a false statement has no accepting trace with all residuals zero. When counting one may freely guess even the final fault and discard inconsistent replays.

Let R=(Z/2^s)[X]/(X^N+1), N=512, pi=X+1. Pack residuals into eight blocks R_j, draw eight independent uniform public ring headers A_j and test sum_j A_j R_j=0. For a fixed nonzero residual write d for the smallest multiplicity of X+1 in the binary reductions among its nonzero blocks. Ternary inputs ensure a nonzero block has a nonzero binary reduction. Then

    Pr_A[sum_j A_j R_j=0] = 2^(-sN+d).

Indeed pi^N is two times a unit: substituting X=pi-1, every interior binomial coefficient is even and the constant is 2. The sign is a unit and the remaining cofactor is 1+pi*b. Since pi^N is divisible by 2, pi^(sN)=0; thus the cofactor is invertible. The Lean ramification lemma proves this nilpotence/unit step from the dyadic identity. The quotient by pi has size two. The sN successive quotients of powers of pi have size at most two because each is cyclic over that residue field, and all have size exactly two since |R|=2^(sN). Every element outside (pi) is a unit (it is 1 modulo the nilpotent ideal), and (pi^N)=(2). The valuation of a block whose binary reduction is nonzero is therefore its binary multiplicity d_j<N. The image of the additive map on the headers is the ideal generated by the blocks, exactly (pi^d), and a finite group homomorphism maps uniform inputs to uniform on its image. This uses no hardness estimate and does not require a block to be invertible.

If active fault bits occupy m_j consecutive slots starting at zero in each block, then divisibility by (X+1)^l leaves at most 2^max(m_j-l,0) binary possibilities in that block. A binary degree-<m_j multiple is the product of the monic divisor with a unique quotient of degree <m_j-l, or zero. Balance the m_j to differ by at most one. For nonzero residuals l never exceeds the smallest m_j (with equality possible when the block lengths differ). There are at most 2^(128+sum_j m_j-8l) traces of minimum valuation exactly l. This is an upper bound by the containing set of valuation at least l; for fixed witness and fault bits no separate ternary-sign factor is charged. Union over all fixed traces before sampling the header yields

    Pr_A[there exists a spurious accepting trace on a fixed false statement]
       <= (128/127) 2^(128+sum_j m_j-sN).

The factor is the geometric sum bound for 2^(-7l), verified in Lean. This per-statement bound even covers an unbounded search after seeing A. It does not assert one header works simultaneously for all statements. With all 4096 fault bits and s=7 this bound is vacuous; s=9 gives less than 2^-383. At s=7 a 769-bit independent two-universal binary linear hash of the fault bitmap instead supplies the missing factor 2^-769. A random binary Toeplitz matrix takes 4864 fair seed bits: for any nonzero input, the first nonzero coordinate supplies a triangular minor in the diagonal-seed to output map. The combined 4353-bit output is longer than transmitting all 4096 fault bits themselves.

**Deterministic partial evaluation with the original eight q=128 headers.** Omit the guessed output of gate 1,5,...,4093 (one-based). Compute those 1024 gates exactly from their actual preceding trace values. Guess the other gate outputs, with the final one set to one. Repack the 3072 retained residuals in eight blocks of length 384, padding each to 512. Compared with the loose original parametrization the verifier may compute omitted wire values in sequence. It still rejects malformed traces and uses the registered true default for unavailable parents. Every authentic witness extends by authentic computation. Replay from the 3072 faults (zero on omitted gates) still determines the trace. Thus the same s=7, N=512, eight-header sampler preserves falsity except with probability less than 2^-383 for every fixed false circuit. Header 28,672 bits; hash 3584 bits. The odds do not rely on the ability to solve the hash equation. The signed residual interface which cannot compute or constrain any predetermined gates cannot silently substitute this parametrization. As an alternative, choose omitted gates by graph coloring if bounded polynomial depth matters: in topological order ordinary internal gates have at most two earlier neighbors, so three colors suffice. Omitting the largest color class among the 4095 nonfinal gates removes at least 1365 mutually nonadjacent gates. The substituted residuals then have Boolean-input arithmetic degree at most four. A second coloring of the contracted graph (at most four earlier neighbors) reduces the retained total to at most 2185; the safe substituted degree bound is sixteen. Retain the final gate and use balanced packing. Circuit parsing, color tables, verifier computation, statements, witness encoding and any appending machine all need additional charges.

**A public-ciphertext composition with an explicit open dependency.** Expose the random header, construct the compressed statement with the declared trace parametrization, and call an ordinary WE encryptor for that compressed relation. An accepting witness translates by one circuit traversal. Conditional on a good header for a fixed original NO, the inner statement is an ordinary NO, so its real public ciphertext and any included predicate data must satisfy the security of that inner primitive. If its signed distinguishing gap is uniformly at most epsilon on these statements, the outer gap is at most epsilon plus the bad-header probability. The weighted signed averaging lemma is Lean-verified. This also permits an efficient true-NO simulator for a scoped inner implementation with its stated error and cost, paying bad mass separately. The inner statement must bind the header, wiring, retained indices and padding; the reduction must pay parsing and sampling costs and account for any nonuniform header advice used. No inner WE, general simulation oracle, or public zero-release program is supplied by this composition. Its witness relation and circuit size differ from the registered 128-input primitive, so simply invoking that target on itself would not close a construction.

**Computational consistency check at the original parameters.** For the all-guessed interface, a finder producing a classical trace from headers alone gives the fixed decision Ring-LWE test using the withheld bodies B_j=A_j t+E_j. At a verified hash zero the secret term vanishes; coefficient zero of sum B_jR_j is a signed sum of w independent CBD(1) errors, where w is the residual weight. In the uniform-body game a coefficient with unit scalar multiplier makes the readout uniform regardless of whether R_j is a ring unit. Test whether adding a uniform dyadic dither changes the readout's half bit. Its acceptance gap is g(w) over the half-bit baseline. The half-circle autocorrelation has nonnegative Fourier coefficients and CBD(1) contributes cos²(pi j/q); hence g is nonincreasing. Exact integer convolution at w=4096 gives g>0.03436 for q=128. A failing finder can take a fixed action identically in both worlds. For success p this proves a signed gap at least 0.03436 p, with the runtime of one finder, trace reconstruction and hash check, a withheld scalar dot product, parsing, and dithering. Giving the finder body-dependent objects without their joint simulation invalidates the conditioning. The classical registry score for this fixed Ring-LWE record was reported as 177; this is not a formal QPT guarantee. The exact arithmetic-energy test for clear bit traces is an alternative, and is not the signed-linear interface treated here.
