## What an all-orders lower bound measures

Take the graph of [the trace-rank fixture](https://github.com/MachinaIO/SigabaPark/pull/165) on seven-bit vertices. Its 21 XOR shifts are 3,7,25,36,37,43,52,57,59,71,77,92,97,105,106,109,110,117,119,121,124. There are 1344 edges. The raw circuit computes the OR of edge products and pads its NAND realization to 4096 live gates. Its extended predicate checks all 4096 claimed gate values and the final bit as well as reading 128 original inputs. The latter input alone has a short public acceptance test.

Every ordering of the 4224 extended variables has a cut with 64 original vertices on each side A,B. Translation diagonalizes in the Walsh basis: the nonconstant eigenvalues are 21−2m where m counts shifts with odd dot product against the character. The Lean certificate enumerates all 127 values, proving 6≤m≤15, hence absolute eigenvalue at most 9. The three successive folds of the seven-bit AND with right shifts 4,2,1 leave its parity in the low bit. If bipartite crossing matching size were at most 44, take a vertex cover with s+t≤44 by the bipartite matching theorem. Outside that cover there are edge-free subsets of sizes a=64−s and b=64−t. Centering their indicator vectors orthogonally to constants gives 21²ab≤9²(128−a)(128−b) by Cauchy–Schwarz and the eigenvalue bound. The exact strict reverse inequality is in the certificate. Thus 45 disjoint crossing edges exist. The same centering calculation for an independent set of size z gives 21z≤9(128−z), so every 45-element set contains an edge.

Orient each matching pair (u_i,v_i) from A to B. For every one of the 2^45 subsets T set x_u=1 on T and x_v=1 off T, with all other bits zero. These are 45-element supports, so their unique honest traces accept. Use their left and right restrictions as rows and columns respectively. In row T and column U, an index i∈T minus U gives matching endpoints (1,1). Its corresponding NAND bit has value one on every diagonal trace, regardless of which side stores it, contradicting those endpoints in the mixed evaluation. Thus the support of the square block is contained in T⊆U and every diagonal entry is one. Sorting subsets by size with deterministic tie breaks gives an upper triangular unit diagonal and rank 2^45 over any field. The Lean `triangular_profiles` formalizes the abstract rank implication. This improves the middle-weight restriction of size binomial(45,22), which gives an identity block. The independent spectral and trace arguments are given here in prose; the finite spectral certificate and linear algebra implication are formal. The original fixture script also checked 24 shuffled orders, eight-by-eight samples per order, all 127 eigenvalues and individual essentiality of every edge.

This bounds exact once-read raw-trace residual rank at some cut of every order. It is not a time or space lower bound for sparse programs, original witness acceptance, or existential projection of the trace. Registered NANDs are functional on previous wires (missing addresses default true), so there is exactly one consistent gate trace for each original assignment. Existentially removing the trace therefore preserves witness acceptance on every well-typed statement, before doing any further rewriting.

## Preserve the simultaneous output, not a guessed list of individual ranges

Write p for retained bits and q for a private block. All external uses of q must factor through a checked boundary F(p,q), possibly feeding an arbitrary nonmonotone predicate ψ(p,F). A source may first apply a publicly computable bijection q=T_p(old); the inverse fixes p. If every realized profile has a section encoded by c satisfying F(p,decode(p,c))=F(p,q), the child checks ψ(p,F(p,decode(p,c))). Project by evaluating the chart and finding c; lift by applying the inverse chart to the decoded value. `chart_projection` states this conditioned equivalence for arbitrary ψ. Sharing intermediate nodes and placing unrelated constraints between cone gates is harmless if the use graph certifies this factorization. Replacing outputs separately by independent selector bits is unsound, already for the joint profile (q,q).

**Joint monotone outputs.** At fixed p regard q as its support S. For each true output retain one sufficient certificate C_i⊆S; for a false output take the empty set. Shrink S to the union. Every true output remains true and every false output remains false by monotonicity. The formal lemma `support_union` gives the full joint profile and the sum-of-sizes bound. For positive DNFs choose one satisfied term per true output and retain its private literals. Shared variables, coinciding terms, external negations of outputs and nonlinear dependence on p in public term guards are allowed. For k edge-ORs at most 2k private ones suffice and scanning each edge list once finds the certificates. Pad a list of 2k indices by a sentinel, decode by set union, and allow duplicates. This costs at most 2k ceil(log₂(n+1)) witness bits for n private coordinates; use it only if cheaper. There are also at most sum_{i≤2k} binomial(n,i) distinct decoded supports. Compatible changes of orientation are applied once to the whole block. Arbitrarily negated private literals break the shrinking argument.

**Joint nonmonotone outputs.** Suppose at each fixed p the Boolean ANF degree in q of output f_i is at most d_i. For a realized profile y, its indicator is the product of (1+f_i+y_i) over GF(2), of degree at most D=sum d_i after Boolean multilinearization. If a realizing support S has more than D ones, fix all except D+1 chosen ones and restrict to their deletion cube. The indicator remains one at the top. Every surviving monomial covers a strict subset T of these positions and occurs on 2^(D+1−|T|) corners, an even count. The XOR of all corners therefore vanishes. Some proper subcorner also realizes y. The Lean `anf_descent` proves this step for arbitrary coefficients and finite monomial families, and `cube_descent` isolates the even-fiber version. Repeating gives a section of weight at most min(n,D), despite output correlations. One can check at most n(2^(D+1)−1) further circuit evaluations starting with the actual witness, without listing all weight-D supports in n variables. Encode D indexed positions as above and evaluate the original output cone on the decoded support. A syntactic degree certificate can propagate constants, linear operations and degree sums under multiplication, capped by n; more exact simplification must itself be checked. Raw circuits with excessive D may gain nothing. Exhaustive tests checked 51200 overlapping three-color edge profiles on nine coordinates, and descent preserved all profiles in 160 trials with two random quadratic outputs on twelve coordinates.

**Exact scalar specialization.** For an edge-OR y, let L(p) contain precisely retained-only edges that fire. If there is a fixed free-free edge, y=1 can always be realized by its two private endpoints; q=0 gives exactly L(p). For an outer selector of F(p) on y=1 and H(p) on y=0, the child predicate is F(p) OR ((NOT L(p)) AND H(p)). It needs no selector witness. Forward translation keeps p. For lifting use the anchor when F(p)=1, else use q=0; invert the chart in both cases. More generally the two extrema of any single monotone Boolean boundary are sufficient. If there is no free-free edge the upper endpoint includes edges from a retained one to a free neighbor. This extremum shortcut does not extend independently to overlapping outputs; use a joint section.

## Registered mixed residuals and arbitrary retained problems

The reproducible script in the companion note builds twice, reads the resulting NAND arrays, recognizes the selector, traces the AND of edge negations, verifies a four-NAND XOR chart, and reconstructs the child. Coordinates 0 through 7 are retained. In the source cone, q₈=old₈ XOR (p₀ AND p₁) is nonlinear on original inputs and is its own inverse at fixed p; all other private coordinates are unchanged. Old wire 8 is used in the boundary only through the replacement. Public checks reject an external raw-private dependency. An unrecognized form must be passed to a separate fallback. Here the parser handles the supplied certificate and exact NAND gadget layouts; it is not a semantic recognizer for arbitrary equivalent circuits.

Eight retained edges are (0,3),(0,7),(1,2),(1,6),(2,5),(3,4),(4,7),(5,6). The fixed free edge is (8,11). The mixed positive residual uses F=(p₀ XOR p₁) AND (p₃ OR p₄) and H=(p₂ XOR p₅) AND ((NOT p₀) OR p₇). The negative variant uses F AND NOT F on the high branch and H=L on the low branch, so an unrestricted y selector would create spurious solutions. Its external selector can be true even though no legal internal trace realizes that case. Compacting the two raw examples gives:

| Case | Input bits, NANDs | Child bits, NANDs | Statement bits before → after | Accepting retained assignments |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Mixed positive | 128, 4096 | 8, 46 | 41,858,998 → 6,140 | 113 / 256 |
| Mixed negative | 128, 4096 | 8, 39 | 41,742,640 → 4,963 | 0 / 256 |

Positive and negative cases have respectively 4058 and 4072 live gates before live identity padding. Both old and child were tested on all 256 retained settings at both attainable endpoints and on one random interior per setting. In the positive case, 17 settings accepted the low endpoint only, 8 the high endpoint only, and 88 both. The reconstruction was evaluated at every retained setting. Serialized statement sizes are exact for the indicated dimensions: each gate (a,b) contributes 4a+2b+3 bits under `encGates`. They exclude outer framing, algorithm descriptions and execution time.

The method can leave an arbitrary unsolved residual. Take any 64-input F with 2500 NANDs (or fewer subject to padding), a fresh selector r, and the OR of the 448 edges from the first seven listed shifts on all 128 coordinates. Apply the same form of chart at private coordinate 65. Accept F(p) AND (y=r), with r at coordinate 64. Every old witness restricts to a witness for F. Given any witness of F, set r=1, set private edge (65,66) to one in chart coordinates, set other private bits zero and invert the chart. This lifts without deciding F or relying on solution density. After both projections the child is literally F, including any shared nonlinear subcircuits and any positive or negative answer. The clause uses y negatively when r=0. A 2500-gate chain replay took 3854 live gates before padding, 4096 after padding, and 38,641,298 serialized input bits against 13,304,508 child bits. Its 160 sampled lifted evaluations agree. The equivalence for every F follows from the section, independently of the representative.

## Composition and WE accounting

Copy the retained circuit as a DAG with one new node per distinct original gate. A checked chart can be a sequence of reversible updates q_j ← q_j XOR h_j(p,q_except_j); undo the updates in reverse order. Recheck private use isolation after every rewrite. A chain of output-preserving sections composes in the same order for projection and in reverse order for lifting. Keep cost ledgers for each decoder, degree certificate and circuit DAG; expressions expanded as trees can multiply. A bounded-support decoder may use nD equality-to-index tests and shared ORs, each index of ceil(log₂(n+1)) bits. Memoizing repeated subcircuits prevents accidental duplication but does not bound growth of the mathematical boundary parameters across stages.

Encryption publicly compiles the same source into the selected child and calls its WE. Decryption converts the supplied parent witness to the child witness. NO maps to NO, and the full ciphertext can be precisely the child ciphertext with a public deterministic, message-independent mode tag if needed. A distinguisher for that complete view yields one for the compiled child with equal advantage, including for quantum observers of classical bytes. Account for the reduction's description, raw input or fixed-statement advice, public compilation, challenge formatting and simulation clock. The two measured child encodings are for the smaller typed relations. A backend fixed to exactly 128/4096 must renumber and pad, or prove a specialization using a smaller relation; it does not inherit the shorter challenge encoding automatically. Recognizing cone gadgets requires scanning the unary input, resolving forward references to true, traversing use/definition edges, checking private uses and emitting the new statement. No certified finite Turing-machine clock for the full compiler, no all-input compact backend, and no new registry hardness proof is supplied here.
