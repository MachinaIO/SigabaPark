Let the layered acyclic graph have edge transfers t_e=a_e x_e, where x_e is the local availability bit, including zero on unavailable edges. Set r_s=beta; all other vertex masks and all y_e are independent uniform field elements. Issue d_e=y_e−t_e r_u for e from u to v, c_v=r_v−sum_(e into v)y_e, and r_t. Condition on all public weights and all circuit descriptors. Replacing c_v by B_v=c_v+sum d_e is invertible given d, and B_v=r_v−sum t_e r_u. The d's are independent uniform also given all the r's. Triangularity makes the map from the fresh vertex masks to the B's a bijection for each beta. Thus one can discard the entire edge-label column and issue B directly, computing every needed local weighted contribution exactly once. This marginal preserves all distinguishing power. With dense explicit representation it takes one word per nonsource vertex and one terminal word; edge traversal cost remains.

Let f_s=1 and f_v=sum t_e f_u, P=f_t. To change beta by delta add delta f_v to each r_v and delta t_e f_u to each y_e, a measure-preserving translation. All d,c stay fixed (edgeInvariant, shift) and r_t changes by delta P. With no directed s-to-t path P=0, so the complete public laws agree. Over a finite field, if P is nonzero the two supports are disjoint. Reverse path sums h_t=1, h_u=sum_(e out of u)t_e h_v give r_t=beta P+sum_(v != s)h_v B_v. Testing whether the residual is zero suffices for a bit, even without dividing by P. This decoder needs all nonzero correction contributions. A path from s to t by itself does not identify them.

More precisely for public linear observations L B in addition to r_t, the two conditional laws are equal if h is outside the row span of L: a kernel vector with nonzero pairing absorbs P. When h is in that span the residual is available. For point queries, one unread index with nonzero h_v masks the test completely (eraseCoordinate). This also holds on leaves of an adaptive querying strategy that has not inspected all such indices, by the same conditional uniformity. For observations involving the uncompressed d columns, the vector to test in the enlarged span is (h,0). Over Z/2^k, an unread group of columns masks the terminal shift precisely when P belongs to the ideal generated by their coefficients. If every accepting witness corresponds to just one path and there are at most 2^128 paths, unit weights modulo 2^129 give the exact nonzero test and uniform sampling uses 129 fair bits per vertex. An odd prime exceeding 2^128 would also preserve this integer count; exact uniform sampling of such a prime from a bounded fair bit tape is impossible by divisibility.

If independent weights are attached to individual edges over GF(2^16), distinct simple paths give distinct monomials; for longest path length L, a nonzero path polynomial vanishes with probability at most min(1,L/65536). Share weights across edges only with a noncancellation proof. In a deterministic unambiguous verifier branching once per primary bit in a fixed order, give a 1-branch at position i weight z_i, a 0-branch weight 1, all other transitions weight 1. Each accepting witness contributes its own distinct multilinear monomial. Thus degree is at most 128, failure per trial at most 1/512, and 15 independent trials give at most 2^-135; an OR of nonzero tests suffices to decode a one. Supply independent 16 fair bits for each z_i and each field mask; public raw weights take 2048 bits per trial. The polynomial x^16+x^12+x^3+x+1 is irreducible: x^(2^16)=x modulo it and gcd(x^(2^8)−x,f)=1, checked in the replay. Independence of per-edge weights need not respect a subsequent symmetry compression.

Testing the undirected connectivity of the enabled graph is insufficient. An enabled graph with s→x, x→z, y→z, y→w, w→t has an undirected s-to-t path but no directed one. The full edge/vertex transcript is identically distributed for both bits in an exact GF(2) enumeration, including disabled s→y and z→t edges. A graph with two distinct directed paths through the two arms of a diamond also has P=0 for unit weights in GF(2). The preceding positivity/modulus or polynomial argument is necessary. For an explicit small graph public reachability or forward propagation already decides existence; applying monotone-span lower bounds to a protocol issuing fields in both availability cases is unwarranted.

Two simple compressions have explicit hypotheses. If each state in one group sends the same total weight to every successor group, group sums propagate under a smaller transfer matrix. If layers are independent tensor products of k local path systems with separable endpoints, global existence is the AND of local existence. Issue k independent local packets on random XOR shares of beta, so any unsatisfiable factor perfectly hides the bit; use reliable local nonzero tests on true factors. This costs the sum of local representations, not the Cartesian product. Arbitrary correlations across factors or unrestricted succinct transfer oracles invalidate these hypotheses.
