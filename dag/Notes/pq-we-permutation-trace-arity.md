### Registered trace and width-five reference

Decode the 128-bit witness and evaluate the 4096 registered NAND gates in order. Wire zero and not-yet-defined references read true. Use each resolved parent bit and output bit as its local triple, with the final output pinned true. The conjunction of all row tests on this trace is the registered output. Conversely any consistent accepting trace supplies a witness by projection to its input bits, since the gate semantics are deterministic.

Write [a,b]=aba⁻¹b⁻¹, and write v^x for v if the bit x is one, identity otherwise. In A5, T=(01)(23) is an involution and can be a commutator. T^z [a^x,b^y] is T precisely on a correct NAND row and identity otherwise. Pairing these tests costs 20 factors and can target any 5-cycle as a commutator of two involutions. In A5 any 5-cycle target can also be a commutator of 5-cycles, or [a,bab⁻¹] for 5-cycles a,b (direct enumeration of A5). Thus binary composition costs 2,2 copies of its children, and ternary [U,VWV⁻¹] costs 2,4,2, putting U and W on target a. A parent scheduler tested balanced binary splits and every ternary middle size with outer sizes differing by at most one. On 2048 pairs this yields 19281920 factors, 231383040 bits for two packed 6-bit choices per position, before addresses and framing. These are achievable counts.

### A marker construction in alternating groups

A six-factor local row can target a 3-cycle t. Choose an involution s conjugating t to t⁻¹ and a commutator [a,b]=t. For t=(012), take a=s=(12)(34) and b=(01)(34). Evaluate (ts)^z [a^x,b^y] s^z. With z=0 its value is t^(xy); with z=1 it is t^(1−xy). Conjugating all instructions by any permutation changes the target to any other 3-cycle and keeps every instruction even. LocalNandSix proves the abstract word identity.

For any arity k≥2 use k−1 chain positions 0 through k−2, one spare point h, and four distinct private points c,d,e,f. Start D=(0 c d), A=(k−2 e f). Middle child i targets C_i=(i i+1 h) for i=0,...,k−3. Apply enabled control cycles to the marker in increasing i (write their product U with the last-applied cycle on the left). After a first disabled control its position is fixed by every subsequent cycle. The other two points of D remain fixed throughout. If any middle test fails then UDU⁻¹ and A have disjoint supports. Otherwise their supports meet exactly at k−2. The commutator of two 3-cycles sharing just one point is a 3-cycle, as follows by multiplying (p e f) and (p c d). Thus [A^x,U D^z U⁻¹] is a fixed 3-cycle exactly when all k predicates hold. Correctness for false outer inputs and the group reduction given the middle condition are also Lean-checked by ChainAnd; the concrete permutation construction and the compiler induction here are mathematical arguments. Relabel the output 3-cycle to the parent's requested one. Inverses reverse the factor list and invert each selected row. The two outer children occur twice each and the k−2 controls occur four times each.

Take k=16 and three identical composition levels above the 4096 row words. The width is twenty, and 6·60³=1296000 positions suffice. This counts all copies and inverses. Every control row for a circuit constant can repeat the same permutation in both slots. A Python check traversed the 16384 middle masks at k=16, verified that the unique nonidentity commutator moves three points, and checked all eight six-factor row inputs. WidthTwentyCost certifies the numeric constants. An A20 element uses 61 bits in an enumerative code, since 2^60<20!/2≤2^61. Dense row pairs occupy 158112000 bits. A 13-bit control address per position raises the payload to 174960000 bits and its single length framing to 349920001 bits. This counts a possible encoding of row instructions. Unranking permutations, sampling any blinders, processing unary-input statements, reading framed inputs, and both role descriptions and clocks remain to be charged. Dense table lengths alone do not certify the strict 2^32 target.

### Complete public rows and the missing equality constraint

For arbitrary masks M_i, public rows M_i⁻¹ P_i(b) M_{i+1} telescope for every independently chosen sequence. BlindedPath proves this without any consistency premise. For a concrete registered false circuit take 4093 unused NAND(true,true) gates, then g=NOT(x), h=NAND(x,g), and out=NOT(h). Honest x=0 or 1 gives h=true and out=false. At g choose x=0,g=1; at h independently choose x=1,g=1,h=0; at out use h=0,out=1. Choose honest triples on all padding. Use each chosen local triple in every copy of its own gate, including inverse programs. Then every leaf and every composition returns its true target with the output pin enforced. One coherent rejecting trace and this mixed accepting sequence therefore give the two designated endpoints. Any public terminal which opens on the latter product can be used on these enumerated rows. The example is independent of branching arity and frame randomness.

More information is visible than single legal products. Let B_i be the product of blinded baseline rows through i−1 and R_i its known unmasked analogue. Then B_i (row_i(1) row_i(0)⁻¹) B_i⁻¹ is M_0⁻¹ R_i (P_i(1) P_i(0)⁻¹) R_i⁻¹ M_0 for baseline option zero. Replace the baseline by the available constant value when pinned. This normalizes all comparisons to a single conjugator. Independent blinding of stages does not yield independently hidden alternatives. Appending additive counters for consistent choices also leaves a risk: every commutator of two comparison elements in a direct product with an abelian counter has zero counter, even if its projected permutation is nonidentity. An accepting counter-zero word cannot be assumed to have come from a path.

### A constructive binding option and its size

At each position i retain the value of every unfixed variable occurring both before and at or after the cut. A vertex stores the permutation-action token (at most w places) and the bits at that cut. On first read branch both ways, on subsequent reads use the stored bit, and forget on last read. Start at a point moved by the target and accept exactly at its image under that target. Apply factors in action order, reversing the program scan if required by the multiplication convention. Induction identifies the reachable actions with exactly the consistent partial assignments. With p live bits at worst this layered graph has ≤(L+1)w·2^p vertices and ≤2Lw·2^p edges. The prior directed-label construction applies to this precise graph. Independent labels, domain-separated directed PRF edge corrections and accepting terminal pads give a NO hybrid per unreachable vertex in topological order. Each incoming edge to a switched vertex comes from an earlier unreachable vertex whose outgoing pad has already been randomized. Therefore simulating the incoming corrections needs no unknown challenge key. Terminal pads at unreachable accepting vertices can then be replaced with independent uniforms. This argument assumes the ordinary single-key PRF game with quantitative loss per switched vertex. It does not supply a new Assumption, a registered PRF implementation, or its quantum resources. When the graph is small, ordinary forward reachability already decides the statement at comparable graph-enumeration cost.

For this particular expanded program even a depth-one cut can retain many controls. At the boundary after U D U⁻¹ in the top commutator (before the inverse of the first outer child), the inverse copy of the first outer child and the inverse conjugate still remain. Every leaf has been read and will be read again. On circuits where all 128 witness bits occur as genuine local parents, this frontier already retains all 128. There are valid padded examples with a separate NOT(x_i) leaf for each input. Thus a construction that explicitly allocates every bit assignment on the cut uses at least 2^128 slots on such circuits. This is specific to the expanded product schedule and the explicit frontier dictionary. The GGH15 multipass reroute obligation and the split-witness ABE all-keys/all-attributes proof both address correlated choices under different interfaces. Neither supplies a short binding key for these public rows. Producing an efficient root release under a fixed registered falsifiable distribution remains open.
