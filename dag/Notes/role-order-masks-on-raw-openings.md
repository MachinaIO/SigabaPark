## Which ORE blocks need restricted evaluation?

For g=4096 write the raw occurrence word as g triples (a,b,z). The left opening consists of independently chosen NAND-valid triples. The right opening chooses actual wire values, repeats every named coordinate, replaces the constant and unavailable reads by one, and pins the last gate output to one. Their common raw words are precisely accepting traces. These different input parametrizations matter for full public keys.

Chenette, Lewi, Weis and Wu, *Practical Order-Revealing Encryption with Limited Leakage*, section 3, Equation (3.1) and Theorems 3.1–3.2, mask each bit by a PRF indexed by its location and preceding prefix, adding that bit modulo M≥3. Comparison uses only the first unequal block; its oriented difference determines the order. Their simulator receives the first unequal bit and relative order for each pair of *issued* ciphertexts, including equality. It does not distribute a circuit-restricted PRF master. [Authors' full paper](https://www.cs.utexas.edu/~dwu4/papers/PracticalORE.pdf)

For high integer h, use left low bits 01 and right low bits (payload,0). At a common high prefix the first suffix label is mask(h) on the left or mask(h)+payload on the right. Earlier high-prefix masks may be public when the high values themselves are intentionally public. For unequal high strings, the first difference and its sign are fixed irrespective of all suffix values (Lean prefixLeakage). Within each role, repetitions and ordering are unchanged with the bit. Explicit-list ORE can apply the cited theorem to the whole issued list, charging its full length, security hybrid and chosen PRF. Puncturing an ordinary PRF at known prefixes gives numerical capabilities at remaining prefixes; it does not require knowledge of a valid raw preimage for each such call.

## A public restriction with a checkable full-key criterion

Here is a specialized order-label generator. To give exact semantics on all inputs take high word (H_A(u),u) ordered lexicographically, with the raw opening as a public secondary high word. The payload comparison on a genuine witness is unchanged. The difference from using only the digest H_A(u) is confined to distinct raw words colliding in that digest. One may instead omit the secondary word if accepting this collision qualification. Nothing below invokes general ORE privacy of the intentionally public high fields. To read the two blocks one can use an unmasked final suffix bit (1 for left, 0 for right), since role identities are public. In the specialized comparison first inspect the clear high fields, then at a tie use the first unequal suffix block in the CLWW orientation. The last bit handles the left 01 vs right 00 case.

Use trits for the finite linear algebra below. Independently sample r[g,a] for each gate and its eight possible raw triples a. Define the common pad on raw u as the sum of its chosen rows. The left public key consists of the four trits for the valid NAND rows of each gate. The left-opening evaluator reads their sum. For the right key, substitute the fixed wire mapping into every raw selector and collect the unique multilinear polynomial on independent Boolean wire bits for

    P(v) = payload + sum_g r[g, triple_g(v)].

Publish its complete coefficient array in a layout determined only by circuit and context. A truth-row selector is a product of up to three literals; repeated variables, opposite factors, and pins are reduced using Boolean multilinearization. Its coefficients can be generated without finding a satisfying input. The right-opening evaluator returns P(v). Two valid roles on the same raw word therefore have suffix-label difference exactly equal to the payload. The mechanism sends masked tables and canonical polynomial coefficients, never source code containing an unmasked payload constant. The topology, public hash and context selection must themselves be independent of the payload.

Write T for the map taking the vector of random masks to the *entire* vector of left rows and right coefficients, and e for the unit shift in the right polynomial's constant. It is a linear map over F3. The complete view for bit b is uniform on im(T)+b e: equal-size fibers follow by translation. The views are identical exactly if e belongs to im(T); otherwise their disjoint cosets can be distinguished by solving the public linear system. Lean linearShift gives the equivalence on supports for any additive group map; the finite uniform statement follows from the same bijection. Equivalently there must exist coefficients lambda[g,a] zero on every left-valid row whose sum over all gates equals one as a Boolean function of *every* right assignment. Then translating each r by lambda absorbs the payload in the entire right key without changing any left key. This covers arbitrary public postprocessing, forged requests and branch mixing since it couples the actual bytes. The analogous special case of independent trits attached to disjoint reachable colors is Lean bankCoupling. No whole-circuit security is inferred from one-role marginals. This local certificate does not require mutually exclusive first-fault classes.

The construction's raw bounds are 8g random trits, 4g transmitted left trits, and at most 1+7g right coefficients since a three-variable gate has at most seven nonconstant monomials. At g=4096, fixed two-bit packing takes at most 90114 bits for both keys, before hash, graph, indices if serialized, and costs of parsing, arithmetic and evaluation. The support of the right table can be recomputed from the circuit. A crude issuer loops through eight truth rows and at most eight monomials per row per gate, before sorting/combining. No tape clock is claimed. Exact uniform trits are not obtainable from a fixed bounded fair-coin sampler; the same construction and translation identities work over Z/4 instead, using exactly two bits per mask and a Smith-normal-form image test over the residue ring. The positive and negative examples below work over both choices. The affine *field*-rank estimate later concerns trits.

For a full-size positive NO family take one unused gate then 4095 NOT gates from the constant one, using NAND(t,t). Pin the final output to one. For the i-th active gate define its lambda on agreeing parents a and output z as (-1)^(4095-i)(z+a-1), and as zero on unequal parents. Every valid raw gate row has lambda zero. On all right assignments the two parents agree, and the alternating sum telescopes to final+initial-1=1. The full NO transcript is therefore statistically identical without any hardness assumption, although neither party enumerates its exponential opening space. The family is publicly decidable; this is an explicit full-key simulation example.

For a failing instance take z=NAND(1,1), y=NAND(z,x), output=NAND(1,y) pinned to one. Three legitimate right tuples (z,x,y)=(0,0,1),(1,0,1),(1,1,0) with weights +1,-1,+1 cancel each invalid row of the first and last gate. Every middle row is valid. Thus the corresponding sum of right responses minus the matching sum of published left trits equals the payload. Unused fixed gates may be inserted before the terminal to reach 4096; choose consistent outputs of all such gates in these three tuples. Their remaining known valid masks cancel in the same accounting. This example uses the authorized function values, not an implementation bug.

A constructive repair refines only the first gate's mask table by one additional public raw bit, namely the occurrence of x in the second gate. The left key still provides only entries with valid first-gate triples for either value of this context. The right polynomial increases in degree by at most one. The following identity on consistent right assignments gives a certificate:

    1 = y + x*z - (y + z*x - 1).

Use y on the last gate's actual input pattern with final output one; it vanishes on valid rows there. Use x*z on the first gate's actual pattern, now with context x, and the negative of the parenthesized expression on the middle gate. On inconsistent-with-pins raw patterns of the outer gates define those coefficients as zero. All valid left rows still have zero coefficients. Thus one extra context restores exact full-key hiding on the failing circuit. For t context bits on every gate there are at most 8g 2^t masks and 4g 2^t left entries. The right Boolean polynomial has degree at most t+3 and at most 1+g(2^(t+3)-1) support positions. Finding bounded contexts with the required certificate for all false circuits, or hiding a noncanonical key when this test fails, is open. If the uniform polynomial-size construction admits efficient certificate decisions for every NO, the same public test also decides SAT because an accepting common word forbids such a certificate. No asymptotic separation or finite lower bound follows.

## Why a key operating on the digest alone changes the experiment

Let m=3g=12288 and A uniform over (Z/512)^(m by n). There are N=4^4096=2^8192 legal left words. On a fixed NO choose any one legal right word w independently of A; it is not a legal left word. The m-bit vectors (u-w) modulo two for all legal u are distinct and nonzero. Each pair is linearly independent over F2 and has an odd two-by-two minor, invertible modulo 512. Thus for each independent header column, both scalar products can be set to any pair of residues with equal multiplicity. Their all-column zero events are exactly pairwise independent. Put M=512^n. The number X of left openings with H_A(u)=H_A(w) has mean N/M and variance (N/M)(1-1/M). Consequently Pr[X=0]≤(M-1)/N by the second moment bound; Lean missingMass formalizes the averaging step. For n=768 this probability is strictly below 2^-1280. This does not give an efficient algorithm to *find* one of the openings.

Any public digest-only restricted key that evaluates the left first-suffix pad whenever h belongs to the mathematical left image must therefore return that pad at the readily computed h=H_A(w) with overwhelming probability. The legal right call yields the same pad plus the bit. The issue applies even to exact circuit-constrained PRFs for membership in the digest set, or a union of dyadic interval keys covering precisely that image, assuming efficient evaluation on h alone. Mathematical set membership differs from carrying and verifying the actual opening. The conclusion also excludes a proof relying on all reachable digest classes of both roles being disjoint at these short parameters. At modulus 4096 and n=896, union bounding the fixed w instead yields Pr[X>0]≤2^-2560. At n=1056 a union bound over at most 2^4224 right words gives at most 2^-256 for any distinct cross-role collision; the 12672-bit digest is then longer than the 12288-bit raw address. These two estimates do not compactly issue preimage-bound keys. If one requires consistent labels also for distinct *within-role* collisions, the cross-role union bound alone does not address that stronger functionality.

Even carriers checking a legal left opening cannot safely use a common affine mask in the *serialized digest bits*. At a single gate use the baseline 001; the other three minus baseline give 010,100,(1,1,-1), an integer unimodular basis. Across all gates these differences form a block-diagonal unimodular basis of Z^m. The all-baseline vector has a unit coefficient in this basis (on each block 001 is the first two difference vectors minus the third). Replace one difference having such a coefficient by the all-baseline vector and add the baseline to the other m-1 difference rows. This gives m genuinely legal raw words forming an integer unimodular matrix. Their digests under the public uniform A are therefore exactly m independent uniform digest strings, at rank n and any 2-power modulus. For D=9n bits, any nonzero affine trit constraint on a random binary word holds with probability at most 1/2: fix all but one nonconstant pivot bit. A nonzero constant constraint has probability zero. Hence except with probability 3^(D+1) 2^-m, the observed [1,digest bits] span F3^(D+1). At n=768 the exponent is below -1320 in base two. The first left suffix labels then determine every coefficient of an arbitrary common affine trit mask. One legitimate right evaluation determines the bit. This is a rank and sample bound for a proposed affine restriction, not a claim about general PRFs or a certified attacker clock.

The registered ordinary LWE header/body short-relation adapter in the comparison-oracle investigation applies to an actual raw collision found from transcript queries. Giving out a digest-only numerical evaluator or a master is not covered by that reduction. Similarly public comparison oracles, prior comparison-key targets (#219/#220), ordered provenance (#176), explicit reachable-state keys (#228), and first-violation partitions (#231) do not by themselves give the two released representations analyzed here. No new assumption is proposed.
