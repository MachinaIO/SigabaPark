## Public sampler and exact order

Abram, Malavolta and Roy, [Tree Encodings I](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/741), sections 4.2, 5.1, 5.2, Definition 5 and Theorem 8. We give a restricted positive instance of that experiment. Let q and p|q be powers of two, k the LWE dimension, n=k log2(q), G the k by n binary gadget, c the arity, s the recursion depth, M=m c^s, t=c n m. The public relation is specified by a matrix H of shape M by J and a distinguished row b. Set

    Y=[e_b ⊗ D | H ⊗ G],      width m+Jn, height Mk.

The statement compiler and all auxiliary data ignore the second shifted key. Fix the public Extract algorithm and its size/depth bounds when instantiating the three hash families. The reduction first sends empty tau (or public graph parameters). Only then does the challenger sample and give D uniform, hk=(A,B), and independently set up and zero-shift SH1 and SH2 to obtain two public shifted keys. The reduction returns the explicit Y and aux specifying H,b in a fixed padded representation. The challenger verifies T=Extract(tau,Y,aux) has max entry norm at most one and YT=I_M ⊗ G. An invalid check aborts with win probability one half. Honest encryption runs the challenge sampler directly without checking T. It never receives or needs the reduction's trapdoor.

For bit zero, r1 and u are the respective public ShiftEval outputs on indexed (Y,aux). Write r for the first m coordinates of u, use standard representatives in [0,p), and group its other coordinates into J gadget words. Set vec(X)=Yu, sample a uniform common secret s0 and a=s0^T D+e^T. Hash(hk,X,[0;r1]) returns d and ek, Gen(hk,d,s0) gives C=(I_(t/n)⊗s0^T)U+E, h=(I_m⊗s0^T)d+e'. The ciphertext contains the sampled public setup and C,h,a. Y,aux,ek and any deterministic projection may be recomputed; they cannot carry independent information in one world. For bit one generate the very same public setup, and sample mutually independent, uniform C,h,a in their respective rings. No other public graph-dependent material is changed. On an admissible NO instance a PPT distinguisher of these *whole views* gives a Definition 5 distinguisher with the same absolute acceptance gap, plus the costs of building Y and packaging the common setup. Winning bias has the usual additional factor 1/2. This cites the paper theorem with its parameter, runtime, shift-length and intractability hypotheses. It gives neither a registry assumption-strength result nor a QPT/finite-clock reduction for the registered goal.

## Admissibility and recovery

On NO require full row rank of H modulo 2. A unit minor gives a right inverse J0 modulo q by elimination with odd pivots. No bound is needed on its lifted residues: to synthesize column (i,h,b0) of I_M⊗G, in each vertex block j set the digit positions for coordinate b0 to the bits of (2^h J0[j,i] mod q). Leave every other bit, including the first m entries, zero. Gadget synthesis followed by H produces exactly the desired column. Entries of T are 0 or 1. The matrix composition underlying this step is formalized. Extract is a deterministic bounded algorithm on all inputs by checking well-formed dimensions, performing elimination, and returning a zero matrix on failure. For the graph below it can instead use cuts. Prescribe polynomial size/bounded depth for this explicit extractor and choose supporting scheme bounds as required in section 5.2; these bounds enter the keys.

On YES a witness yields a short signed vector l with l_b=1 and l^T H=0 in Z_q. Then sum_i l_i X_i = D r, with no other u-block entering. Expand(hk,ek,C,h) gives v. For tau0=||l||_1 eta + m(p-1)E0 where eta is Lemma 17's simultaneous expansion error and E0 bounds ||e||_infty, the centered residue sum_i l_i v_i-a r lies in [-tau0,tau0] except on the two error tails. This is a witness-specific correctness statement: the coefficient of the sole D block is normalized to one. The inequality requires a parameter choice leaving headroom (2 tau0+1)/q small. The decoder also validates the supplied witness for the application.

In the ideal world condition on the full setup, hence on r and ek, and on C,h. The independent uniform a maps under a→a r onto the subgroup of multiples of g=gcd(q,r[0],...,r[m-1]) uniformly. Any interval of L consecutive residues contains at most ceil(L/g) points of its coset when L≤q, giving probability at most (L+g)/q. To average g one scalar marginal suffices. The actual input-succinct SH2 in section 4.3 adds a fresh independent public t in Z_p to the inner shifted evaluation and trapdoor encoding. For one fixed index the pre-mask expression is independent of t because Y,aux are chosen independently of the SH2 key and contain no field derived from it. The section 4.2 inner construction separately contains a uniform rounding offset; it is unnecessary here. Thus r[0] is exactly uniform in {0,...,p-1}. Reusing the mask at other indices poses no problem. For p=2^a, there are p/2^(j+1) representatives of 2-adic valuation j for 0≤j<a, and zero contributes q. Consequently E[g]≤E[gcd(q,r[0])]=q/p+a/2 and ideal window acceptance is at most L/q+1/p+a/(2q). This argument does not use independent parities across scalar indexed evaluations. A new independent complete setup/sample can repeat the experiment with majority for honest error reduction, charging all repetitions and hybrid losses.

## Programming the outer mask without a retry

A stronger honest sampler exploits that *every* projection uses the same distinguished D row. Use the actual outer mask t of the input-succinct SH2 in section 4.3. On making the SH2 key, generate its other fields exactly normally, and evaluate just the first relevant coordinate with this mask set to zero. Let b0 in [0,p) be this value. Draw j uniformly using log2(p)-1 bits from [0,p/2). Set t=2j+((b0+1) mod 2). If the output is vector-valued, do this for its relevant mask coordinate and sample other coordinates normally. Retain this newly sampled outer t in the entire released key and use that very key for the full calculation of u,X,d,ek and for decoding. The appended mask is used in neither the zero-shift TDH generation nor the inner shift. The baseline Y and auxiliary data are independent of this mask. Conditional on all pre-mask fields, the permitted t values are exactly the half that make (b0+t mod p) odd. Hence the resulting setup law is exactly the original law conditioned on the public event E that r[0] is odd, including all correlations of that mask at every other index. This consumes one extra coordinate evaluation, no retries. The ideal a remains independent and now has a unit coefficient for **every** generated setup, so the ideal window probability is exactly L/q for a contiguous window of L≤q residues.

On NO an ordinary Definition 5 reduction uses the same fixed preliminary tau and the same Y,aux in all cases. On E it supplies the entire challenge view unchanged to the distinguisher; on the complement it outputs a fixed bit. E is public from setup and has probability exactly one half in both challenge worlds. Its signed acceptance gap is therefore exactly half the distinguisher's conditioned gap. Inverting this implication incurs factor two; there is no assumption that a challenge can be reprogrammed after issue. Real correctness conditioned on E loses at most a factor two in its unconditional error bound (or use the simultaneous bound and the unchanged marginal of hk and the independent noise). The two additional Lean lemmas verify the exact bijection for chosen masks and the signed averaging identity. This condition targets a globally fixed coordinate; it would not certify all witness-specific subvectors in the exponential table variant.

## A graph with many paths sharing one reference

For undirected reachability, remove loops and choose any deterministic spanning forest F of the input graph, keeping the original edges for checking path witnesses. Orient its edges arbitrarily and append a distinguished s_target→s_source edge b for distinct terminals. H has rows the signed incidences and columns the vertices. Pad to the next admissible M with independent identity rows and fresh columns. If terminals are disconnected then F+b is a forest. For each edge, removing it divides its tree into two parts. The indicator of the part containing its head has zero potential drop on every other edge and drop one on that edge. These indicators give a *binary* right inverse J0, also handling identity padding. Thus T=[0;J0⊗I_n] itself is binary. To instantiate Extract succinctly, include the cut indicators in aux, generated by exactly the same edge-deletion protocol on every input including cyclic ones. For an edge on a cycle, its endpoints remain in the same component and its cut vector has difference zero there. Reading the relevant digit from each of at most M rows and adding the selected digits computes one coordinate of T times a digit vector; balanced modular adders suffice. This takes O(M log p) elementary adder gates and O(log M log log p) depth with ordinary bounded-fanin carry-lookahead adders, excluding computation of the input digits and cryptographic evaluations. The challenger still makes the full admissibility check. If terminals are connected, follow the forest path source→target with signs from the orientations, append b with coefficient +1, and set padding to zero. The sum telescopes in every vertex column, l_b=1, ||l||_1≤|V|. The same path can be found after checking any original valid witness. A chain of d diamonds (two two-edge routes per diamond) has 3d+1 vertices, 4d original edges and 2^d simple endpoint paths. The same construction, including the NO case obtained by separating the terminals, uses O(d) graph rows before padding. This sharing is genuine but reachability in these explicit graphs is already efficiently decidable.

Before padding H costs M J residues densely; explicit Y costs kM(m+Jn) residues and dense binary T has (m+Jn)Mn entries. A sparse incidence representation reduces storage only where the Sampler/Extract input contract accepts or regenerates it. Bit encoding each q residue adds log2(q). The online sample after setup costs ((t/n)t+2m) residues for C,h,a; D contributes km residues, and hk and both shifted keys still count. Even with compact Hash outputs, ek=w0 has nM bit digits up to sign. Section 5.1 expansion computes v of length M, or multiplies the required linear functional through the tree; neither proves polylogarithmic evaluation of dense Y or its shifted-key inputs. The paper's laconic dependence on input bounds and the dimension of d does not remove the honest algorithm's full work. Polynomial time for polynomially bounded sizes is enough for this restricted experiment; no registered instruction-clock bound has been derived.

As a finite independent check, all 10,650 simple graphs with a choice of unordered terminal pair on two through five labeled vertices were tested (9,044 connected and 1,606 disconnected terminal pairs). All signed closure identities and every fundamental-cut gadget column modulo 16 held. The dyadic expectation formula was also checked across 36 pairs of moduli up to 256.

## Why general circuits are open

The explicit table version takes one block [D,(1-C(z))G] per witness. The second block yields the unit target on every NO row and a YES row opens to D r_z. It costs at least 2^128 rows. The related signed correction B_not = D S - (1-C)G gives an exact NO gadget using D S-B_not and a YES D-preimage using S; signed short digits or gadget decompositions are needed for the trapdoor and S u enters the noise. It must be an actual component of Y u, not an unrelated EvalM matrix. For a public H compiler as above, rank over F2 is directly testable: a YES signed relation modulo q with l_b=1 survives reduction modulo two, whereas a NO right inverse stays a right inverse. Thus an everywhere correct uniform polynomial-time compiler for all growing SAT instances would imply SAT in P. Randomized setup with per-instance high-probability events yields a randomized decision procedure at the corresponding errors; nonuniform setup/advice or merely existential, hidden extraction requires separate treatment. This is not a complexity separation for a single fixed 128-bit instance.

In the real hash let t*=(B_(s-1)^T⊗G)r1-Yu. Then w0=-digits(t*) and w1=r1-L_(s-1)digits(t*), followed by d=(I_m⊗G)w1. Local evaluation of a digit does not automatically compute this global sum. The proof's H3→H4 replacement leaves an uncorrected component equal to target minus YT digits(target); it vanishes by admissibility on NO. In the witness-table construction with T chosen only on rejecting blocks it remains exactly the target on every accepting block. Using the proof hybrid as honest encryption would require a new affirmative correctness proof. Section 3's EvalM/EvalH equations give evaluated matrices and bounded correction terms, not this complete sampled d or a distribution-preserving short sampler for all public keys. The fixed D in Definition 5 is uniform and independent of the preliminary input; programming D or asking the challenge to choose it from the circuit is another experiment. The cited paper uses Decomposed-LWE. A reduction to the goal's permitted typed assumptions and a full capped general-circuit construction remain open.
