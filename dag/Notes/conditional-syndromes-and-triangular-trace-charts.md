### What an independent-ports code adds to a unique-witness instance

Split the linear wired address space into H (zero secret at every port) and a linear section of its semantic quotient. Read the full ciphertext modulo W-perp as (B,Y), where B comprises all pairings on a basis of H and Y the remaining coordinates. Choose a message-shift representative with zero action on H; this is inherent in the anchor-secret functional. If a semantic word u is invalid then every lift has zero coefficient in the raw law. Partial Fourier inversion over H therefore gives E[chi_u(Y)|B]=0 on every positive-probability B. On a unique-witness circuit there is just one potentially surviving odd semantic word v. The zero-message conditional density relative to uniform on Y is an even function plus delta(B)chi_v(Y). The message flips its second term, so entire TV is exactly E_B |delta(B)| (conditionalGap). The optimal test is a parity times the syndrome-dependent sign. The argument allows different statement-dependent laws; only the support restriction is used.

If both bit errors are ≤epsilon, the relation v dot Y=h(B) has failure probability at most epsilon under public zero-message samples, with h the optimal sign bit. If h lies in a publicly computable binary feature span of dimension D, take T=d+D+lambda examples with d semantic coordinates. Solve for an odd v' and unknown feature coefficients by binary elimination. The true pair is consistent with probability ≥1−T epsilon. For every incorrect odd v', the conditional parity is fair given every B, so a particular feature coefficient vector survives independent trials with probability 2^-T. The union bound gives error ≤T epsilon+2^-lambda for recovery of the unique witness, which is directly verifiable. A finite syndrome alphabet can use indicator features, a bounded-degree sign polynomial can use its monomials, and approximate sign agreement contributes its own per-sample error. Without a small feature span, efficiently finding a consistent nonlinear sign may be hard even with polynomially many examples. Merely calling h a GGM PRF keyed by the witness does not supply an issuer able to generate valid training examples without that witness, and does not prove full NO privacy.

### Nonlinear chart with inexpensive exact sampling

For this construction use scalar raw ports. Suppose a public chart of raw addresses V(u,v), with u,v each m bits, has common anchor one, consists of locally legal NAND rows and is injective. A chart member need not wire to the statement. Suppose every coordinate has the form
V_i(u,v)=g_i(u)+sum_j L_ij(u_1,...,u_(j-1))v_j.
The g_i may be arbitrary efficiently evaluable Boolean functions and include high-degree circuitry. The derivative on each v_j may vary nonlinearly with strictly earlier u bits. Define S(z)=sum_(u,v) (-1)^(u·v + z·V(u,v)). Summing first over v kills every u except the unique triangular fixed point u_j=sum_i z_i L_ij(u_<j). Thus S(z)=2^m (-1)^(z·g(u)). It is computable without enumerating the chart or knowing any accepting trace. Give z density 1+2^-m S(z) relative to uniform. A fixed direction flipping one common anchor reverses S and leaves the triangular solution unchanged; send a uniform z with this bit corrected so S is positive. This samples the density exactly. Its only nonconstant Walsh coefficients are 2^-m (-1)^(u·v) at the distinct V(u,v). Consequently every raw false statement remains exactly private after W-perp masking and shifting, including a chart with rejecting traces. If V(u,v) wires to the provided accepting assignment, its public sign corrects the observed parity to give bias 2^-m about the payload. Independent repetitions O(2^(2m) log(1/epsilon)) suffice. Raw packet size is 4G bits before descriptors. Known instance-specific parameter partitions are distinct from changing the hardness assumption.

One source of certified charts is actual execution on variables (u,v). Track raw gate wires affine in v, keeping an overestimate of their supported v_j and u dependencies for v-free wires. Products of two v-free wires are unrestricted. For a NAND of a v-affine wire and a v-free guard P, require that every supported v_j has every u dependency of P strictly earlier than j. Then multiplying each old triangular coefficient by P preserves triangularity; the constant term may be arbitrary. A doubled input is Boolean NOT and also preserves the class. The circuit can read otherwise unused v bits in unary gates to make their addresses visible. Require visibility or other proven injectivity for the stated coefficient magnitude. For sound fallback, if these syntactic conditions are not certified, issue an independent uniform packet. This keeps the NO proof on all encodings while leaving general YES correctness open. Coefficients can also be computed by evaluating the circuit with the earlier u prefix filled and later u set to zero at v=0 and v=e_j, then differencing; the proven dependency condition justifies filling the unknown later bits. One further evaluation at the recovered u and v=0 gives g. Counting a circuit interpretation, not a multitape clock, this is at most 2m+1 executions plus linear raw dot products per draw. The class includes arbitrary circuits on the u half alone, together with readouts of v and triangular controlled products. Padding and unused primary inputs can be included at the registered size.

### Exact openings on a witness slice

Suppose that for one accepting u and v0, V(u,v) wires to an accepting trace throughout a d-dimensional affine slice v0+D. The receiver knows u,v0 and a basis for D (for example all v are free in the terminal test). For each basis direction t, the difference K_t=V(u,v0+t)+V(u,v0) is wired and even. It is linear in t. Injectivity gives d independent differences. Check the d public equalities K_t dot Y=u·t on the ciphertext. They hold with probability exactly 2^-d: all nonconstant raw even frequencies vanish, and these wired pairings are unaffected by both message shift and W-perp masking. The sum of the corrected parities over the slice is zero outside this event and is 2^d times one corrected parity on it. Its expectation in the zero-message world is 2^d 2^-m, so conditional bias is 2^(d-m). No enumeration is needed for the receiver. A ternary vote (zero off the event) has variance at most 2^-d, mean ±2^-m; a Bernstein bound gives O(2^(2m-d) log(1/epsilon)) packets. For d=m the event opens the bit exactly, with erasure probability 1−2^-m per draw. This improves over the single-parity repetition exponent and allows independently hard-shaped computation on u; a direct public search of u may be cheaper. Exhausting a two-bit triangular example with both u_1 u_2 and u_1 v_2 features verifies all 128 amplitudes, all 127 nonzero frequencies, and the event probabilities/conditional biases at d=0,1,2.

### A fixed Ring-LWE hybrid for an optional public auxiliary field

At the registered gate count take a fixed decision-Ring-LWE family member with N=2048, eight ring samples, q=4096, uniform secret coefficients, and centered-binomial parameter 3 errors. The eight bodies supply 16384 coefficient high bits, exactly the raw scalar input length. Choose a private truly uniform raw z as above and additionally publish the eight A polynomials and z XOR the body's high bits. Keep the bodies and secret private. Apply the chart correction to z, then add the wiring pad and payload shift. Correctness of the chart reader is unchanged because its own raw input is still genuinely uniform. In the ideal Ring-LWE experiment the high bits are independent unbiased bits even given A. The auxiliary string is then independent of the entire ideal chart experiment, including the payload. A distinguisher between payloads on NO gives a real-to-ideal distinguisher on a fixed challenge with at most a factor two loss via the intermediate common ideal law; all witnesses and circuit data are outside the challenge. Independent auxiliary banks can be replaced one at a time, charging the full number of banks and their simulation. This optional field tests the feasibility of a fixed-assumption joint simulator. It does not make the encrypted private raw coins intelligible to an honest witness or compress the packet. Estimator security, concrete reduction time, candidate-key simulation for a witness-controlled PRF, full registered costs and a chart covering general circuits remain unproved.
