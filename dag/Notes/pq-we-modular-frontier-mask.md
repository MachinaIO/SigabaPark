In the shared-reader model for [PR 113, section 3](https://github.com/MachinaIO/SigabaPark/blob/54fdd46353a518dd353bf145566e0b0b2c410bbc/dag/Notes/pq-we-point-insertion-frontier.md),
let U be independently uniform in (Z/q)^d, B collect all readings to
preserve, and t be the target row. All coefficients are fixed independently
of U. Write L for the integer row lattice generated by lifts of B and qI,
and L' = L + Z t. Compute o = det(L)/det(L'), where determinants are
positive covolumes. The exact set of displacements achievable without
moving BU is the subgroup generated by q/o in Z/q. For even q, a half-modulus
flip is possible precisely when o is even. Unit displacement requires o=q.
This distinction matters for bit messages.

Indeed the possible changes are t(ker B), a subgroup. Each nonempty fiber
of the view BU is uniform on a translate of ker B, so the target is uniform
on a translate of that subgroup. To compute its size, the pairing
(x,u) -> exp(2 pi i xu/q) identifies the module with its character dual.
For a subgroup S of rows, |S-perp| = q^d/|S|: sum the characters over both
S and all columns, using cancellation on nontrivial characters in each
order. Apply this to S=row(B) and to S+<t>; the ratio of annihilator sizes
is |t(ker B)| = |S+<t>|/|S| = [L':L]. A witness for a permitted displacement
Delta is obtained by solving Bv=0, tv=Delta modulo q by Smith normal form.
The shift may also be applied to one column of a uniform matrix, provided
the stated readings are all that must be preserved.

**Polynomial preprocessing of exponentially many prefixes.** At each level
maintain the exceptional row and a row Hermite basis of the integer lattice
of other rows plus qI. To extend, reduce basis rows modulo q, multiply by
each bit transition, include the exceptional row times the off-path
transition, append qI and compute row Hermite form again. Advance the
exceptional row along the target bit. Initially the lattice is qZ^d.
The two possible extensions of already divergent prefixes and the first
divergence generate exactly the new lattice mod q. Bases have d rows,
and input entries can be reduced mod q before every normalization. Integer
normal forms on these polynomial-bit matrices give a polynomial-time test
in h,d,log q; no factorization of q is needed.

**Simultaneous limit for one common bank.** Even for a composite even q,
at most d targets can individually admit half-modulus flips while fixing
all other readings. Project to R=Z/(2^a), where 2^a exactly divides q:
q/2 is nonzero there. Such a target is indispensable among the projected
rows. Their span S has at most d generators by Smith normal form. The
images of the actual reading rows span S/2S, of dimension at most d.
Select at most d actual rows giving a basis there. Their span T satisfies
S=T+2S and iteration gives S=T since 2^a S=0. Every indispensable row
is among those selected. This argument does not require the other prime
factors to permit a unit displacement.

For q=8, d=1, B=(4), t=2, fixing BU leaves target displacement subgroup
{0,4}. Adding 4 to the target is perfectly hidden even though the full
target is not uniform conditional on BU. For q=12, B=(3), t=1 gives
{0,4,8}; positive residual entropy here cannot hide a shift of 6.
The test concerns exact mask translations before noise. Independent noise
preserves an established translation symmetry, but other public preimages,
correlated noise and challenge-dependent coefficients require their own
joint simulation. No point insertion in the full ciphertext follows.
