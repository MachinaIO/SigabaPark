A packed fresh-target encryption bank supports a single legal tagged product and hides the prohibited value even from arbitrary affine combinations of its base-field coordinates. The adversary can choose every bit of the candidate ciphertext. The format makes the adjusted phase computable by the reduction despite its unknown lattice secret. The bound uses a rank-nullity double count and a polynomial root bound. This improves the missing-power *guessing* game of [task 168](https://github.com/MachinaIO/SigabaPark/pull/168) and changes the large-modulus rounding decoder of [task 171](https://github.com/MachinaIO/SigabaPark/pull/171). A full garbled-program security reduction remains open.

### Dictionary and honest algorithms

Number positive variables i=1,…,N, N=4224, and assign opposite tag M−i where M=2N+1=8449. The 2N exponents are exactly [1,2N]. A pair sums to M exactly if the two tags oppose the same variable. The legal pair sums are [2,4N] except M, since same-positive sums fill [2,2N], same-negative sums fill [2N+2,4N], and the missing sum only arises on opposite tags. Add the needed unary 1 for D=4N−1=16895 and the constant 0 for 16896 payloads. This D is minimal for distinct positive integer monomials in one variable with a single missing sum and all unary records: the minimum exponent plus the ordered exponents followed by the other exponents plus the maximum give at least 4N−1 distinct sums. Remove one and add the minimum unary which cannot be a sum.

For each lane use a field K/Fp of size Q, a uniform nonzero source point t and an independent uniform nonzero target blinder g. All lanes have independent (t,g). At degree e publish source c=m−t^e for a fresh uniform K-label m. Reused occurrences share the record. Source c can equivalently be sampled uniformly and m defined from it. The distinguished public key bank contains Z_j=H_j·s+e_j+mask_j in the target modulus, with fresh target secret s, header H_j and retained CBD(1) error e_j. A degree slot carries the coordinate vector of **g·t^d**, including g at d=0. Nothing in the public keys reveals g itself. See below for the single joint registered target distribution.

A canonical response consists of an arbitrary base-field weight matrix h and arbitrary body vector V with fixed public dimensions. Each row derives U from h and the public H, or verifies an explicitly supplied U and rejects on mismatch. An out-of-range element, malformed or incorrect-length response is rejected. The destination privately subtracts Us and h·e and divides each output lane by its own g. This uses the secret errors from the bank that the response refers to. Its gate input consists of authenticated or fixed public coordinates only in the local experiment described here. Knowledge of an earlier random label can compromise a bare source encoding.

For a legal pair u,v take the polynomial (c_u+X^u)(c_v+X^v) over K. Each degree coefficient acts on the r base coordinates at that degree by its ordinary multiplication matrix. Set V=hZ. The true unnormalized phase then equals g times the product of the two source labels, including constant, unary and mixed terms. This also covers u=v with a memoized row. For positive and negative sets of variables S,T, the analogous product of aggregate row polynomials has coefficient |S∩T| at M. All consistent products therefore omit M and use the same algorithm. Counts are modulo p in a single component; the CRT instance checks both modulo 257 and 769, whose product exceeds N. A nonempty overlap can vanish in one component, so both groups receive a separate quantitative security bound.

For *every* candidate h,V, the signed unnormalized output equals c'+h·mask, c'=V−hZ. This is a vector identity in the base fields. It holds for adversarial weights, repeated slots and bodies unrelated to the honest evaluation. canonical_phase proves the algebraic cancellation with a scalar linear header; at multiple target coordinates apply the same distributive identity to the inner product. Each block of a weight matrix is an arbitrary Fp-linear map between lanes. Only genuine public recomputation of U permits the argument. A decoder accepting an unrelated U leaves an unknown term involving s.

### Falsifiable fixed-distribution game and proof

The challenger issues the source records and the specified target bank, including all padded and auxiliary unmasked test records. It tests whether the adversary's arbitrary canonical response, after actual correction and normalization, is the vector of forbidden t_l^M for the component being tested. The single-challenge reduction chooses all t,g and source records, transforms the challenge headers as needed and adds their known payloads to the challenge bodies. It tests c'+h·mask=g·t^M using the public h,V,Z and its own t,g, without learning s or the challenge errors. In the real experiment this event equals actual decoder success. In the ideal experiment the translated bodies stay jointly uniform conditional on the headers, including slots of the same ring sample, and c-source rows are jointly independent of hidden t,g. Thus the chosen maps and affine constant are independent of the hidden values. Arbitrary local public postprocessing and adaptive offline selection of one submitted answer are included. Returning secret decoder outputs on arbitrary preliminary queries would change the experiment.

Fix one field group with a lanes and a post-conditioned affine output map. Write L_{i,j,d}:K→K for the base-linear block which contributes from lane j, degree d to output i; it may vary with every i,j,d. For fixed t=(t_j), put

    E_t(g)_i = t_i^M g_i − Σ_{j,d} L_{i,j,d}(g_j t_j^d).

Winning is E_t(g)=c'. A fiber of a linear map is empty or a translate of its kernel. Temporarily allow all Q^a blinder vectors, including zeros. The trace pairing summed across lanes is nondegenerate, so the kernel and the trace adjoint kernel have equal cardinality by rank-nullity; dual_kernel_card verifies the general linear-dual statement. For a fixed dual vector b the jth adjoint equation at t_j is the ordinary polynomial equation

    b_j t_j^M − Σ_d (Σ_i L^*_{i,j,d}(b_i)) t_j^d = 0.

This follows from the trace adjoint identity and commutation of field multiplication. trace_kernel_equation proves the one-coordinate trace-to-polynomial equivalence under the nondegeneracy and adjoint identities. Finite fields supply them. Even arbitrary cross-coordinate Frobenius maps have adjoints here. Whenever b_j≠0, the coefficient at the missing exponent M is exactly b_j. Each such polynomial has at most B=4N roots, independently of all other source points. missing_root_count proves the finite root bound for an arbitrary missing monomial; affine_double_count gives the corresponding fiber-sum inequality. The complete product-count argument and cryptographic hybrid are given here on paper.

For uniform nonzero t_j set u=min(1,B/(Q−1)). If b has k nonzero coordinates, the simultaneous root probability is at most u^k; for zero coordinates bound the probability by one. Summing over b and dividing by Q^a gives at most [(1+(Q−1)u)/Q]^a for unrestricted uniform g. Conditioning all g_j nonzero costs at most (Q/(Q−1))^a. Thus the success bound, also with arbitrary affine constants and cross-lane coefficients, is at most

    min(1, ((B+1)/(Q−1))^a) + Adv_joint-decision

plus the explicitly incurred finite sampling distance if the honest implementation approximates the ideal samplers. This is one fixed local decision challenge, with a separate bound for either CRT prime; if success is defined as either group's forgery, a union bound on the two ideal probabilities and one joint decision comparison suffices. Nothing requires an oracle deciding whether the NAND instance is false.

Two falsification checks motivate the independent blinder. With unframed scalar points in F257 and F769 the legal mixed exponent M+768 agrees identically with M in both multiplicative groups. In a small extension an Fp-linear Frobenius can also transport one exponent to another. In the exhaustive F9 replay, cubing the legal cubic recovers the missing linear value for all eight source points without a blinder. Applying that same map to the framed cubic works in 16 of 64 source-point/blinder pairs. All 6,561 homogeneous map tuples and all 59,049 affine choices give a maximum of 24/64. An ideal view only hiding the *value as a clear guess* would miss the unframed ciphertext-level problem.

### Fixed concrete packet and cost

Use K1=F257[X]/(X^4−3) in eight independent lanes, and K2=F769[X]/(X^4−11) in six. The constants are primitive modulo the respective primes (test the prime factors of p−1); the irreducible-binomial criterion applies since both primes are 1 mod 4 and the subgroup index is one. Keep the displayed power bases public. The group bounds are ((16897)/(257^4−1))^8 <2^−143.82 and ((16897)/(769^4−1))^6 <2^−145.81. Slot coordinates in the two components are combined by CRT into q=197633. There are 32 slots per degree; the final eight F769 slots are zero payloads. A canonical weight modulo q permits arbitrary coefficients independently in each CRT arm. Cross-prime group homomorphisms are zero; an adversary may still use the *entire* view to choose all coefficients and constants.

The underlying registered ordinary Module-LWE challenge has ring degree n=128, module rank k=8, m=4240 samples, uniform secret and CBD(1) error. It occupies 4224 payload blocks exactly, followed by 16 unmasked reserved blocks, or 2048 extra scalar slots. A canonical weight on an unmasked slot contributes zero to h·mask. The fixed core returned ModuleLWE.H=169 at these parameters. This is its estimator output, not a proven quantum lower bound or a 169-bit end-to-end reduction.

Both primes admit primitive 256th roots. In each CRT arm use the root g0^((p−1)/256), where g0 is the corresponding primitive base-field constant. Uniform secret and header vectors may be sampled in their bijective negacyclic evaluation coordinates. For each block take k pointwise products and sum, perform one inverse transform in each arm, and add the *same* small signed error to both residues followed by the two payloads. Transmit header evaluations and coefficient bodies. Inverse-coordinate extraction at slot j and evaluation coordinate v contributes n^−1(ρ^(2v+1))^−j times the header at v; this specifies U on each of the eight secret-polynomial coordinates. The Python replay constructs all 4240 blocks, generates the 8448 source records, computes 56 switched output coordinates and verifies their normalization with only the intended secret material. It hashes the full uncompressed CRT lattice records and retains the small subset used for that test. Runtime and digest of the host representation are not tape measurements.

The dense weight number uses payload columns only. If arbitrary reserved columns are allowed, use 542720 columns and 286,556,160 bits, plus the same body. The replay generates U on demand. An honest single gate can encode the degree IDs and its four extension-field multiplication maps sparsely; a canonical parser can expand that representation, add repeated coefficients and check bounds. Decryption needs access to h and the matching public bank. Publishing one full dense response for every gate would already exceed 2^32. The retained error vector must be included in subsequent key material if another layer uses this decoder. Replacing it by a seed requires an independent pseudorandomness hybrid, which is not used here.

A rank-8 issuer draws 4,342,784 independent CRT uniform residues for headers plus the secret (about 76.4 million bits of entropy), 1,085,440 exact CBD coin bits, the source labels, and 28 nonzero quartic elements for t,g. Fair coins cannot sample a uniform odd residue exactly in bounded time. A fixed choice uses w+192 coins reduced modulo each w-bit prime. Per residue total variation is <2^−192 and fewer than 2^24 component draws including the secret/source incur <2^−168 by a union bound. For every nonzero quartic draw, use sufficiently many independent field draws and fall back to one if all are zero. Six draws suffice: each fallback has probability Q^−6 and the union over 28 is <2^−187. The hashing replay uses a seeded rejection sampler rather than a bounded implementation. Fixed-width reduction of the larger random words, storage, layout changes and output must all be charged.

Each arm performs 4,341,760 variable pointwise products, seven radix-two stages per block, 1,899,520 butterflies, and up to one inverse normalization multiply per output slot. Stream g·t^d in each lane by successive multiplication by the fixed quartic t. Schoolbook binomial-basis multiplication uses 16 base-field products per lane per update, plus reduction of the three high coefficients. The registered Horner body from task 171 costs 3(2W+3)+1 ticks for one multiplier bit. For literal raw products of w-bit words use W=2w, then reduce. The pointwise products alone charge 10,255,237,120 ticks in this particular schedule. Separately, the source updates alone charge 4,405,395,456 ticks before modular reduction. Rates of 9- or 10-bit base-field operations are not rates of Turing-machine steps. The trusted machine also charges 6s·3^k(4+4k+Nat.size(s)) bits for s control states and k working tapes; module rank and working tape count are different parameters. Complete clock and descriptions have not been certified. A faster option would store nonzero residues as discrete logarithms and implement addition/subtraction using a size-p Zech logarithm table. Tables, their control-state or tape implementation, zero/sentinel handling, twiddle scheduling and serial data movement have to be priced before claiming savings. The smaller moduli and plain public basis require no huge extension-field logarithm table.

### What a full WE reduction still requires

In the local simulator the source t,g and random messages are known to the reduction, the lattice secret s and real errors are unknown, and the only secret-dependent public lattice records are the challenge headers and translated bodies. A separate last-key-dependent terminal cannot be freely generated. In Kluczniak's [ePrint 2020/1502](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/1502), Construction 4 also publishes encrypted wire labels, gate rows for actual truth-table entries, a message-dependent accepting unlock (random or F(sk)), and information supporting CycleTest. Even when input-stage tables can be generated by a reduction which knows source labels, giving correlated tables in the *ideal* game may destroy independence of the source t,g from public c and the adversary's chosen response. There is no simulation here of tables that require the hidden challenge secret or retained e, of successful oracle queries to a terminal, or of the change from a random terminal to F(sk). Multiple gate queries can disclose related labels; replayed versus independently reissued records matter. Nested corrected responses must keep their coefficient provenance and authenticate that the selected IDs belong to real incoming labels. The local falsifiable game excludes none of these possibilities for a finished scheme. Source blinder exposure, a public full-decoder oracle, or an unverified U directly invalidate its proof. The registered quantum strength, the entire false-statement transcript, and the strict cost target remain open.
