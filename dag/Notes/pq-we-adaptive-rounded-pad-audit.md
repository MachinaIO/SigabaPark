## Reviewed experiments

This review freezes the section “A fixed experiment” of [PR 170](https://github.com/MachinaIO/SigabaPark/pull/170), in the note with SHA-256 ae577779ea52b7b196838361588098a7c6732d5e4a654c57ce0dd6cb8b96a1d4, and the message-only construction in [PR 173](https://github.com/MachinaIO/SigabaPark/pull/173), in the note with SHA-256 b6ae4294a3a7227f5b93af51190a798403654d3f0e00c10da7f4a740428eba72. Neither nonstandard hardness hypothesis currently has a registered strength in the fixed CircuitSAT registry.

**Rounded experiment.** At lambda >= 8 put N=lambda^2, d=lambda, M=4N+2, b=2lambda+24. Sample a public odd b-bit prime p by testing at most b^3 uniformly drawn odd b-bit candidates. A common abort is observable in both branches. In the successful experiment draw a secret uniform root tau in F_p^d except the all-zero vector; zero coordinates are allowed. For each signed literal at index i use exponent 2i-1 or M-(2i-1). Publish a separate uniform nonzero diagonal and uniform additive vector per signed tag. Its value is the coordinatewise affine image of the componentwise power of tau. Condition on the complete successful public setup, including p and salts, in the calculations below. Exact field draws can use expected-time rejection; a bounded transducer must account for truncation, timing and aborts. Exact capped rejection gives the same uniform value upon success; if abort odds or observable timings depend on secret data, they require a separate joint-law argument. Independent uniform odd candidates return the prime law conditional on passing the test; the finite abort probability depends on the actual fraction of primes in the candidate interval.

At most lambda^4 distinct classical queries are allowed. Each consists of two ordered signed literal indices u,v, bounded gate and bit index g,j < lambda^4, and row and slot r,s < 4, with injective versioned encodings. Fix a fresh query before generating its header. Return uniform h in F_p^(3d), uniform b0 in F_p, and one rounded bit from b0 plus the inner product of h with (x,y,x coordinatewise-times y), reducing to [0,p) and taking floor(2z/p). The alternative returns the same header distribution and a fresh fair bit. Cache the entire record per address in both worlds. Further classical/quantum computation can be arbitrary; there is no coherent access to the challenge oracle. There is no extra authorization, initialization key or terminal tester in this game. A distinct finite SHAKE256 version uses a scalar root and a single fixed-output bit per distinctly encoded tweak; its fixed-size state and rate provide no unrestricted growing security claim. The following distribution bounds are specific to the field projection.

**Finite block experiment.** The other assumption gives one classical 512-bit string: either the public, fixed IETF ChaCha20 block including feedforward on a uniform hidden 256-bit key, with zero 32-bit counter and zero 96-bit nonce, or a uniform 512-bit string. The caller does not learn the key, another block under it, a tester, or its first bit. The public function itself remains locally evaluable, including as a quantum computation. At fixed key size define a finite observer resource bound and advantage (the difference of output-one probabilities, twice the excess success over one half). The specification and test vectors fix the function and say nothing about the required advantage at that budget. [RFC 8439, section 2.3](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8439#section-2.3)

## Which bias is visible

With p fixed, write u=(p-1)/(2p). For every history, chosen fresh address, fixed hidden root and newly sampled h, averaging over the fresh b0 gives probability u of a real one. Consequently a fixed number J of *fresh* raw bits has the product Bernoulli(u) law after discarding the headers, even when the next query depends on earlier records. An optional stopping time can bias the selected prefix; extend to J fresh dummy records and then project to the stopped view for a distance upper bound. Repeated addresses return their old bit and header. If p is marginalized, the bit law is a mixture of products, with covariance Var(u) between different fresh bits. For equally likely primes 3 and 5 that covariance is 1/900. These primes illustrate the conditioning identity rather than constituting legal lambda parameters.

For fixed p the squared chi-square distance expression for the J-bit product against fair bits (the usual chi-square divergence) is (1+p^-2)^J - 1. Thus their total variation is at most one half its square root. The per-query hybrid J/(2p) is valid but looser. Conditioning also on *published* b0 and the root determines the real answer, so the bit-only bound cannot by itself certify the public transcript. For example the joint (header, root, answer) has TV exactly one half from the same (header, root) followed by a fair bit at one fresh address. This is an auxiliary-information comparison, not an observer in the stated game.

## Adaptive public transcripts

Let S=p^d-1. Call an ordered pair of roots bad if at least one of the signed tag vectors agrees; include identical roots. Write delta for the fraction of bad pairs among two independent uniform roots. A good pair has distinct ordered addresses for *every* allowed query, independent of which query a transcript selects. Additive salts disappear in comparisons between two roots under the same tag and nonzero diagonal scalars preserve those comparisons. The cross-product block need not distinguish roots when an operand block already does.

Draw independent public headers H_1,...,H_J in order of first use. This gives exactly the fresh-address law by lazy generation; a cached reply consumes no header. Fix the strategy's randomness for now. For two fixed good roots drive both executions using this same header tape. On any common prefix the next address agrees. Their projections differ at some coefficient. After conditioning on every other component of the new h, the pivot and b0 map bijectively to the two pre-rounded residues; their coefficient difference is nonzero. This is the verified dither_pair_bijective. Thus the two residues are independent uniform *over the new public randomness*, even conditional on prefix agreement. The probability their rounded bits agree at that step is
gamma = u^2+(1-u)^2 = (1+p^-2)/2.
The probability of agreement through J fresh steps is gamma^J. The checked adaptive_branch_mass gives the finite-tree induction; it does not assume that future queries are fixed in advance. Unrelated fresh fields need not be collision-free; the calculation samples headers with replacement.

The same upper bound applies when each body XORs its rounded bit with a bit determined by the hidden root, previous history, query and public setup *before* this header arrives. Conditional on the two trajectories, different pads of this kind change the agreement probability to 1-gamma <= gamma. This is a statistical extension of the game; a computational oracle reduction does not automatically obtain an unknown root-dependent plaintext from a bare challenge bit.

For a fixed header tape the strategy and root determine a J-bit body string. Write P_H for its law over the random root. Averaging the conditional collision probability sum_y P_H(y)^2 over H gives at most delta+(1-delta) gamma^J. Cauchy–Schwarz applied to centered occupancies, then Jensen on the square root, proves the full-transcript bound
```
TV <= (1/2) sqrt( (2^J-1)delta + (1-delta)((1+p^-2)^J-1) ).
```
Here the ideal experiment has the same header tape and independent fair body bits. Addresses, cached repeats and the delivered transcript are functions of the tape, fresh bodies and the strategy. For a randomized classical-message quantum observer, each fixed classical interaction history determines a transition kernel for the next communicated query and the corresponding conditional internal state, the same kernel in both worlds. A fixed private random tape can implement those kernels for the purpose of this information-theoretic argument; no efficient classical simulation of quantum computation is asserted. Convexity and postprocessing give the same bound. This requires independent initial auxiliary state, or the explicit initial-leakage conditions below.

For the raw unmasked game one may instead compare to independent Bernoulli(u) bodies with the same header tape. For any target bit string y of biased-product probability q(y), a good pair traces y together with probability q(y)^2 by the same sequential calculation; a bad pair does so with probability at most q(y). The expected chi-square divergence against the biased product is at most (2^J-1)delta. This isolates the known marginal bias from entropy loss and does not replace the fair-bit ideal in the specified game.

For an arbitrary odd prime let G be the set of different gcd(e,p-1) for the scheduled exponents. Redundant divisors may be removed when a multiple is present. For any nonempty I in G the intersection of its tag-collision relations has exactly (1+(p-1)gcd(I))^d-1 ordered nonzero-vector root pairs: in one coordinate either both are zero or the first is nonzero and the ratio belongs to the intersection of the multiplicative root subgroups; exclude the zero vector pair once. Hence an exact bad-pair probability is
```
delta = S^(-2) sum_{empty != I subset G} (-1)^(|I|+1) ((1+(p-1)gcd(I))^d-1).
```
Union-bounding per root gives the simpler delta <= F/S with F=min(S, 1+sum_e(gcd(e,p-1)^d-1)) <= 2N (M-1)^d. A permutation-only profile has delta=1/S. Public salts require no extra hybrid. Use the bound conditional on p and average with the probability of successful public setup; a shared abort atom contributes zero distance. A general prime sampler has no guaranteed safe-prime output, nor is that assumption needed here.

Suppose a string L is revealed before the headers as a deterministic function of the root and public setup, with at most B possible values. Conditional on its fiber of size s, a given root has at most F bad partners. Thus the collision argument uses at most min(1,F/s). Average over fibers with weights s/S, then apply Jensen: the same bound holds with delta bounded by min(1,BF/S). One can simulate the *same* initial leakage distribution by drawing an independent copy of the root and computing L, then drawing independent fair bodies; that fake root need not be used in the bodies. Public/independent coins can be conditioned on throughout. This only gives a WE switch if L itself is available to the simulator or is efficiently reproducible with the correct allowed inputs and with all other published information. Whole same-root labels, adaptive openings, public data chosen using a *current or future* header, or a root-dependent terminal correction not included in L require separate analysis. A terminal observation computed solely from the already simulated public view is harmless by postprocessing.

**Concrete literal regime.** At lambda=128, N=16384, d=128 and b=280. The full fixed-width public salt arrays require 4Ndb = 2348810240 bits. Each fresh (h,b0,body) takes 107801 bits. Even ignoring all other costs, the strict exploratory 2^32-bit budget permits at most **18053** fresh records in a literal serialization with these arrays. For every allowed p>=2^279, S>=2^(279*128)-1. Even allowing B=2^15000 deterministic initial leakage values, 2^J BF/S <= 2^-540: F <= 32768 * 65537^128 and 65537^128 <= 2^2050. The checked strict_literal_entropy_budget verifies the integer and exponent inequalities. Put x=p^-2; since Jx<1/2 the binomial sum is bounded by a geometric series with ratio Jx, so (1+x)^J-1 <= 2Jx < 2^-542. The resulting full TV is **less than 2^-270**. The cap here is a physical serialization filter, not the registered 2^100 bound, and does not by itself supply a WE packet or affordable decoder. Removing unused salts or deriving them from a public seed changes this record-budget implication. The entropy proof for J under its stated bounds remains applicable if its source and header laws are retained.

Conversely, for any fixed public tape and strategy, at most S body strings can occur in the pure real game. Independent fair strings assign them probability at most min(1,S/2^J). Joint statistical distance is therefore at least max(0,1-S/2^J) on the header-plus-body view for a fixed J deterministic strategy. At J=lambda^4 and large lambda this leaves the expanding game a genuinely computational question. The support count proves no efficient decision procedure. Short-disclosure results and this entropy ceiling coexist.

## Static router reduction

The graph at N=4224 has 33776 degree-two nonterminals, twelve degree-one, a terminal, 67564 outgoing incidences and 50672 unary or binary records. Independent uniform 256-bit seeds are sampled *with replacement*, and the fixed source-opening mask is independent of all seeds. A row contains its child seed XORed with one half-block pad of each parent, with disjoint incidence slots. A compatible triple opens the terminal; privacy concerns the union of a fixed source set whose closure excludes it. The terminal emits beta XOR bit zero of its unexpanded seed, and the message-only view has no intermediate checkers. The flat payload is 12972033 bits including the terminal bit. The local mask supplier adds 3N*257 bits for its flags and authorized or zero seed fields. This supplier receives the authorization as input; it does not establish who may open a source in a general SAT construction.

The forward hybrid is valid on precisely this view. For a challenged dead nonsource, *every* incoming row has an already switched dead parent. Choose one fresh incidence pad on each such row. Different rows select different independent pads even when the same parent or child recurs; complementary incidence halves of a uniform 512-bit word are independent. Integrating out these pads makes all incoming rows jointly uniform conditional on the child seed and the remaining needed data. A challenged dead source is absent from the opened seed fields. Use the block sample jointly for both outgoing halves, keep rows into live children, and compute the other deterministic expansions on their independently sampled seeds. Accidental seed equality is allowed: known real expansions equal evaluation of the same public function whenever the challenge happened to use that seed; there is no equality-conditioned key sampler. In the fully switched world, toggling a terminal seed bit plus one exclusive uniform incidence bit in each incoming terminal record gives an exact coupling of both payloads. No single-block indistinguishability step may expose the hidden seed to initialize an opaque external wrapper.

For D dead nonterminals a uniform worst-case one-block acceptance gap epsilon yields 2D epsilon between real beta views. More precisely add both lists of per-hop advantages at their actual costs; simulate the observer once per hop with up to 33787 other block calls, source generation, framing and rendering of all visible records. A math-only single-block strength at the naked observer's runtime cannot be substituted. The largest two-sided simple loss 67576 costs about 32.09 bits in a squared-advantage comparison before wrapper time. No finite H or numerical QPT epsilon at that enlarged time is established. One block classically sampled for a QPT observer is a different interface from access to a keyed oracle. For the counter-one tester variant the challenge must contain two blocks for the *same* hidden key; marginal pseudorandomness alone cannot imply pair pseudorandomness (two identical copies of one pseudorandom block supply a generic counterexample). That comparison does not assert equality of actual ChaCha counter outputs. Revealing a hash/check predicate or a raw correlated seed bit changes the one-sample game unless its joint distribution can be simulated. The finite output range also has at most 2^256 elements within 2^512; computational hardness is a resource-qualified property, and RFC conformance establishes no such numerical value.

The reviewed local packet ledger quotes 2265773616 encryption ticks, 605128904 decryption ticks and a literal combined cost below 2^85 after its two large descriptions. This audit uses the static packet interfaces for the distribution argument, without independently refining the complete transducers or inheriting their bounds for upstream WE. Context-dependent seed delivery, pooling of openings into another closure, or auxiliary fields depending on dead keys are the principal missing reductions. The fixed lattice registry does not contain either new assumption.

Small exact controls used adaptive, header-dependent second queries over F3 and F5. Two-step good-pair agreement was respectively 25/81 and 169/625. Enumeration of all nonzero two-coordinate roots for p=3,5,7,11,13 and exponents 1,3,5,9 agreed with the inclusion-exclusion formula. These fixtures exercise the mathematical identities; they do not measure computational security.
