**Compiling a projection.** Split the original witness into eliminated bits e and retained bits z. At any NAND, an achievable one is exactly the OR of the two individually achievable zeros; the other parent is evaluated on whichever complete extension supplies that zero. An achievable zero needs two ones on the same extension. Repeated parents are a NOT. The disjoint-scope recurrence is the special case of the following exact merge with trivial interface.

For fixed z and ancestor constraints let the surviving e range over an affine translate of V. Suppose the two child functions depend on e only through the linear observations L e and R e respectively. Retained-dependent offsets and arbitrary Boolean postprocessing of those observations are allowed. Write H=(ker L intersect V)+(ker R intersect V). For two complete points x,y in the current fiber, an extension with the left observation of x and the right observation of y exists exactly when x-y belongs to H. Indeed, write x-y=a+b in the two kernels and choose x-a=y+b. Conversely any glued point supplies that decomposition. This is the glue theorem. Label a point by its coset in V/H (using one affine origin). The splitJoin theorem identifies simultaneous satisfiability with an OR over cosets of the AND of the two conditioned possible-one predicates. It never combines rows from different cosets.

Over F2 the branch exponent r=dim(V/H) is the dimension of the intersection of the two observation row spaces **on V**. Restrict by all ancestor constraints first. For example, e1 and e2 are independent before conditioning, but agree on the line e1=e2; opposite required values then have no common witness. In dual representation, reduce each child's rows modulo the current constraint row space, intersect the two reduced spans by Gaussian elimination and branch only on a basis of that intersection. After the split one can discard every ancestor equation invisible to the child, retaining exactly the intersection of the full constraints with its observation row span, with inherited right sides. Memoize by (node, requested value, canonical local affine equations). The attached compiler supports arbitrary NAND networks over affine eliminated-bit leaves with retained-variable offsets. Ordinary raw input wires are singleton affine leaves, so applicability is universal, while the chosen linear summaries govern efficiency. Recognizing an XOR subcircuit or certifying a more general compressed observation pays preprocessing.

This number of cosets is also exactly the minimum rectangle cover for universal pairing of the two observation spaces in this fiber. Each coset gives one complete rectangle and different cosets are disconnected. Selecting one pair from each coset gives an identity support; one monochromatic rectangle cannot cover two selected diagonal pairs (rectangle theorem). This bound is for gluing *arbitrary observations*; fixed predicates may need far fewer tests.

**Polarity and conditional Fourier certificates.** In an observation basis, suppose both achievable-one predicates can be pushed to the same favorable extreme in some shared coordinates, while private coordinates remain unrestricted. After splitting the others, push both separate witnesses to those extremes, preserve the respective private observations, and then glue. The polarity theorem checks the Boolean pasting step with explicit favorable-extension premises. Physical disjointness of parents is unnecessary. The companion sign-propagation procedure records sufficient syntactic directions by reversing sign at each NAND, and brute force checks its recurrence.

Another certificate is predicate-specific. For each fixed retained input, let F and G cover the supports of the Boolean indicators' Walsh transforms on the current eliminated direction space. The expectation of their product is the sum of matching Fourier coefficient products; in the absence of a common nonconstant frequency it is the product of their individual expectations. After conditioning on all values of a subspace U of dual frequencies, characters restrict to the same direction character iff they differ by U; their constant signs may depend on the fiber. If (F+U)/U and (G+U)/U have no common nonzero residue, independence holds on every fiber and separate feasibility can be joined there. Use a support envelope valid for all retained inputs, or charge the retained-dependent certificate. The *least* U meeting this support-only test can be found by starting at zero. Whenever a in F and b in G have a+b in U but a outside U, append a to a basis. Every feasible superspace must contain a (forcedFrequency theorem); termination takes at most the ambient dimension productive insertions. The corresponding numerical predicate test can be smaller when nonzero terms cancel. The replay checks the conditional criterion and its minimality on small systems, and a 127-variable full-period-rank pair with disjoint nonconstant spectral frequencies. Spectral certificates do not improve the universal rectangle bound.

**Size of the projected circuit.** Let M count distinct requested and canonically memoized reachability states and r_v the local split dimension of a possible-zero joint state. Using NAND, a pairwise AND costs at most two gates and an OR costs three. A balanced join on 2^r_v pairs adds at most 5*2^r_v gates and at most 2+2r_v levels above the two children. A possible-one state costs at most three gates and two levels. Repeated parents and constant outcomes can simplify. Thus size is bounded by the retained offset graph plus 3M+5*sum_v 2^r_v, and depth by the largest sum of the corresponding local increments along a path. A coarse M bound for l eliminated inputs is 2g times the number of affine subspaces of F2^l (plus original leaves); for coordinate-only conditioning, 2g*3^l suffices. The algorithm generates only reached states. Arbitrary truth tables on the same l observations can require all 2^l classes for universal observation pairing; a small root split alone does not bound ancestor-induced state counts or the cost of finding useful affine summaries. Operation counts for a compiler using lists include polynomial-time Gaussian reduction in l per queued edge, and linear search through prior memo keys if used. The replay verifies 4,320 retained-input projections against full enumeration.

**One scalar reference.** In the independent-row template of the predecessor, write B_j in Z_q^(n by m), gadget G padded to m>=nt, t=ceil(log2 q), and d uniform in Z_q^n. The newly compiled J(z) is a Boolean, so take P=BD(d) with just one m-coordinate binary column and GP=d. For each z independently sample a row secret s and issue the k input vectors of m residues, one reference scalar c_ref=(B_J P)^T s+U_R, and t_D=d^T s+e_D+Delta*beta. At an accepting witness J(z)=1, subtract P^T c_J-c_ref directly from t_D. The value is Delta*beta+e_D-P^T e_J+U_R, checked by oneReference. No residue lifting, division by a count, public rotations or repetitions are needed. For the conventional odd q, Delta=floor(q/2), a sufficient bound is 2(E+m W F_I+F_R)<Delta, where infinity errors satisfy |e_D|<=E, |U_R|<=F_R, |e_J|<=W F_I. One can use W=(m+1)^D for projected NAND depth D (or a smaller proved weight). There is no 2^l cofactor sum.

On a NO, every J(z) is zero. In the hybrid for one fixed z program B_j=A S_j-z_j G with the predecessor's independent sign columns; simulation gives B_J=A S_J on this row. Input and reference bodies are generated from one challenge response with the **same** unknown secret s; the single reference is its known S_J P projection. The target scalar uses that same row's extra challenge column at d. Fresh independent floods amortize no correlation across other z. The usual per-row conditional shifts cost at most k*m²*E/(2F_I+1)+m²*W*E/(2F_R+1), plus header simulation and error tails, with endpoint/hybrid multiplicities still chargeable. The challenge has m columns for A and an extra for d; each other table row has its own independent secret and is regenerated using the common programmed public matrices. The concrete fixed-distribution machine simulation, strength, flood widths and full honest clocks remain open. In particular do not replace independent s across addresses by one reusable key with both bit encodings.

**Direct issuer comparison and a 4096-gate benchmark.** A public exact J also lets the issuer evaluate J at every reachable z, returning the payload if any accepts and fixed zero otherwise. Its worst-case work is compiler cost plus 2^k ordinary Boolean evaluations; no hardness assumption or encrypted rows are needed. With a fixed-width description of S gates on a sequential tape, a simple evaluator visiting at most four full value-table lengths per gate and reading the program and reinitializing per z uses O(2^k(S+k)^2 log(S+k+2)) tape steps plus compilation. Conversely encrypted common matrix evaluation is done once, so its cost is not generally 2^k times the full Boolean circuit size. Its uncompressed raw serialization is t*[knm+n+2^k(km+2)] bits assuming no transmitted B_J; an ordinary framed output doubles this and adds one. Receiver row access, public matrix evaluation, actual sampling, both clocks and both descriptions add to that floor. Uniform registration also requires an algorithm to compile the raw statement.

Here k=8, l=120. Partition the 120 eliminated wires into four disjoint nonempty groups of 30 and let a,b,c,d be their parities. The left child requires (a+c,b+d)=(1+T(z),0); the right requires (a+b,c+d)=(0,0), all in F2. Individually **both** children always have an accepting extension, even for the table that is constantly zero. Each group participates in both children and the parity of 30 bits has no uniform monotone direction in any single bit. Their linear observations share exactly the one parity a+b+c+d. The fourGroups theorem checks all 16 target pairs. Thus J(z)=T(z) exactly. The raw NAND circuit realizes every one of the 2^256 eight-input truth tables via 256 independently selected constant pins, one ordered mux tree, balanced group XORs, and the joint test. Gates not needed to reach 4096 are dead padding before the terminal. For T(z)=1 choosing every eliminated wire zero gives a valid full witness. The exact projection here is a 256-entry table; reading its 256 truth values once directly decides nonemptiness, including a unique late accepting address.

For the literal unsimplified depth-17 mux lookup used as a BGG evaluation path, n>=128, m>=nt, F_I>=1 and the sufficient worst-case error envelope above imply t>18 log2 m. Since m>=128, t>=127, whence m>=16256>2^13 and t>=235. The common matrices alone have at least 8*128*(128*235)*235 raw bits, already above 2^32. This is a bound for this *specific sufficient envelope*, not a lower bound on every gadget evaluation. It is separate from the registered goal's 2^100 ceiling.

LinearProjectRole.enc is a registered one-work-tape machine recognizing exactly the grammar on success. It checks the canonical parameter frame, all gate tokens 1^(8a+3)0 1^(4b+1)0, optional table pins, statement end and message. With an empty table it can terminate with fixed zero as soon as the flags end. A positive table proceeds through the entire fixed suffix before releasing the message. A 16-bit tape counter is initialized from each token's hard-wired length and decremented per one; a premature terminator or overflow rejects. The fixed marker cells before and after the counter are blank. Each decrement uses two moves plus at most twice the number of trailing zeroes (summed over counting down, below twice the initial length). Loading, testing and rewinding at each token fits the extra 100 transitions charged to it. The total encoder clock is 112,537,198; 236,109 states give a 110,499,012-bit canonical transition table. The copier decoder has clock 11,171,895 (covers the longest recognized body, witness and bit framing) and 294 description bits. The native pair costs **234,208,399**, including both roles. Finite table size and clock are checked on the actual core machine objects. Replays include the ordinary registered statement encoder. The finite-control walk is justified by the parser and tested on actual transitions; a machine-level all-input bisimulation has not been formalized. Other raw statements may be rejected even when satisfiable. There is no general bounded compiler, complete quantum WE, or universal capped submission in this result.

On the four 120-eliminated-input symbolic fixtures (empty, singleton, full, pseudorandom) the compiler visited 18 memo states, its largest local shared rank was one, and it examined 10 joint branches. All 1024 retained values agreed with the actual table. The separate conservative coordinate-polarity replay checked 1200 projected truth values. These counts concern the stated implementations.
