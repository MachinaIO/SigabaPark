## From public equality to recovery of a nonzero multiple

Here is a direct prime-modulus LWE alternative to the projected CPRF reference profile. It uses the Evalpk/Evalct/Evalsim identity behind Boneh, Gentry, Gorbunov, Halevi, Nikolaenko, Segev, Vaikuntanathan and Vinayagamurthy, *Fully Key-Homomorphic Encryption, Arithmetic Circuit ABE, and Compact Garbled Circuits*, sections 4 and 4.2 (May 2014). We give the NAND specialization and the extra reference row explicitly. The conditional reduction below uses ordinary decisional LWE. The costs and error budget for the registered security strength are open. [Paper](https://eprint.iacr.org/2014/356)

Let a witness be (u,z) with l eliminated and k retained bits, l+k=128. For each z form the ordinary integer count J(z)=sum_u C(u,z). Take an odd prime q>2^l, t=ceil(log2 q), an LWE dimension n, and m>=nt. For G use the n binary gadget blocks of t columns, padded with zeros to width m. Let g=(1,2,...,2^(t-1)). Independently choose uniform B_i in Z_q^(n by m) for the retained input bits and uniform d in Z_q^n. Set P=BD(d g), a public binary m by t matrix with GP=d g. All residues have canonical t-bit representations. With the rules below, compute once the public matrix B_J, independent of the chosen z. It can be sent in the header, or reconstructed from the statement and B_i. The receiver must still compute the public transformations for its evaluation path.

For each z in fixed order independently sample s in Z_q^n. Issue k input vectors c_i=(B_i+z_i G)^T s+U_i; a t-coordinate reference c_ref=(B_J P)^T s+U_R; and a scalar d^T s+e_D+floor(q/2)b. Each coordinate of U_i or U_R is independent uniform on the centered integer interval of radius F_I or F_R respectively. Use the registered LWE scalar error distribution for e_D, with bound E or a charged tail. The issuer needs neither the auxiliary matrix A in the reduction nor honest sign matrices. It can precompute B_J P once. Other rows use fresh s, fresh scalar error and fresh floods. All output rows and common parameters are public.

Represent a public gate value x by a common matrix B_x and a row c_x=(B_x+xG)^T s+e_x. For a constant v use B_v=-vG and c_v=0, including the eliminated bits fixed within each cofactor. For NAND(x,y) set R=BD(-B_x), B_out=-B_y R-G and c_out=-R^T c_y-y c_x. Since GR=-B_x this has plaintext 1-xy, and its error infinity norm grows by at most m+1 per level. The coefficient y is public to the evaluator who computes each clear gate on its particular input and cofactor. Compute the count by simply adding all cofactor matrices and rows. No product tree or nonzero-test circuit is necessary. At a valid witness, 1<=J(z)<=2^l<q. The difference P^T c_J-c_ref then equals g^T J(z)(d^T s) plus bounded errors. Recover this scalar first, multiply by the inverse of the **known exact count** modulo q, and open the scalar message using the nearer of its two encoding centers. Dec must count all cofactors at the chosen z (or otherwise establish the count); the witness alone only proves positivity.

To recover any u in Z_q from approximations to g^T u with coordinate errors <q/6, normalize each coordinate by q. Maintain a real lift to 2^j u/q. At the next coordinate add the integer making its observed value closest to twice the previous lift, treating the initial representative modulo one. The true integer is within 3 times the normalized per-coordinate error, hence is the unique nearest integer. The last lifted value, divided by 2^(t-1), estimates u/q modulo one with error below 1/(2q). Round to an integer after multiplying by q. Rational arithmetic suffices. Thus inversion of J never amplifies ciphertext noise. Require the scalar-message error E below half the smaller circle distance between the encoding centers.

## One selected NO row

Use ordinary decisional LWE with uniform columns (A,d), where A is n by m, and responses a=A^Ts+e_0, v=d^Ts+e_D, or independent uniform (a,v). The challenge address z* is fixed for this hybrid. Sample independent sign matrices S_i of size m by m, and program B_i=A S_i-z*_i G. A uniform A gives a two-universal hash on sign vectors for odd prime q, since distinct sign columns have a nonzero difference coordinate. The leftover hash lemma and a column hybrid give total variation at most delta_B=(km/2)sqrt(q^n/2^m) from independently uniform B_i, even with A. This requires entropy overhead beyond the minimal gadget width. In the initial parameter replacement all real row noises are independent of these signs, so no auxiliary-input extension of this leftover estimate is needed.

For a programmed gate, B_x=A S_x-xG. Keep S=0 at constants and update S_out=-(S_y R+y S_x), with the same R as in the real matrix evaluation. At the count output B_J=A S_J-J(z*)G. Every cofactor is zero on NO, so B_J=A S_J. Simulate the challenged row by S_i^T a+U_i, P^T S_J^T a+U_R, and v+floor(q/2)b. All other rows, whether carrying the old or new bit, can be generated normally from public matrices and sampled secrets. With real LWE responses the shifts in the input floods are S_i^T e_0, and the reference flood shift is P^T S_J^T e_0. A uniform response v in the ideal distribution hides the challenged message even given all the other outputs. Replacing each row in turn yields a sum of at most twice 2^k individual LWE distinguishing gaps and statistical errors, at the actual adapter cost of producing the other rows and S_J. The core has no statement-specific hardness assumption. This is a paper reduction, not a verified implementation or a parameterized Submission.

For a fully explicit conservative accounting, let h bound original cofactor NAND depth and H=2^l(m+1)^h. Each note-specific NAND rule bounds the largest column l1 norm of S_J by mH and the ciphertext error of c_J by H F_I. Each column of P has l1 norm at most m. Hence it suffices for correctness that m H F_I+F_R<q/6. Conditioned on |e_0|_infty<=E, simulating one row's floods incurs total variation at most

    k m^2 E/(2 F_I+1) + t m^2 H E/(2 F_R+1).

Add delta_B and the LWE error-tail probability for the endpoint. This follows by independently shifting coordinates of uniform interval noises, and holds conditional on all other programmed values. Unbounded error distributions need an explicit tail in both roles. Picking q large can make this system consistent at the expense of the modulus bit width and lattice strength; it does not establish 128-bit security.

## Costs and distinct row secrets

The common raw matrices B_i require knmt bits. Each row uses (km+t+1)t bits without labels; the reference has only t residues. A straightforward issuer precomputes one n by t matrix B_J P, performs the cofactor B evaluations once, and pays independent dense input matrix-vector products and sampling costs per z. A receiver pays indexed table access, public NAND transformations and row evaluation for its projection, integer count evaluation, t scalar lifting steps, modular inversion and the final scalar decision. The simulator additionally evaluates S_J. The input tables and outputs of arithmetic have not been converted into registered machine clocks.

For the concrete *literal materialization* implementation assume n>=128, a global h=4096 and the above sufficient correctness condition with F_I>=1 and m>=nt. This forces t>4096 log2(m), first implying t>2^14 from m>=128, then t>2^16 from m>=128t. For k>=1 one row writes at least kmt bits, and separately materializing one n by m q-matrix per cofactor accounts for at least nmt bits per cofactor. Their unit-cost product is at least k n^3 t^4 >2^85, whereas the two-workload inequality in the companion note requires product at most 2^70. For k=0 the naive literal cofactor loop alone has 2^128 iterations. This excludes this materialization strategy using the stated conservative all-depth certificate. It is not a noise lower bound, and does not cover symbolic sharing, shallow specialized circuits, streaming representations or other encrypted evaluation rules.

A tempting compression replaces different z secrets by a single s and gives both input encodings per coordinate. For equal B_i the difference of these two rows is G^T s plus the noise difference. At the small-noise parameters required for correct gadget recovery this exposes s independent of any NAND witness. Per-row independent secrets avoid that identity. Any compressed seed or related-row schedule needs its own statement-independent joint simulation. Finite tests checked the public, ciphertext and programmed NAND identities with the scalar bookend for 16 random fixtures and all 31 secrets at q=31. A separate exact rational test recovered every residue for every {-1,0,1} error pattern at q=11,13,17,19,23. These checks certify neither a hardness estimate nor a full-machine budget.

For a sharper certificate, NAND is commutative, so charge the factor m to the smaller parent weight: W_out<=max(W_x,W_y)+m min(W_x,W_y). Repeated parents are a NOT, implemented by B_out=-B_x-G and c_out=-c_x with no amplification. For a formula with L leaves, orienting the larger leaf-count subtree as x makes every factor-m descent reduce the remaining leaf count by at least half. Expanding this inequality gives W_root<=L m^floor(log2 L) for unit leaf weights. An orientation minimizing computed weights can only improve this certificate. For a shared DAG, define V_0=V_1=1 and V_{i+1}=V_i+m V_{i-1}. In chronological order, two distinct gate parents have two different indices; a new gate can have at most one immediately preceding gate. Thus the i-th new gate has weight at most V_{i+1}, by induction, treating original wires as unit weights. This weighted Fibonacci certificate grows asymptotically as ((1+sqrt(1+4m))/2)^i at fixed m. It changes the depth charge, not the field or finite table identities. Counting formula leaves after DAG expansion may itself be exponential.
