Use a degree-1024 ring modulo X^1024+1 and q=2^19. The proposed source has 512 independent uniform headers with common uniform secret t, and independent coefficient errors uniform on {-1,0,1}. This is a conditional real/ideal decision comparison. No registry hardness score or machine cost is certified here. For a reserved row write L=A t+e, H=q/2. On an admitting false statement, the complete affine helper from the companion note is simulatable by its zero-phase law conditional on the whole fixed challenge. Other released prefix data must have a joint simulator from that same challenge; the affine example needs no prefix. Encryption from fair coins may approximate each of the eight needed ternary errors by reducing a uniform 160-bit word modulo three. The resulting total variation penalty is less than 8*2^-160 per real view.

**Two unconditional phase bits.** Let z=(At)_0. Seal the index (two bits, independent mask coins) of its nearest multiple of q/4, with any fixed tie rule. Send the public header and y=L_0+bH modulo q. Subtract the rounded phase; circular distance to bH is at most q/8+1<q/4, so decoding is exact. The NO simulator seals two zero bits without knowing t. A single reserved challenge row gives the real shifted body; in the ideal law it is a uniform independently shiftable scalar. This sends two scalar helpers plus 19*1024+19=19,475 raw explicit header/body bits.

**One guarded phase bit.** Consider eight consecutive scalar coefficients indexed i=0,...,7 of the same reserved row. Use the least index with lower residue (L_i mod H) in {1,...,H-2}, or index zero on fallback. Seal just the top bit of the noiseless phase z_i=(At)_i mod q. Publish i, A, and y_i=L_i+bH mod q. The honest decoder takes the XOR of the sealed bit opened by its witness and the top bit of y_i. On a guarded coordinate z_i and L_i have equal top bit because their distance is at most one and the low residue is away from both boundaries. The Lean guarded-phase lemma proves the stronger exact integer quotient statement for signed errors. Index selection depends only on publicly readable low residues; selecting it using the true secret phase without a simulatable selection rule would require another argument.

Here is a sample-size proof for eight correlated coordinates of one random ring product. Conditional on t, consider its reduction modulo two, and let d be its (X+1)-adic valuation, capped at 1024. The eight coefficient functionals on the independent header A are cyclic shifts of the reversed coefficient vector of t modulo two. A nonzero dependence would produce a nonzero polynomial of degree below eight annihilating that reversed polynomial in F2[X]/(X+1)^1024. Reversal preserves d, since substituting X^-1 changes X+1 by a unit factor. The least degree of a nonzero annihilator is 1024-d when d>0 (if d=0 there is no nonzero annihilator in the quotient). Thus d<=1016 suffices. Equivalently the 8-by-1024 matrix has an odd 8-by-8 minor, invertible over Z/(2^19), so a uniform A produces eight independent uniform scalar phases. This conclusion uses the conditional rank of the rotation measurements, not a blanket independence claim about arbitrary coefficients given t. Uniform t has d>=1017 with probability 2^-1017. On good t the eight L_i remain independent uniform after independent errors, so all eight fail the guard with probability (4/q)^8=2^-136. The total correctness failure is at most 2^-136+2^-1017<2^-128. This bound holds even for biased errors supported on {-1,0,1} sampled independently of the phases. An alternative uses eight independent reserved rows and their constant coefficients; one odd secret coefficient suffices there, with exception at most 2^-1024.

NO reduction: get the full reserved header/body, apply the same guard rule to the eight low residues, seal zero with fresh affine masks, and shift only the selected scalar by bH. The real law agrees with the honest implementation up to its stated noise approximation. In the ideal law, conditional on all low residues, the header, and any independently available preceding challenge rows, the selected top bit is uniform. Hence both ideal bit laws coincide even on fallback. Other front material cannot be correlated with the unshifted top bit unless its complete joint simulation justifies the same conclusion. A fair choice of b and appropriate output complement converts an endpoint distinguishing gap into half that gap for the fixed source, before approximation losses. Neither this reduction nor the security of the restricted affine release requires lattice hardness for direct scalar release on that same circuit subclass.

The helper uses B raw bits, plus 3+19456+19=19,478 raw index/header/body bits when transmitted explicitly. For B=129*2^k, framing this single payload with length 2(B+19478)+1 fits below 2^32 at k=23 and exceeds it at k=24. For the parity fixture B=4. Honest preparation of the eight selected-coordinate candidates can use 8*1024 modular products of 19-bit coefficients with negacyclic signs, modular addition, 19*1024 fair bits each for t and A, plus eight error samples and the affine mask coins. The reduction must read the relevant challenge material, sample the zero helper, and write the full encoded output. These are raw operation and bit counts, not registered tape clocks. Changing to this terminal removes full anchor/lift reconstruction, and does not by itself yield a frontend on arbitrary false NAND statements.

**Why a fixed one-bit phase quantizer alone fails.** For one fixed scalar index and one bit f(z) independent of the noisy body, exact decoding would require f(z) unequal to both f(z+H) and f(z+H+1) for every modular z. Otherwise one can obtain equal public body and hint at opposing messages with errors differing by zero or one. The two inequalities imply f(z)=f(z+1), contradicting the first. The Lean conflict lemma works without even assuming periodicity when z ranges over the integers. In the uniform z, uniform b, uniform ternary-error model, the best possible average error for any such binary hint is exactly 4/(3q), even allowing an arbitrary decoder. To see this put g_i=f(i)-f(i+H) as integers and extend by antisymmetry. The maximum-likelihood error times 6q is 3q minus the sum over i of |g_(i-1)+g_i+g_(i+1)|. The maximum of this sum on the cube [-1,1]^H is attained at a sign vertex, which is feasible. There must then be a change of sign and its antipodal mate; at the four distinct adjacent centers the loss is two each, for q>=4. The two constant opposite semicircles attain eight total loss. Conditional on any fixed header having at least one odd coefficient the unmasked fixed phase is uniform over a uniform secret; such a header occurs with probability 1-2^-1024. The lower bound applies with no additional secret-dependent side information and fixed shared coins. A body-dependent bit selecting the closest of R and R+H to z, where R is the noisy low residue, can trivially describe the top bit of the unshifted body; it lies outside that lower bound. The guarded-index construction instead seals the actual top phase bit of a selected coordinate.

Finite replays checked all phases, both messages and all three errors for q=8,16,32; every fixed phase-bit quantizer for q=6,8; a small entire ideal selected-index distribution; and the cyclic row-rank criterion for every binary secret through ring degree 16. These checks complement the algebraic derivations. The source hardness field, complete machine implementation/pricing and general compact NO certificate family remain open.
