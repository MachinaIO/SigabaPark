The 128 inputs and 4,096 claimed gate outputs can each select just one instruction while computing an exact NAND-verification energy. This removes repeated choices from the honest verifier. A clear or secretly affine-masked terminal that opens the message throughout the zero-energy hyperplane remains unsound on a concrete all-live false instance. Two ordinary binary claim paths suffice to manufacture a point of that hyperplane. Public group multiplication gives a separate obstruction even in handles that do not expose affine coordinates.

### Relation and one-pass instructions

Wire zero and unavailable indices read true, including self and future references. With x,y the actual claimed inputs to a gate and z its claimed output, put t=1−z. The integer penalty is xy−2xt−2yt+3t. Its values on the eight Boolean triples lie in {0,1,3}; it vanishes exactly for z=NAND(x,y). Append the penalty 1−out. For any gate list and input, zero total energy occurs for a trace iff the registered SigabaNeo.evalGates accepts that input. The zero_iff_registered Lean theorem uses the actual registered evaluator and its getD convention and proves both directions by following the claimed/honest gate lists. quadratic_and_bounded proves the integer local polynomial and a global bound 3g+1 on g claimed rows. In particular all energies on the 4,096-gate problem lie between 0 and 12,289. Use the prime p=16,381, so zero reduction mod p entails genuine zero. The quadratic scan is on the complete 4,224-bit candidate. Honest decryption starts with the 128-bit witness and computes the 4,096 gate bits by the registered evaluation before accessing them in the advertised order.

Expand Q=c+Σ l_i w_i+Σ_(i<j) C_ij w_i w_j. Before coalescing, each gate contributes at most the pairs xy, xz and yz. Substitute the constant true, fold repeated binary variables using w_i²=w_i, sort variable pairs in the chosen order, and add duplicate coefficients. Expanded local terms are xy−2x−2y+2xz+2yz+3−3z. Implementations may keep duplicates with the same net result. Initialize an accumulator at c, and future[j] at l_j. For each i, if its single externally selected bit is one add future[i] to the accumulator and add C_ij to each later future[j]. Otherwise leave the state unchanged. Reordering inputs changes the orientation of each unordered monomial, not its product. The induction invariant is the partial energy on the processed prefix with unprocessed bits zero, and future[j]=l_j+Σ_(i processed) C_ij w_i. It proves an exact one-pass energy for all binary traces. The replay checks integer direct versus compiled mod-p energy on 128 full registered traces including forward aliases.

Equivalently use the Heisenberg multiplication (a,b,c)(a',b',c')=(a+a',b+b',c+c'+a·b'). In dimension n=4,224 take a_i=e_i and b_j supported on indices i<j with coefficient C_ij. The zero instruction is identity; the one instruction is (a_i,b_i,l_i). With initial central value c, the final central coordinate is Q; the horizontal parts need not vanish. ordered_pairs in Lean proves that a pass accumulating the products of a bilinear pairing yields the sum over all ordered earlier/later pairs. Feed a zero triple for a zero bit. The list recurrence exposes each pair once; the elementary coefficient collection and registration-specific sparse Python implementation have not been compiled to a Lean Machine. For fixed word selectors, copying arbitrary repeated choices is unnecessary. Applying the entire program and its inverse in a terminal commutator would reintroduce the separate consistency condition of [task 164](https://github.com/MachinaIO/SigabaPark/pull/164).

### Boundary-state rank and storage

At a cut, let M be the prefix-by-suffix interaction matrix. The pending linear correction is w_prefixᵀ M, in a space of dimension r=rank M. With a public per-cut basis, its r coordinates plus the partial energy suffice: extract the coefficient for the next variable, restrict the old correction to the remaining suffix, then add the new row when selected. The basis maps may go between different dimensions; supply their computation and movement costs. For exact linear correction this affine dimension is minimal, since the zero prefix and all singleton prefixes realize zero and every row. Selecting only r linearly independent rows already gives 2^r distinct sums on binary prefixes. This need not bound the width of a verifier that answers only zero vs nonzero.

The all-live parity fixture first negates the 128 witnesses, combines them by 127 four-NAND XORs, then pads with 3,460 inverters. Its physical witness-first cut contains a diagonal 128×128 submatrix 4I from w_i to its copied NAND(w_i,w_i), giving rank 128. Of the 2^128 witnesses, the 2^127 odd-parity ones have different unique accepting trace suffixes; every even-parity witness has empty accepting suffix language. Thus an exact deterministic trace-language automaton in that order needs at least 2^127+1 distinct states at that cut. This is an explicit state-table obstruction, not a lower bound of 2^127 bits on working memory. On the same raw circuit, placing each next witness bit, its complement, and then its XOR gadget together yields a maximum of three live bits. Explicit reachability finds at most four nonrejecting states in any layer and 9,590 nonrejecting states summed across noninitial layers; add a rejecting sink for a total transition function. This language check tracks local NAND constraints and can discard already inconsistent paths earlier than a global accumulated energy. On a family for which a public order and bounded reachability really run within an agreed cap, the issuer can decide SAT and send the message on YES and a fixed byte on NO, with exact distributional security. Sorting the order, constructing and exploring the graph and the decoder's witness/input moves count. The fast order for this example is not a guarantee for arbitrary 4,096 gates.

For n=4,224 and m=4,096, at most 12,288 pairs survive, each representable by two 13-bit indices and one 14-bit residue. Including n slopes and the intercept, fixed-width coefficient data use ≤550,670 bits (68,834 bytes rounded up for the whole stream, in addition to record counts/framing). The future array and scalar are 59,150 bits. Logically at most n+12,288 field additions suffice after compilation, plus bit tests, addressing and initialization. A dense one-branch group table costs 499,640,064 bits and both full branches 999,280,128 bits before framing and masks; the sparse fixed construction does not require dense issuance. The core length_encGates_le gives an upper bound 103,821,312 bits for the unary gate-list encoding alone. Generating sorted/coalesced coefficients, reading the unary input and any heads seeking or rewinding tape are not included in the field-operation count. Registering an actual clock would additionally charge both transition descriptions, 6·s·3^k·(4+4k+size(0)+size(s)) per zero-oracle machine with s states, k work tapes. A fitting output bit length or Python run time is not a proof that Enc.time+Dec.time+Enc.descLen+Dec.descLen is below 2^32.

### The terminal failure uses two legal choices at every layer

Build a concrete all-live circuit. Gate 1 computes NAND(w₁,w₂). Gates 2 through 4,093 negate the preceding output. From the current u the last three gates compute n=NAND(u,u), k=NAND(u,n), and NAND(k,k), which is identically false. Every gate feeds the output. The all-zero bit string of witnesses and claims has e₀=12,289. Turning on just w₁ with all other claims still zero gives e₁=12,287. Both trials are legal branch selections and neither is an accepting trace.

Suppose the public evaluator can obtain its two end-state vectors s₀,s₁ and perform affine combinations in a common coordinate frame. Suppose the terminal mechanism opens β on **every** state for which the same possibly secret affine scalar L vanishes, and L(s(w))=ρQ(w) on honest path choices for a common scalar ρ. The terminal need not expose its coefficients. Set t=e₀/(e₀−e₁)=14,335 modulo 16,381. Then s*=(1−t)s₀+t s₁ has L(s*)=0 identically, hence the purported terminal returns the message on a false statement. The Lean two_trace_endpoint proves this over every field and vector space, with an arbitrary hidden affine offset. This also covers noninvertible intermediate dimension changes and affine linear authentication of the end state if only these affine checks are enforced. A left/right Heisenberg relabelling of the full coordinate vector is affine in its variable argument. This test is for terminals specified on the entire hyperplane; correctness on valid traces alone does not promise any particular value at s*. A representation that validates a nonlinear proof of reachability and releases no additional distinguisher needs a new complete-view proof.

Adding fixed polynomial moment tags need not fix the problem. If d+1 distinct positive energies are reached by binary path choices and the terminal checks the affine readouts of Q,Q²,…,Q^d, take their Lagrange weights at 0. Their sum is one and they recover the value at zero of every degree-at-most-d polynomial. The same weights transport all the readouts to the target simultaneously. moment_endpoint proves this shared family of weights. On a second 4,096-gate all-live NO circuit, set the 4,093 initial gates to u_j=NAND(u_(j−1),w₁) starting at constant true and use the same last-three-gate contradiction. With all inputs zero, independently flipping claims of the 2,046 even chain gates through index 4,092 adds one unit per flip and affects no other penalty; all neighboring gates still have second input zero. Hence all energies 1 through 2,047 occur. Clear affine moment readouts through degree 2,046 admit off-path interpolation on this instance. Conversely, if all distinct attained positive values form a known set S and d≥|S|, the polynomial ∏_(s∈S)(X−s) is nonzero at 0 and zero at every sample. It separates the target moment vector from their affine hull. This is an exact geometric positive condition for resisting those interpolations, not a compact way of hiding a public β-dependent program.

### A second check using only group operations

Suppose both masked edges e_i^b=R_(i−1)⁻¹ g_i^b R_i are accessible as group elements, g_i^0=1, and their inverses/products can be formed. The all-zero prefix P_(i−1) normalizes the local difference to D_i=P_(i−1)e_i¹(e_i⁰)⁻¹P_(i−1)⁻¹=R₀⁻¹g_i¹R₀. Thus the available group operations produce conjugates of whole words in the one-generators, far beyond the 0/1 language in fixed order. The public_group_direction Lean lemma verifies the frame cancellation and the common conjugation for a commutator. In the triangular Heisenberg assignment, [g_i,g_j] shifts only the center by C_ij for i<j. In the first all-live circuit C₁₂=1. Put the normalized commutator to power 4,092=−12,289 mod p before the product of zero edges. Its horizontal displacement is zero and its center cancels the all-zero energy. A terminal that opens throughout the central-zero hyperplane again opens β. This does not require interpolation in a visible coordinate frame, but does require the exposed group-operation interface. Random masks in the same group leave this identity intact.

Even changing the lower half of the public dot-product completion does not generally repair this terminal. If any two generators fail to commute, their commutator gives a nonzero central displacement and hence all displacements over the prime field. Otherwise the dot-product completion is symmetric, with arbitrary diagonal d_i. For any field powers t_i, the center of the product becomes c+Σ[l_i t_i+d_i t_i(t_i−1)/2]+Σ_(i<j) C_ij t_i t_j. On the four variables w₁,w₂,g₂,g₃, the cross minor with row indices (w₁,g₂) and column indices (w₂,g₃) is diag(1,4), independent of all diagonal choices. Thus the quadratic form has rank at least two (the symmetric matrix of the quadratic part has this minor scaled by 1/2). Restrict to a nonsingular binary diagonal subform and complete squares. Over an odd prime field a nondegenerate binary form represents every residue, because each scaled set of squares and the target minus the other scaled set both have (p+1)/2 elements. One can therefore construct powers with central energy zero using the public completion. The explicit triangular commutator and affine interpolation are tested in 100 random frames; the more general completion argument here is algebraic, not a Lean proof of finite-field diagonalization.

A terminal represented only as an ideal membership handle over a restricted set would require disjointness of the reachable closure under *all publicly available manipulations* from accepting encodings on false circuits, and indistinguishability of the actual program bytes. Algebraic read-once computation supplies neither property. No registered hardness assumption has been used to assert hiding; a fully private terminal, the corresponding concrete reduction and a uniform total budget below 2^32 remain open, as does the general 2^100-capped post-quantum construction.
