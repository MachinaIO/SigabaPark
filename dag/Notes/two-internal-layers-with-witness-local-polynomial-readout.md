### Restrict the potentials, then compute only what the witness can open

Suppose r_i is uniform on a public subspace U_i. The same forward translation proving NO secrecy works if the actual source shift f_i lies in U_i at each layer on false instances and the exposed terminal is shift-invariant. This is weaker than invariance of the entire ambient spaces under transfer. A witness may supply a row functional g_i with (g_i A_i−g_(i-1))|U_(i-1)=0, g_1 A_1 s nonzero, and an available g_last(r_last). Summing the g_i of successive residuals telescopes on every honest random tape. It need not reproduce the backward sums on the full ambient space. It does need actual evaluable residuals: a public program that simply contains a secret multiplier or a noncanonical coefficient on an identically zero function can leak it on false instances. In the construction below all fields are canonical coefficients in a fixed Boolean monomial basis.

Use the same abstract index j in {0,1}^n at successive internal layers. Source edges and successive identity-index edges are enabled by local Boolean flags a_1(j),...,a_k(j); the last edges to the sink are enabled by j in an affine set B. Take each a_i as a publicly certified low-degree polynomial. Let D_i be the sum of degrees through i, capped at n. Write U_i as all multilinear polynomials of degree ≤D_i for i<k; take U_k as the subspace of those polynomials vanishing on B. Set r_0=beta. Independently sample coefficient vectors r_i uniformly on U_i, and issue z_i=r_i+a_i r_(i−1) in the multilinear Boolean quotient ring (x_j²=x_j). All these tuples have a predetermined coordinate list. For a true supplied witness j in B every a_i(j)=1, r_k(j)=0 and sum_i z_i(j)=beta. Thus one evaluation functional at the witness per layer suffices; no terminal field is needed, since the terminal sum of potentials on B is identically zero. The issuer operates on one local polynomial and the preceding share at a time; it never forms the global acceptance polynomial.

On NO, p_i=a_1...a_i belongs to U_i for every i, including i=k because p_k vanishes on B. Translate r_i by p_i to switch the bit; multilinear identities keep every published coefficient unchanged. This argument is joint and exact, without hardness. On YES the actual forward polynomial need not belong to U_k. The ordinary unit-weight path sum can even vanish while the witness still opens. To generate U_k, take an affine parametrization j=j0+Vt of B and the vector Phi of all degree-≤D_k monomials. It suffices to generate the span of Phi(j0+Vt) using t of weight ≤D_k. Each vector-valued restricted function has multilinear degree at most D_k, and Boolean Möbius inversion expresses every coefficient as a sum of evaluations on a sub-support. The span thus equals the full evaluation span. Sample uniformly in its perpendicular by binary elimination. An empty B uses the whole coefficient space. At n=128 and degree two, there are 8257 coefficients. This is separate from the previous local-row-space projection approach (task #252); a single-layer kernel is its elementary dual formulation. The multilayer advance is the actual link through the masked intermediate polynomial a_i r_(i−1), the NO shift p_i in each different potential space, and pointwise witness cancellation without the full reverse covector. As degrees accumulate, feature counts may approach 2^128. No general small degree or compressed circuit compiler follows.

### An explicit 4096-NAND pair and its native core

Let u and v be parities of the first and last 64 input bits, respectively. Require at three successive stages u=1, v=1 and u+v=d. For d=0 there are exactly 2^126 valid primary witnesses; for d=1 there are none. All raw index layers have 2^128 states before compression. Both templates use ordinary 4-NAND XORs for each half, NANDs testing the last constraint and conjoining all three predicates, followed by unused constant gates and a final output-preserving gate. The included test constructs exactly 4096 legal gates with the constant at wire zero. The final affine set contains 2^127 indices in either case, so the ordinary unit-weight backwards covector immediately before the sink has that many nonzero raw coordinates. Even a true path sum of 2^126 vanishes in characteristic two.

Choose five fresh fair bits A,B,C,h,g. Let r_1=A+B u+C v and r_2=(u+v+d)(h+g u). The latter parameterizes exactly the two-dimensional degree-two space on the two independent summaries which vanishes on the final affine line. Send the three coefficients of r_1+beta u and four of r_2+v r_1, ordered as (1,u,v,uv). This is seven public bits. The false shift is r_1↦r_1+u, r_2↦r_2+uv; uv vanishes on u+v=1, so both uniform choices retain their laws. In the true case a valid witness evaluates the two fields at (1,1); their XOR returns the bit. Enumerating all 32 random tapes for both payloads yields identical supports and frequencies of size 32 on NO and disjoint supports of size 32 on YES. Recognizing these particular public templates makes their SAT decision easy. For general explicitly afforded bounded-degree features, one could also test by public linear algebra. These are restricted constructions, rather than a hardness claim about that public procedure.

TwoLayerPath.enc accepts predecoded d and beta, samples five fair bits and emits the seven coefficients using a 14-step zero-work-tape machine with cost 172814. TwoLayerPath.dec accepts the 128 raw witness bits followed by seven raw packet bits, computes u and v, evaluates the fields with a streaming parity accumulator, and uses 135 ticks at cost 98055. Their checked total cost is 270869. Neither machine includes the registered raw statement recognizer and surrounding tuple framing. The two templates and conceptual exponential graph are never expanded by these component machines. The calculation therefore prices the concrete core while leaving a general registered implementation open.
