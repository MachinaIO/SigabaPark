# Programming a public header with its own encrypted shift bit

Consider the following restricted cyclic sampling problem. Everything is over Z/q and all vectors have fixed public dimensions. At row i the native issuer samples uniform independent headers A,B under the same secret s, independent errors e,f, and produces

    V = <s,B> + <s,D(A)> + e + m(A)
    c = h(A,B,V)
    Y = <s,A−cG> + f.

The gadget column G and the functions D,m,h are public. They may use the earlier retained transcript, including its response bits. The function h is Boolean. In particular c can be a bit of a ciphertext of a secret-dependent affine expression and can depend on the very header being programmed. The real issuer returns A,B,V,Y. This is a restricted Regev packet with no GSW top gadget. The statement is a reduction to ordinary fixed decision LWE; it introduces no new hardness assumption.

**One trial.** Take two fresh challenge samples (H,L),(J,K) with one common secret. Guess one fair bit g; put A=H+gG, B=J−D(A), V=K+m(A). Keep the tuple (A,B,V,L) exactly if g=h(A,B,V). Otherwise consume another fresh pair. Conditional on any real s and prior transcript, (A,B) are uniform independent and (A,B,V) have the native inner law independently of g. On acceptance H=A−cG; the untouched response L has independent error f and the exact native outer law. Each trial succeeds with probability 1/2, and its retained law is independent of the number of discarded trials. The ideal challenge yields two uniform independent public bodies, even after the test, since L is untouched and g is independent of the proposed inner transcript. Discarded challenge samples are private to the reduction. This derivation cannot inspect L in the accept test. For instance with q=2, s=1, f=0, G=1 and c=L, acceptance forces A=0; the uniform-header reasoning would be false.

Use a fixed instance of decision LWE with 2T sample columns to produce M retained scalar rows. Pad the trial pool to T and on exhaustion return the same dummy result in either world. The challenge advantage of this reduction is exactly (1−δ) times the desired distinguishing gap, where δ=2^(−T) Σ_{j<M} binom(T,j). This is valid for prefix-adaptive public predicates: the success probability is conditionally constant and each accepted native output has the right conditional distribution. At M=1025 the smallest T with δ<2^-128 is 2728, or 5456 scalar samples. If a row has vector bodies, count each scalar column in each challenged block. Strength and machine costs must be recalculated at the actual enlarged fixed parameters. The retained row distribution and the reduction statement apply to classical sampling with a quantum final distinguisher; they do not supply a quantum hardness theorem for those parameters.

**What fails without rejection.** With one public deterministic bit c(A), pushing uniform A through H=A−c(A)G gives multiplicity 1−c(H)+c(H+G) at H. Its statistical distance from uniform equals half the probability that c changes along one G step. It is a permutation iff c is constant along every such orbit. An arbitrary c cannot simply be substituted into the independent-header argument. For c equal to the low bit, q even and G odd, the distance is 1/2. The rejection proof works for this case as well.

**Several programmed headers.** For bit functions of all native headers alone, form the public dependency digraph, drawing j→i when c_i uses A_j. Process strongly connected components in topological order. Inside a component guess bits on a feedback vertex set of size f. Form their A from challenge headers using the guesses. Process the remaining acyclic rows directly with their now-computable shifts and independent challenge headers, then check the f guesses. The tentative native header vector is uniformly distributed independently of all guesses (condition on s and the past). One attempt succeeds with probability 2^(−f), and all real retained bodies then match the native law. A component of size m thus uses m*2^f expected scalar challenges in the one-body case. Prefix auxiliary transcripts can be included. Within a cyclic component any auxiliary sampler used in the predicate needs its own proof of independence from provisional guesses; blindly conditioning on provisional outer bodies does not meet that requirement.

**Scope for Tree Encodings.** In ePrint 2026/741 §4.1, Lemma 12 programs a public matrix with bits from GSW ciphertexts. For the §4.4 self-dependent master cancellation, those ciphertexts can depend on the instance of the public CRS being programmed. The restricted construction above deals with such a dependency when the inner message is affine in the challenge secret at a publicly computable coefficient and the dependencies have the stated order. A full GSW ciphertext of a secret coordinate also shifts its top gadget: absorbing the plaintext into the top uniform header leaves a term containing a product of secret coordinates in the bottom row. The honest construction also contains a CRS-dependent master matrix. No representation of that full message in the admitted two-sample form is established. Moreover a full NO obfuscation must evaluate to zero, whereas a uniform ideal shifted key has an independent public pad that makes the output bit fair at a fixed probe. The lemma is a building block; replacing the entire NO key by independent uniform fields fails this simple test. A genuine application needs a common, structured NO simulator for both messages and a role cost below the goal cap.

## Why directional GSW self-messages do not complete the scalar construction

Let v=(s^T,−1), and encrypt a possibly secret-dependent matrix M(s) as [B;s^T B+e]+M(s)G, with a fresh uniform unpublished B. Condition on s and put A=B+(M(s)G)_top. The body is s^T A+e−v M(s)G. If the row v M(s) is affine in s with public coefficients, the entire fresh packet can be produced by affine header programming. Indeed there is a publicly known constant matrix M0 with v M(s)=v M0 at every secret. Encryption of M(s) has exactly the same distribution as encryption of M0, pointwise in s. Public auxiliary functions are allowed by conditioning; releasing the original B would change the argument.

For the usual GSW external product with an independently encrypted right operand, its fresh uniform top matrix B2 contributes v M(s)[I;s^T] B2. Universal exact cancellation of this contribution requires v M(s)[I;s^T]=0, equivalently v M(s)=m(s)v for a scalar m(s). The latter's last coordinate makes m affine if v M(s) is affine. Every earlier coordinate then requires s_i m(s) to be affine. Over the full binary cube in dimension k≥2 these simultaneous constraints force m to be constant over any Z/q: write m=d+Σa_j s_j and take the Boolean mixed difference in coordinates i,j of s_i m with i≠j. It is exactly a_j, whereas every affine map has zero mixed difference. Thus even arbitrary matrix-valued encodings cannot supply a nonconstant own-key scalar by this particular affine-programming and full external-product interface. Directional projections such as M(s)=s_j E_jj do satisfy affine programming since s_j²=s_j, but their decrypted row is not a common scalar times v. This is not a lower bound against general KDM or GSW constructions.

For the specific message s_j in a top gadget column of weight q/2, the target product (q/2)s_i s_j has mixed difference q/2 mod q. If all four centered approximation errors have magnitude <q/8, their signed sum has magnitude <q/2 and cannot be congruent to q/2. Consequently any fixed affine candidate misses the bound on at least one of the four Boolean corners; independent randomized affine candidates retain failure probability at least 1/4 on uniformly random corners. Only the scalar-corner obstruction is claimed, without assumptions about adversarial access to the secret. The Lean file checks the mixed differences and integer modular bound; gsw-affine-closure.py exhaustively checks the classification for several small cubes.


## The exact bounded transcript

Condition on the shared secret and on any advance-sampled tapes for the independent errors. At one real attempt let X consist of the two independent uniform headers. For fixed guess g their map to the native provisional (A,B) is headerPair: first translate H, then translate J by a function of the translated H. V is computed from this pair and the fixed inner noise. The outer noise is used only for L. In the ideal experiment also include the two uniform responses in X: compose the header permutation with K ↦ K+m(A), and leave L untouched. The public test can read V but not the unprogrammed response L. In either case this is a permutation pi_g. The transformation (x,g) ↦ (y=pi_g(x), accept=[g=h(y)]) is itself a permutation: given y and the accept flag, the guessed bit is h(y) or its complement, so invert pi_g. Thus uniform input is a product of an exactly native provisional seed and an independent fair flag, in both worlds. Integrate the initially conditioned noises back; all success flags remain independent of the entire independent noise tape and the common secret. Real outer rows agree with the native equations on acceptance, not necessarily on a rejected attempt.

The formal adaptiveTape works by induction on the whole bounded pool, passing the same updated prefix in the forward and inverse maps; use splitTrial at each step with that prefix. Native uniform seeds and flags then have product law. Conditional on any flag sequence with at least M successes, the first M success indices select independent native seeds and fresh noises, because unused trials do not update the retained prefix and raw trial indices do not alter the native algorithms. Inductively this gives exactly the required M-row adaptive transcript. Thus the final distribution is a mixture of the target law of weight p and the same fixed dummy of weight 1-p, where p=1−2^(-T) sum_{j=0}^{M−1} choose(T,j). Exact integer verification uses C(T,0)=1 and C(T,j+1)=C(T,j)(T−j)/(j+1); compare 2^128 times the sum to 2^T. Running time of the reduction includes up to T calls of the predicate and inner sampler, 2T scalar challenge columns for the one-column case, and complete output assembly. Independent ideal K and L are necessary. Per-column LWE hardness at an unrelated count is not a bound at these parameters.

## Two ways to carry a scalar key message

First, let t be an ordinary LWE secret in k dimensions. Independently sample a fair bit b, enlarge the secret to ((1−b)t,bt,b), and use v=(sᵀ,−1). The fixed linear transformation v ↦ (0,bt,b,−b) equals b v on this support. In a GSW column only the contraction with v matters after absorbing its top gadget shift into the fresh unpublished uniform matrix. This gives an honest GSW of b usable in the usual external product. To simulate the lifted native rows, put the challenge header in the active block, sample the entire inactive block and scalar selector header independently, and add b times that last header to the challenge response. The ideal response remains uniform. Dimension is 2k+1. The Lean selector_row and sample_body identities verify the scalar coordinate algebra. The conditional header permutation proves equality of full ciphertext distributions only with fresh raw headers and errors per column. The selector bit is sampled by the reduction.

When the reduction knows b, a smaller construction suffices: append known freshly sampled coordinates a to an independent hard secret t, giving (t,a). In ordinary native header (H,U) return L+⟨a,U⟩ and add the entire gadget f(a)G to the ciphertext. The unshifted headers and ideal bodies are uniform even conditional on a. The same reduction supports any number of scalar functions of a costed at their full column count. If a and t are uniform over the same alphabet the concatenated secret has its ordinary product law. This construction has no fixed public affine projector requirement; it preserves the entropy of the independent hidden core, and does not encrypt arbitrary functions of that core.

**All-coordinate low digits.** Fix q=d m, d,m≥2. Start from one decision-LWE challenge with uniform t in (Z/m)^n, independent rows (H,L) at modulus m, and error law E. Once for the entire ciphertext, independently sample a uniform vector a with digits in {0,...,d−1}. The native secret s=a+d t in (Z/q)^n is exactly uniform. Per scalar column sample independent upper header digits j_i in {0,...,d−1}, which give A_i=H_i+m j_i, and a fresh dither u uniform over any fixed integer interval of d consecutive integers. Return Y=d L+⟨a,A⟩+u mod q. In the real case Y=⟨s,A⟩+d e+u mod q; liftedColumn checks the scalar congruence including carries and digits checks the finite radix bijection. In the ideal case, conditional on a and A, d L+u is uniform mod q by the same bijection and a fixed translation. All rows are jointly independent uniform conditional on a in the ideal.

For each standard GSW encryption add f_j(a)G to the entire fresh block, both its top and bottom. This preserves the uniform ideal law conditional on a and gives precisely a GSW encryption of f_j(s mod d) under the **same standard uniform s** with native errors dE+u. Any classical side information sampled using a, independent public input and fresh coins can be retained; ideal rows are uniform given it. The proof permits sequential shifts chosen from previously emitted fields and a when the current unshifted block is independent; it does not justify shifts computed from the current raw challenge. One native n-dimensional, (n+1) by (n+1)ceil(log2 q) standard GSW block needs (n+1)ceil(log2 q) source scalar columns, one high header digit per native coefficient and one dither per column. It transmits (n+1)^2 ceil(log2 q)^2 fixed-width bits. For centered dither |u| ≤ floor(d/2) the honest magnitude is at most d|e|+floor(d/2). The new error law, challenge modulus, count and hardness must be priced. In particular this does not reproduce the discrete Gaussian in the printed LFE. If the two-stage sampler is combined with a low-digit native row, first make independent native rows with the lift, then apply the trial transformation at modulus q; neither the challenge count nor the retained auxiliary inputs become free.

## A structured common NO simulator

Here is one explicit, restricted application. Take the statement Lw=c over F₂ where L has n rows; columns need not be independent. Choose d=2 and the secret split just described with the same n. Issue public R=Lᵀa and tag τ=b+⟨a,c⟩. Also issue bona fide GSW encryptions, under that very s, of the coordinates of a or any fixed Boolean gate values of a. One may additionally issue the M accepted two-stage native header/body tuples with coefficients and tests depending on the public prefix. GSW blocks use independent source rows and the feedback pool uses its own 2T rows, all in one fixed modulus-m LWE challenge with the same underlying t. First lift the challenge, then add GSW gadgets or run the row programming as appropriate. The reducer knows a,R,τ, and every native gadget value; it never sees t. On YES a valid w decrypts by τ+⟨R,w⟩. The auxiliary GSW values are exposed only as ciphertexts and can be processed jointly by an arbitrary observer.

On NO there exists u with Lᵀu=0 and ⟨u,c⟩=1; this follows from the finite-field orthogonal complement criterion for c outside the column span. Translation a ↦ a+u shows (R,τ) has exactly the same distribution for either b, with τ uniformly random given R. After switching the challenge, all shifted GSW blocks are uniform conditional on the public prefix and a. The retained ideal rows have independent uniform bodies and native uniform headers as above. A common structured simulator samples a fresh a, publishes Lᵀa and an independent fair τ, samples uniform GSW blocks and the ideal retained rows. If the adaptive functions use R,τ or previous transcript, run them on these simulated values. The two independent challenge games for b=0 and b=1 lose their individual distinguishing bounds divided by p, since a fixed dummy handles pool exhaustion with probability 1−p. Decryption here uses the affine syndrome; the GSWs establish joint privacy of an additional encrypted evaluation interface, not greater circuit expressiveness. A short public exact span compiler for every NAND instance would itself decide satisfiability. No such compiler, general structured NO endpoint, or cap-certified role is supplied.

## How much terminal phase is determined by the known digits?

Fix a coefficient vector F independent of t conditional on a and all side information used here. Put g=gcd(m,F_1,...,F_n). The map t ↦ ⟨t,F⟩ mod m is a homomorphism whose image is precisely the multiples of g (Bezout). All fibers have equal size. The conditional phase ⟨s,F⟩ mod q is therefore uniform on the coset ⟨a,F⟩+dg(Z/q); its optimum exact prediction probability is g/m. For even q, if m/g is even, translation by q/2 leaves that coset invariant and an antipodal message code is completely masked. If m/g is odd then dg is even and the two cosets reduce modulo dg to centers separated by dg/2. After removing the known offset, nearest-center decoding succeeds with centered error strictly less than dg/4. When m is a power of two the odd case requires m=g. These statements apply only to the stipulated t-independent conditioning; extra secret-dependent responses need their own full-view calculation. Multiplying an entire lifted response by m to force divisibility also multiplies its fresh uniform dither, leaving a uniform residue over the subgroup of order d; for even d this masks an antipodal bit.

Independent enumerations checked the two-stage real and ideal laws, all q=2,...,8 self-shift Boolean tables, directional GSW on small cubes, and nine digit factorizations d,m in {2,3,4} for every scalar secret, gadget column and a weighted error law. Neither this algebra nor these finite tests certify quantum LWE hardness at any proposed concrete parameters. For the original circuit application the coefficient span and encrypted CRS-dependent master remain unresolved. The large-modulus raw NO key must admit a structured simulation compatible with evaluating every guard. Replacing it by the independent uniform shifted-key ideal leaves a fair bit at a fixed probe.

For multiple public coefficient rows with m even, fix their matrix A independently of t and grant the observer a. Without error the jointly normalized vector is uniform on im(A)+b(m/2)v in (Z/m)^r. Privacy is exactly membership of the public half-shift in im(A); independent additive noise preserves equality in the membership case but can also erase the difference of disjoint cosets. A uniform efficient compiler making membership hold on every NO and fail on every YES would decide SAT by public modular linear algebra. At a single parameter size this is a finite decision algorithm, not an asymptotic impossibility. A witness-only short annihilating vector with bounded accumulated error, versus only long noisy relations on NO, is outside that criterion and still requires a full fixed-challenge reduction.
