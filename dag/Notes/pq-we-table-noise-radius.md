This is a correctness-of-design audit of our Bernoulli ciphertext, not a claim about the hardness theorem in [Fenzi and Weissenberg](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2634). The matrix in that theorem was designed to have a primal weight gap. Its binary row words also have structure, which matters for our proposed use of them as noise masks.

**Block code.** Fix a single evaluation table and a single sample position s in the paper's binary Construction A.4. There are Q=tau^a label columns indexed by lambda in K^a, with K=GF(2^e), tau=2^e. For each row of H in Construction 4.5, its entries on these columns are binary linear combinations of the field coordinates of monomials lambda^eta with every component of eta between 0 and 4(d-1). Indeed interpolation in s, extracting coefficients, evaluation in the extension, moment maps, field multiplication by fixed coefficients and taking Frobenius powers are binary linear on the table measurements. The outer equations only combine such measurements. The additional square-free feature checks of our previous note have the same property. Selecting a row basis preserves this containment.

Put l=ae and r=a ceil(log2(4d)). In binary coordinates of the label, each coordinate of lambda_i^j has Boolean degree at most popcount(j): Frobenius powers are binary linear and field multiplication is bilinear. Thus every clean header block belongs to the binary Reed-Muller code RM(min(r,l),l). The degree bound is conservative. For r<l put Delta=Q/2^r. Every such block can be uniquely decoded in polynomial time below Delta/2 errors. For completeness, after subtracting higher-degree terms, a j-fold Boolean difference on the coordinates of a degree-j monomial gives its coefficient. The 2^(l-j) disjoint coordinate faces yield votes for it, with at most one affected vote per erroneous position. Descend by degree to obtain the bounded-distance decoder; the number of monomials and the length of each pass are polynomial in Q.

**Noise budget.** Let D=sigma tau denote the number of blocks and p<=1/(4D) the bit noise rate of the candidate. If 2^r<=D/2, the expected errors per block pQ are <=Delta/8. The binomial tail at Delta/2 is at most (exp(1)/4)^(Delta/2), by the binomial Chernoff bound. Thus except with probability D (exp(1)/4)^(Delta/2), all blocks decode. On a consistent target take any public solution v0, without a weight requirement. Recovering the clean header H^T t suffices to compute its inner product with v0 and remove the pad. For the quotient format one can equivalently lift the syndrome of E to any header in its coset and decode the row word there: the discrepancy is the original E. This argument concerns one repetition and uses no NP witness. Inconsistent targets assigned a dummy law are excluded.

**Does this apply at the proposed gap?** For every fixed a the source parameters give d < 2(8 sigma Gamma)^(1/a), tau>=16 a d c with c>=N, and sigma polynomial in N. Consequently 2^r < (8d)^a <= C_a sigma Gamma for a fixed constant C_a, and Delta >= (tau/(8d))^a >= (2aN)^a. For Gamma=8(lambda+1), on growing lengths with N>=lambda one has tau/Gamma tending to infinity because d grows with Gamma. Thus 2^r<=D/2 eventually, and the failure bound is negligible for polynomial N in lambda. In particular even choosing the compression parameter a=1 suffices to exhibit this failure; optimizing to another *fixed* a does not remove it in the stated regime. If the consistency test alone excluded all NO targets on these lengths, it would already decide the compiled language on them. The calculation does not require assuming which specific false formulas have consistent linear systems.

The candidate's honest polynomial resource and witness correctness bounds survive, but the proposed search hardness on the actual projected code cannot hold in this regime: the Bernoulli error is recoverable from its syndrome with overwhelming probability. Distance gap alone is compatible with this failure. A redesign must address local decodability of the masking row space. Merely increasing the noise within the displayed bounded-distance radius, or compressing by the same row space, preserves the obstruction. Exceeding the radius alone supplies no security proof.
