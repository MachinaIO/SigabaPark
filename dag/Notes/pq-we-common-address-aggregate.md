## Independent differences and the ideal interface

Let W be the address set and let P₁,…,P_m be the public checks on the same address. In the registered relation an address can be the original 128 bits, using deterministic gate evaluation, or the original inputs together with 4096 claimed bits, using local checks plus final output. A false statement has no common passing address. In the independent-table construction choose every F_i(w) uniformly, release F₀, β+F_m, and F_i−F_(i−1) only at w where P_i holds. Work over bits initially. Changing variables from (F₀,…,F_m) to (F₀,D₁,…,D_m), D_i=F_i−F_(i−1), is invertible. Subtract the public F₀ from the endpoint. Thus the core records are independent restricted differences D_i and one endpoint β+ΣD_i. Honest cancellation uses a single common w.

On NO, translate D_i(w) by t_i(w)=[all preceding checks pass and P_i fails]. A passing restriction does not change and exactly one t_i is one at each w. Translating β and all D_i therefore preserves the entire printed frame. In the original coordinates these are the adjacent differences of δ_i(w)=1−prod_{j≤i}P_j(w). The local identity is `first_failed_shift`. Exhaustive enumeration covered all 64 systems of three predicates on two addresses, with all 256 original four-level mask tapes and both bits in each system. Efficiently evaluating a translation on a given w does not imply that translating short seeds for a proposed real representation preserves their law.

## A finite real encoding with no cryptographic oracle

Choose public finite linear mask spaces V_i over a field, an ambient coefficient space E and fixed linear maps to E representing each entire function D_i. Use an injective canonical representation of the sum as a function on the chosen feature domain, such as sorted multilinear Boolean-cube coefficients with duplicates added. Let R_i represent evaluation of D_i on the allowed domain of P_i, or any linear record that exactly suffices there. Draw independent uniform coefficient vectors in V_i. Issue R_i D_i for each i and a single ambient vector for H=β·1+ΣD_i. No source master or unrestricted difference need be issued. The decryptor at a passing w subtracts all locally allowed evaluations from H(w). Including public shape data, all input/output encodings may be deterministic functions of this packet and the public statement.

Write K=⊕V_i, R for the stack of all restrictions and A for coefficient aggregation, and e for the constant-one coordinate in E. Exact NO secrecy of these keys is equivalent to **e ∈ A(ker R)**. Indeed the two distributions are uniform on translates of the image of k↦(Rk,Ak); if they intersect, those affine spaces are equal, and otherwise they are disjoint. A single u with Ru=0, Au=e couples every input seed to its translate. This is the formal `view_coset` equivalence plus uniformity on a finite group. Correctness precludes the membership at a passing address. All statements hold for arbitrary postprocessing of the classical records, including a quantum observer. For a general field the translation sign is chosen according to the direction of the game hop. No new hardness premise is required for this conditional, concrete construction.

For a bag B_i of at most b Boolean coordinates containing the guard, sample all local coefficients, issue the allowed local truth-table cells, and accumulate the canonical global ANF coefficients. The endpoint need only index monomials occurring in some bag and the constant. This costs at most 2m·2^b field elements in total before framing. Transforming one full truth table into multilinear coefficients uses b·2^(b−1) additions/subtractions by coordinate differences. All shared canonical monomial indices must really be merged. Extra duplicate rows may be removed from the printed restrictions when their equality is structural. When bag coordinates are public functions of a larger address, an identity on the full feature cube is sufficient; emitting keys for unrealizable feature cells must still be counted. A syntactic factorization with β as a separate literal does not implement the masked endpoint representation.

The class of NO statements on which the small scheme is private must be proved, or an adequate fallback must cover other inputs. For clear small matrices the criterion is itself a polynomial-time consistency test. A separable dual functional is a concrete distinguisher outside it. This positive bounded format thus cannot secretly hide the result of an unknown arbitrary satisfiability problem merely by calling a small kernel a PRF.

## Three checks, an actual distinguishing observation, and the repair

Let P₁ say x=0, P₂ say y=0 and P₃ say x OR y=1. Each pair is satisfiable and the triple has no solution. Originally take V₁=span(1,x), V₂=span(1,y), V₃=span(1,x,y,xy), all over GF(2). Issue D₁(0), D₂(0), the three allowed values of D₃ and the four canonical coefficients of H. There are nine public bits. In the XOR of H(0,1), H(1,0), H(1,1), each forbidden unary value is used twice, so the remaining contributions besides β are all among the five released values. This recovers β with certainty even if direct key inspection is replaced by just those three endpoint evaluations. The formal `three_address_leak` checks the identity for every key and bit.

Add exactly one independent coefficient on xy to D₂. Its release on y=0 is unchanged. The zero-restriction shifts are x, (1+x)y, (1+x)(1+y), which sum to one at every address. The cross term xy was missing from the second mask. The permitted records still occupy nine bits. In concrete coordinates sample a₀,aₓ,b₀,bᵧ,bₓᵧ,c₀,cₓ,cᵧ,cₓᵧ as nine fair bits and emit

- a₀, b₀ and D₃(0,1), D₃(1,0), D₃(1,1);
- the four coefficients β+a₀+b₀+c₀, aₓ+cₓ, bᵧ+cᵧ, bₓᵧ+cₓᵧ.

This is the complete packet under the fixed format. There are no hidden-query or authorized-party restrictions on reading it. The Lean lemma `three_address_repair` gives the local shifts. The unextended frame has two disjoint 256-atom distributions. The repair gives exactly the same 512 equally likely complete frames for both bits. For a YES control replace P₃ by (NOT x) OR y, still issuing its three allowed evaluations. A witness at (0,0) recovers the bit in both schemes.

A registered realization computes x,y by two interleaved 2037-gate NAND chains on the last 126 input bits followed by two XORs with the first two independent input bits. In particular for every fixed tail, varying the first two bits realizes all four (x,y). Both controls use 4091 gates before live identity padding, and all 4096 gate outputs are live after padding. The raw statement encodings take 36,277,198 and 36,277,228 bits respectively, using 4a+2b+3 bits per NAND (a,b). The three printed guard records apply to the functionally evaluated features, not to 4096 independent claimed trace bits. The replay checks both controls and all four first-bit assignments on 32 sampled tails, besides the exhaustive nine-bit packet test. The XOR chart proves the feature-surjectivity statement for every tail. The nine-bit ledger excludes the source statement, recognition, feature evaluation, registered framing and machines. This subsection is a known syntactic family; its satisfiability is also directly decidable.

## An overlapping-cover construction over a small field

Write v_i=1−P_i for the Boolean violation indicator, interpreted in F₃, and S=Σv_i. For a family of NO instances where every address has a number of violations nonzero modulo three, add one direction v_i S to the i-th mask space. Each such direction is zero on its own passing checks. Their aggregate equals S², identically one on those instances. Thus the same compact scheme gives an exact joint coupling, without a trusted certificate or a single fixed check that must fail. This is `residue_cover`. In the simple version sample one trit r_i per guard, issue no data for its identically zero restriction, and print canonical coefficients of β+Σr_i v_i S. A passing witness directly evaluates the endpoint. A uniformly sampled trit is a mathematical sampler; a finite fair-bit implementation needs bounded sampling with statistical error or a charged alternative. The supplied three-check ternary replay used all 27 trit tuples and got identical distributions on NO. On the YES control they are disjoint.

With three-bit guards, a single product v_i v_j uses at most six bits: expand and multilinearize on Boolean inputs, then merge all coefficients in the common format. There are at most 64·m² candidate monomial occurrences before deduplication, plus the constant. Large instances still need concrete storage and preprocessing clocks, including sorting and the guard front end. Printing the factorized formula with β exposed would break security. More generally any public low-degree polynomial Q(s) with sQ(s)=1 on the possible nonzero NO weights yields shifts v_i Q(S); representing their sum canonically remains a real cost. For at most t simultaneous violations one can also use the sum of all nonempty products of up to t distinct binary violation indicators, assigning each product to an index it contains. At every NO address this evaluates to one in GF(2) by inclusion–exclusion.

Chinese remaindering alone does not remove the common-address issue. Even with product of primes greater than the maximum count, a different prime can be nonzero at each address. For example take five violations (1,1,1,x,x). Counts are three or five and the instance is always false. If independent mod-3 and mod-5 schemes expose separately necessary message shares through masks v_i S^(p−2), the mod-3 endpoint is unmasked at x=0, the mod-5 endpoint at x=1. All shares can be collected across these two addresses. A combined Boolean function recognizing whether *some* residue is nonzero has a small arithmetic circuit in several moduli. On a cube of m independent violation bits, the unique multilinear polynomial for OR is 1−prod_i(1−v_i), with degree m and 2^m−1 nonzero monomials. This is a lower bound on this universal clear canonical representation on the independent cube. It gives no such lower bound for a specific circuit with dependent violations, or for every cryptographic representation.

## What linear closure and current cryptographic interfaces guarantee

If a statement-independent linear space of functions on n Boolean bits contains one and is stable under multiplication by both value indicators of *every* input bit, it contains every single-address delta by successive multiplication. This is the Lean `literal_isolation` lemma. The disjoint deltas form the standard basis for all functions, so dimension is at least 2^128 for the original 128-bit domain, or 2^4224 if the condition applies to all extended inputs. Requiring only finite local kernel shifts for a particular family avoids the premise. This observation neither prohibits nonlinear compact keys nor says a fixed random function must be written as its full table.

Giving each independent difference a GGM tree ordered with its own local gate bits first does yield small honest restricted keys: expose the roots of the authorized depth-b subtrees and evaluate the remaining suffix in their native order. What is missing is one offline object for the sum at every address with the constant shift hidden. Publishing the unrestricted component seeds along with an explicit β, or enough keys to reconstruct every component at one address, leaks the bit directly. Single-key restricted PRF pseudorandomness does not simulate all restricted keys together with their aggregate.

[Banerjee et al., TCC 2015, Sections 1 and 4](https://eprint.iacr.org/2015/180.pdf) give lattice prefix constraints with key homomorphism. A single common prefix order is insufficient to make arbitrary gate-local bits initial for every gate, and independent orders do not automatically yield one evaluable sum key. [Peikert and Shiehian, PKC 2018, Construction 1 and Lemma 1](https://iacr.org/archive/pkc2018/10770193/10770193.pdf) hide one shifted key. Their unshifted evaluation in equation (17) is linear in the master s. This invites publishing S=Σs_i under shared public parameters to aggregate base evaluations, but the literal independent one-key simulation fails a short-residual test. In equation (18) the first fixed-one gadget block satisfies a_(i,0)=s_i(A₀+G)+e_(i,0). An observer computes Σa_(i,0)−S(A₀+G). Choose one coordinate. If each real coordinate error is bounded by B except with joint probability δ, the test for a centered magnitude at most mB passes with probability at least 1−δ. Replacing even one of those component bodies by independent uniform data conditional on S and the others yields probability at most min(1,(2mB+1)/q). This explicitly distinguishes the naive independent ideal, not the two bit-conditioned real worlds. A correlated ideal preserving the summed residual and a simulator for its relation to all shift ciphertexts are required. Approximation and rounding errors for honest use must also be bounded.

[Lewko and Waters, Eurocrypt 2011](https://iacr.org/archive/eurocrypt2011/66320571/66320571.pdf) bind attributes to a global user identifier through authority-issued keys. Setting the identifier to w makes the desired consistency apparent. It does not provide an offline public packet able to issue exactly the allowed key for each of exponentially many w without an authority secret. The prior modular-frontier note analyzes one linear displacement; here all constraining records must remain fixed under one simultaneous translation. The outstanding unrestricted task is a genuinely succinct aggregate with that joint distribution, or a fixed circuit-independent falsifiable reduction for its actual keys; we do not assume security of a predicate-dependent equivalence experiment.
