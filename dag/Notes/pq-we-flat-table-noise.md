For the binary fixed-gap matrix H, use D blocks of size Q=2^l, l=ae. Recall the field label coordinates in GF(2^e)^a. Set r=a floor(log2(4d-3)) and assume r<l as in the previous parameter regime. Clean blocks belong to C=RM(r,l): the maximum binary popcount among exponents from zero through 4d-4 is floor(log2(4d-3)). In addition the rank of H restricted to any one block is at most e(4d-3)^a <= L 2^r with L=2^a e: every row is assembled from binary coordinates of the monomials with individual exponents between zero and 4(d-1). This includes the square-free homogeneous checks if used. We use this second, smaller dimension bound independently of the dimension of C. The fixed frontend has weight gap D versus Gamma D and at most one selected label per block for each honest witness. [Fenzi-Weissenberg, Constructions 4.5 and A.4](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/2634).

**Replacement distribution.** Start E at zero. For 2^r independent trials, with probability 1/4 select a uniform block and add the indicator of a uniform affine subspace of codimension r in that block. Each such indicator is a product of r affine linear Boolean factors, hence E belongs blockwise to C. Use fresh E per repetition in the previous syndrome ciphertext. In the full header, a bounded-distance decoder for C now encounters a codeword even in every modified block; returning that word does not separate its clean and noise parts. The quotient representation uses the same distribution. Sampling via a uniform full-rank r-row binary matrix and a uniform RHS, with Gaussian elimination or rejection, has polynomial expected cost since 2^r<=Q. Cap rejections and charge their joint abort probability if a strict time bound is needed.

**Correctness.** If a witness uses h<=D blocks, the probability that one trial changes its parity is (1/4)(h/D)2^(-r). Its final bias is (1-h/(2D 2^r))^(2^r)>=1/2. Hence the same 8(lambda+1) repetitions give error below 2^(-lambda).

**NO-side estimate.** For a putative representation Hx=y write x_i for its i-th block and let t_i be its absolute Hamming distance to C-perp=RM(l-r-1,l). Define q_i as the probability that a uniform codimension-r flat has odd intersection with x_i. This is the canonical flat rejection test at degree l-r-1. [Bhattacharyya et al., Theorem 1](https://people.csail.mit.edu/madhu/papers/2009/bkssz-full.pdf) gives q_i >= c min(1,t_i/2^r) for an absolute c>0, since the relative distance is t_i/Q and the flat has Q/2^r points.

Subtracting a local dual word preserves Hx. Alternatively the same local syndrome can always be represented with at most rank(H_i)<=L 2^r positions by a column basis. Taking the better replacement in every block produces a representation of weight at most sum_i min(t_i,L 2^r). For a NO target this sum exceeds Gamma D. Since min(t,L 2^r)<=L min(t,2^r), one gets sum_i q_i > c Gamma D/(L 2^r). Independence of the *trials*, without assuming independent blocks, gives

    |E[(-1)^(x.E)]| = (1 - sum_i q_i/(2D))^(2^r) <= exp(-c Gamma/(2L)).

Thus every message-sensitive affine parity of independent repeated ciphertexts has at most this bias; averaging over the uniform secret removes frequencies not representing the target. The decision-tree argument from the frontend gives gap at most 2^(j+1) exp(-c Gamma/(2L)) for j adaptive classical affine observations. Taking Gamma=(N+lambda+2)^2, fixed a, keeps the frontend polynomial and L logarithmic in N+lambda. The bound then remains negligible even for j=lambda. This result covers a restricted class of tests. It is not a computational assumption on the particular formula.

**Wire format and outstanding security.** The entire uncompressed header now lies in C^D. An information set for C has k_C=sum_(h=0)^r binom(l,h) bits. In these coordinates row(H) still has its original rank, so quotienting the header and tag as before gives D k_C-rank(H)+1 bits per repetition. Coordinate changes and both directions of the previous simulation are linear and efficient on this ambient space. The message pad still depends on a specified target parity of E. In particular the clean subcode on a block can be much smaller than C; nothing here proves that the random flats are hidden modulo that subcode, either locally or jointly. Full-view post-quantum hiding from a fixed falsifiable assumption remains open.
