A fresh-target gadget bank compresses one layer of products of independent random field labels for N binary variables. A single fixed Decomposed-LWE decision instance suffices to hide its missing mixed degree from the full specified local transcript, including the source label records. A second local game below admits a public opening check under the same decision challenge. Neither experiment contains the final garbled-table unlock query of the proposed witness encryption.

### What the original hybrid needs

In [Kluczniak, ePrint 2020/1502](https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/1502), Construction 1 issues, per tag, nℓ single-secret and n²ℓ same-secret quadratic encryptions, and, per compatible pair, n²ℓ mixed encryptions under the pair-specific concatenated secret. Construction 4 introduces η times alphabet-size tags, one tagged input encryption per variable/value, up to alphabet-size to the fan-in garbled entries per gate and a terminal key cycle. Appendix B separates levels and Remark 6 permits keeping a single orientation of symmetric mixed keys. Formula conversion with 128 witness bits and 4,096 trace bits has N=4,224. Joining two syntactic operand supports with all four tag-value combinations requires every variable pair at the lowest common ancestor of representatives, giving 4·binom(N,2)=35,675,904 unordered cross-variable tag pairs; at 121 emitted bits each the total is 4,316,784,384. Honest wires may be shared by gate index. This count applies to the explicit trace formula and this support interface.

Definition 12 compares designated ideal-cipher queries on keys and ciphertexts from a challenge graph under two experiments. The ideal version logs provenance and substitutes fresh randomness on conflicts. Theorem 3 changes the random unlock distribution by first aborting on a query that produces the unlock key and, on a false instance, using the ideal logger to rule out consistent discovery. It then substitutes F(sk) for the random terminal and uses CycleTest for honest decryption. Decision GLWE does not provide that query comparison. Our replacement games sample explicit distributional challenges and check their winning predicates in polynomial time on any statement. They cover just the two local interfaces defined below.

### Exact degree dictionary

Take N≥2, M=3N and the two exponents i and M−i for variable i=1,…,N. A mixed product is forbidden exactly when its degree is M. Same-polarity pairs, including squares, give every integer in [2,2N] and [4N,6N−2]. Mixed pairs give [2N+1,4N−1] except M, by taking all nonzero differences i−j. Thus legal products need 6N−4 degrees; including unary exponents adds only degree 1 for D=6N−3. The bank grants the complete linear span of its entries, regardless of tag provenance. The omitted monomial has coefficient zero in every such formal linear combination. Distinct univariate polynomials of degree at most 6N−2 remain distinct as functions on the nonzero field elements for p−1>6N−2. Evaluating at a single secret point supplies no unconditional hardness by itself. Giving out the source point, or even decrypting the degree-1 row, would immediately reveal the omitted value. Arbitrary same-grade multiplication by a public copy of the indeterminate invalidates formal linear omission.

The index intentionally forgets the pair-specific target. At fixed nonzero difference the ordered (left,right) pairs form a matching, so a Cartesian-product router containing two edges also includes unrequested crossed endpoints. We switch to one independent destination, using the collision only for a single layer. For N≥5 the consistent triple (+3,−2,+4) and conflicting (+1,−1,+5) both have exponent M+5 and the same signs and length. Keeping scalar degree and polarity counts through three factors already loses provenance. Gate-local checks which retain authenticated history require their own analysis.

### Source, issuance and local reduction

Fix primes p>6N and q>p, a source point τ uniform in Fp*, and destination s uniform in Fq^k, independently. T_d denotes the integer representative of τ^d mod p. The source row for a label m of degree u is (a,c=m−aτ^u) over Fp with independent uniform a. Each distinct source label is independent uniform in Fp, and repeated occurrences reuse the same record. Conditional on τ and all a, the vector of c is uniform. Revealing m, independently encoding the same m twice, or replacing m by a known Boolean phase can leak T_u, so those interfaces are outside the game.

Use [ePrint 2025/339, Definition 4.1 and Theorem 4.2](https://eprint.iacr.org/2025/339) (the reference is identified by its ePrint number). With ℓ=ceil(log₂ q), concatenate t² blocks of kℓ columns, each H_ij=A_i B_j+δ_ij G. The real body Y_ij=sᵀH_ij+e_ij, the alternative is a uniform independent body for all columns conditional on A,B. Assign each degree d a distinct (i,j,h) in a fixed lexicographic order where 0≤h<k and t²k≥D. For each 0≤l<ℓ select column hℓ+l and publish its full H and Z=Y+2^l T_d mod q. Honest issuance samples the real instance or generates the selected columns by the same rule. The receiver only needs H,Z and the source rows. A simulator knows τ, all input labels and their public encodings. It obtains H,Y from the fixed decision challenge, adds the known offsets, and invokes the recipient. Conditional on A,B,τ,a,c and any independently generated coins, the ideal selected bodies are jointly independent uniform by coordinatewise translation. This remains true if any fixed efficiently sampled auxiliary data using τ and source labels, but independent of the destination challenge given A,B, is included, although that data can independently compromise the eventual guessing bound.

Concretely the game asks for the canonical T_M after giving the specified source rows, matrices and switched bank, and any public processing thereof. The decision reduction samples τ so can check the answer efficiently. In the ideal view, all source rows and selected bodies together are independent of τ. Hence success is at most gcd(M,p−1)/(p−1)+Adv_DLWE; this is an exact ideal baseline because the power map on Fp* has uniform image of that size's reciprocal. Checking uses no satisfiability oracle. Input is classical; any QPT recipient can be run on this view under the quantum version of the fixed decision assumption. The finite-parameter cost, quantum hardness, and full distribution pushforward are not formalized by the algebraic Lean statements. The cited Succinct-LWE-to-Decomposed-LWE distributional reduction requires prime q, a superpolynomial flooding ratio and its other dimension/width hypotheses. A fixed numerical 2^128 ratio alone is not an asymptotic theorem for every parameter family. Both named assumptions have separate DAG entries and do not belong to the unchanged WE registry.

### Switching full random labels across different moduli

For legal u,v let rows be (a,c),(b,d). Over Fp the product expands with coefficients h₀,…,h₃=(cd,ad,cb,ab), taken modulo p, at indices 0,u,v,u+v. Let b_j be the nearest integer to q h_j/p (canonical h_j). Add b₀ to the output body. For each j=1,2,3 add all (H,Z) columns of its degree whose bit l in b_j is set, obtaining U,V. Then V−sᵀU equals b₀+Σ b_j T_degree_j plus an error of magnitude at most 3ℓB whenever all selected sampled noise coefficients have magnitude ≤B. This bound allows repeated use of one column; it needs no independence across occurrences. Since the ideal rational scaled phase differs by a multiple of q from (q/p)(mn mod p), its circular error is at most (1+3(p−1))/2 + 3ℓB. If this is <q/(2p), round p(V−sᵀU)/q to the nearest integer modulo p to obtain exactly mn. The same argument covers squaring a reused label and any two legal records; the complete good-noise event for the bank suffices simultaneously for arbitrarily many public recombinations of this prescribed form. Multiplication here returns the product of two full random labels, not the original gate-table decryption or public unlocking operation.

An acyclic sequence of switching-key banks into independent targets s₁,…,s_L can itself be replaced in reverse order. For instance, for bank 2 give binary gadget encryptions of the entries s₁,i and s₁,i s₁,j under fresh s₂. To replace bank 2, the reduction samples τ,s₁ and therefore knows the masks and can simulate bank 1. To replace bank 1 next, bank 2 is already uniform and needs no s₁; sample its independent public matrices. The loss for the static bank transcript is additive over levels, also after any public postprocessing. Repeated scaled, noisy ciphertext multiplication, source/garbled-table correlations and a terminal object that depends on the last challenge secret still need separate proofs. Dense next-layer basis size is k+k(k+1)/2 before gadget columns; a new common target at each layer alone does not prevent mixing labels from different assignments.

### A public opening test tied to the same fixed challenge

To tolerate complete disclosure of permitted scalar products in a separate local test, replace scalar powers by independent uniform μ_d for all nonzero offsets d=−(N−1),…,N−1, reserving the unissued diagonal value μ_0. Work in K=F_(q^k) with a fixed public Fq-basis and let μ_0 encode the k-vector challenge secret s. For binary supports x,y let c_d=Σ_(i−j=d) x_i y_j and b=Σ_(d≠0) c_d μ_d. Because q>N, c₀ is zero iff no variable is selected on both sides. A consistent opening is t=b. An honest holder of the hidden value can form t=b+c₀μ₀ for arbitrary supports.

Reserve v further, disjoint decision-challenge columns (H_j,Y_j), with t²kℓ≥Dℓ+v if sharing the switching bank. Publish these columns without offset, and publish all off-diagonal μ_d. The public verifier checks t=b when c₀=0. Otherwise unpack (t−b)/c₀ as s' and accept iff all centered residuals Y_j−s'ᵀH_j have magnitude ≤B. For the genuine t on inconsistent supports, the real verifier accepts whenever all reserved errors meet the cutoff. In the ideal decision case, even after conditioning on all H, switching-body masks and all independent off-diagonal disclosures, the v reserved Y_j are uniform. A union bound over q^k secrets gives Pr[any s' accepted] ≤ min(1,q^k((2B+1)/q)^v), for 2B+1≤q. This is simultaneous over supports and arbitrary adaptive attempts. Therefore making *one* accepted inconsistent opening in the real game has success at most that quantity plus one fixed DLWE advantage. The reduction checks the public predicate itself. All consistent-query answers are public functions and can be supplied at will. The same disjoint columns can be included in the missing-degree game above, as their ideal bodies reveal nothing about τ. Optional verification-output access for a guessed candidate is public computation in either game.

This is a falsifiable, publicly checked local replacement for one form of tag inconsistency. The supports themselves are public and a dishonest evaluator may declare different disjoint supports unless they are bound to the actual hidden gate labels. Exposing a tag vector containing μ₀ would likewise break the game. Off-diagonal entries of the Toeplitz form μ_(i−j) do not reveal μ₀, unlike the scalar-power basis after destination decryption. Conditioned on off-diagonal entries, its determinant is a monic degree-N polynomial in μ₀, giving rank N with probability ≥1−N/|K|; a generic explicit bilinear realization therefore has dimension at least N. Finite field extension multiplication, a basis/irreducible polynomial, random element generation, and all such data must be charged in an implementation. As coefficients c_d lie in the prime subfield, this particular verifier needs only vector additions and scalar operations in Fq using the chosen basis. There is no identification of the Toeplitz witness statistic with the switched scalar product, or with the terminal cycle. Merely placing the two local views in a joint root challenge does not provide that binding.

### Concrete ledger and remaining interface

Illustration, without a hardness estimate: p=2^255−19, q=2^521−1 (prime), ℓ=521, k=256, m=266752, σ=2048, σ̃=2^139, B=2^149. The widths meet the explicit arithmetic parameter guards in the DAG definition. The rounding radius is <q/(2p) and the total probability of a noise exception in the used coordinates is at most (Dℓ+v)·Pr_{e←D_σ̃}[|e|>B]. The local opening tester may use v=512; its ideal probability bound is <2^(−55000), using q>2^520 and 2B+1<2^151. The power-guessing baseline at N=4224 is 12/(p−1).

For a public, dense fixed-width encoding, the scalar Z bodies occupy Dℓ² bits, the full projected H,Z bank (k+1)Dℓ² bits, and each distinct source record 2·255 bits. The optional v residual checks add v(k+1)ℓ and explicit off-diagonal disclosures add 2(N−1)kℓ bits. For N=8, D=45 versus 112 original cross-tag pairs; the full single switching bank is 3,139,215,165 bits before source records, optional checks or program costs. For N=64 the corresponding figures are 381 degrees, 8064 pairs, and 26,578,688,397 bits. At N=4224, D=25341, t=10 suffices with the checks; scalar bodies alone need 6,878,586,381 bits. The full H,Z bank requires 1,767,796,699,917 bits, plus 4,308,480 bits for 8448 distinct source rows. Thus the dictionary compression reduces degree count, while literal emission already exceeds the 2^32 target on the full trace formula. The goal registry separately sets 2^100 as a maximum. Publicly regenerating H has no effect on the body lower bound for this layout.

Honest generation of the selected H by direct dot products uses k m multiplications and k(m−1) additions in Fq per column, plus gadget-diagonal updates. Each body then uses k field products, k−1 additions, noise and mask arithmetic. Reusing a factored matrix may lower generation work while retaining all published materials or changing the transmitted headers. Legal evaluation adds at most 3ℓ columns of k+1 field elements, besides four coefficient products in Fp, four q/p scalings and secret decryption if authorized. These are field-operation counts; they omit tape traversal, sampling, conversions, framing and machine descriptions. Exact Gaussian samplers in particular require a bounded statistical replacement for a fixed-clock implementation. A small-instance fallback can enumerate just the compatible pairs reached on the fixed typed interface and keep the smaller public dictionary; if degree merging is unsafe, retain distinct destination keys and the original per-pair count. This bounds an implementation choice by its dense local baseline, without claiming WE security for that baseline. The previously proved brute-force WE has a documented explicit cost above the registered cap.

The next obligation is a ciphertext and gate-authentication mechanism under which the polynomial or Toeplitz statistic is forced by actual evaluation and the terminal challenge is simulable while both hidden choices and all encoded garbled rows are in view. A full false-statement hybrid also needs coherent-oracle treatment if an oracle is ever granted to a quantum adversary, actual security parameters, a compatible registry premise, explicit machines and a complete cost bound. None of these obligations follows from a one-layer key switch or a local public opening test.

### Two specific alternatives

For a fixed (two-positive, one-negative) triple, store both r=i+k−j and d=i²+k²−j² in an odd prime field of characteristic >N. Then r²−d=2(i−j)(k−j), which vanishes exactly for a conflict. Equal positive tags are allowed. This repairs the three-tag provenance statistic on its stated family. It gives neither an authenticated encoding of the two moments nor an unbounded-depth gate rule.

The two local reduction proofs also work with a different honest generator that samples the H columns independently uniform in Fq^k. In this alternative take a single ordinary decision-LWE challenge with k coordinates, Dℓ+v samples, uniform secret and the same error; assign disjoint samples to the switch columns and the public checker. Real and ideal simulation for this modified generator is exact for the same local games. It removes A,B sampling and matrix-matrix multiplication, without claiming that the two header distributions coincide. The directly generated bank needs k independent uniform residues per column and k modular products for its body. Ordinary decision LWE belongs to the WE registry. This variant still needs actual parameters of adequate strength, the terminal and a complete WE reduction. Neither local game by itself requires the additional succinct structure obtainable from Decomposed LWE. The factored-challenge route is of interest where later authenticated gate material requires correlations with A,B.
