A public NAND relation can sometimes be compressed without an assumption. We give a complete translation search on sparse Boolean polynomials, a sufficient test for shared dense circuit expressions, and a single-bit pivot that can succeed even if the raw guard vector admits no global syndrome action for that bit. A shorter witness can be computed from an actual satisfying witness. This is a conditional front end for the registered primitive. A backend, worst-case registered clock and general post-quantum security proof remain open.

### Complete sparse translation search

Represent every coordinate of F by its unique square-free ANF over F₂. Define Δ_t F(x)=F(x+t)+F(x). For maximum degree d start with all t, since their differences have degree at most d−1. Within a subspace K_j where every difference has degree at most j, the degree-j homogeneous component of the difference is a linear map in t. Indeed Δ_(u+v)F(x)=Δ_u F(x+v)+Δ_v F(x), and translation of a polynomial of degree at most j preserves its degree-j component. On each basis direction, form all (output, degree-j monomial) rows, take their simultaneous binary kernel and descend. Induction shows that the kernel is exactly the space on which the degree drops again. After j=1 the remaining K consists of all constant differences. Degrees zero and one start directly at this stage. For a monomial indexed by mask m the degree-j contribution is the symmetric difference of m minus each nonempty (|m|−j)-subset of supp(m)∩supp(t). Skipping the restriction to K_j would apply linear algebra to a generally nonlinear map. In one quadratic monomial xy, its degree-zero difference at zero is the product of the two translation bits.

Let L(t)=F(t)+F(0) on K, H=im L, and let P have kernel H (basis of the annihilator as rows). Then PF(x)=0 iff F(x) lies in H. Choose a linear section σ of L on H. Sending x to (x−σF(x),F(x)) identifies the projected zero set with the original zero set times H. The inverse adds σu. The Lean result fiber_split proves the equivalence over additive abelian groups from the translation law. In the finite binary case the count on the original variables grows by exactly 2^rank(L). An incomplete space of certified constant directions gives the same identity for its own image. Recompute after projection: canceling highest guards can expose new directions. Stop on zero image; a constant one in the row span certifies NO, while empty guards accept every remaining assignment. A constant zero can always be discarded.

For actual witness transport record independent input preimages of each round's image basis. Every earlier recorded direction fixes every later guard. The accumulated directions are independent: at each round an old-span direction lies in the current kernel, whereas the selected new span maps injectively to the current image. Choose a coordinate complement to their span by RREF. Project by subtracting recorded rows until all pivot positions vanish and delete the pivot bits. This leaves the final projected syndrome invariant. The child substitutes zero in the deleted positions; the polynomial substitution only drops monomials. Lift a child by inserting zeros and working backward, correcting each round's old syndrome by its stored section. Each step preserves subsequent checks and kills its old syndrome. Orbit representatives are unique: between two satisfying parents in one coset, take the latest round with a nonzero direction component, evaluate that round on their difference and use injectivity there (all earlier components leave this round unchanged). Thus witnesses on the reduced variable set are in bijection, even for partially solved children.

### Circuits with dense ANFs

Read the registered gates in order with wire 0 equal to true, treating unavailable references as true. Recognize the exact four-NAND XOR motif and fold constants, complements and equal inputs. Write each wire as an affine parity of raw inputs and shared AND atom outputs. An atom joins two earlier affine words. Traverse the live atoms from the current guard forms, including all transitive inputs. A translation annihilating the direct raw coefficient parity on both inputs of every live atom preserves all those atoms, by induction. The shift of each guard is precisely its direct raw parity. The Lean xag_shift theorem proves this for the topologically ordered selected atom list (indices must be remapped when actually deleting unused cells; leaving their placeholders is also valid). Hash-consing and four-gate recognition are executable checks in the replay. Their general extraction from registered raw NAND has no Lean proof here. A scan bounded by 4096 raw gates and a bounded live-word elimination suffice to propose this certificate. Vanishing input parities are a sufficient condition and can miss semantically equal product expressions or jointly canceling changes.

The 1,939-gate suffix-OR fixture connects forty inputs to suffixes of later inputs, mixes the forty constraints by 320 reversible XOR row operations, joins their negations, and pads to 4,096 gates. Its 127-input OR has 2^127−1 distinct ANF monomials. Without expanding it, the XAG checker returns forty successive image ranks of one and no remaining constraint: exactly 2^88 witnesses. Independently, fixing the last 88 bits uniquely determines the first 40 in reverse order. A syntactic triangular recognizer works before row mixing; the parity certificate survives the mixing. An arbitrary nonlinear recoding can destroy this sufficient syntax and can destroy all global constant directions. No complete polynomial-time semantic certificate is assumed.

### Pivots on a residual zero set

Partition invertible linear combinations of independent guards into one q and a vector G. For physical coordinate b, write τ for its flip. It suffices that G=0 is invariant under τ and q toggles on this zero set. Then deleting b identifies parent witnesses with the child G(0,y)=0; lift by setting b=q(0,y). The Lean guarded_pivot constructs the equivalence with exactly that projection. One exact polynomial certificate, modulo x_i²=x_i, is ΔG_i=Σ M_ij(x)G_j and Δq+1=Σ a_j(x)G_j. Every residual-zero point maps to a residual-zero point. The involution gives both directions; the second identity toggles the pivot on that set. Lean ideal_certificate proves the ring-valued certificate consequences, with 1−q representing the Boolean toggle over F₂. Constant coefficients specialize to a row-span certificate. The replay tests row span first, then the span enlarged by multiplying every row by one candidate coordinate. The candidate generator is incomplete, but testing a listed identity is exact.

For each of 16 disjoint eight-bit blocks put h=yz+ab, k=uw. The cubic guard vector is (x+uv,h,xh+k). Flipping x changes the syndrome by (1,0,h). All 255 nonzero input directions in one block give nonconstant vector differences (exhaustive); independence of blocks and invertible output mixing preserve triviality of K. Choosing x=0 in the child keeps h and k. The 2,030-gate cubic fixture loses 16 witness bits and 16 guards, leaving 112 bits and 32 guards, with exactly 60^16 witnesses.

The quartic version replaces the pivot with q=x+uv+xhw. Here Δq=1+hw and Δ(xh+k)=h, so the same child and lift work even though the first identity needs a nonconstant residual multiplier. There is no function on the unreduced syndrome implementing τ: with input encoding x,u,v,y,z,a,b,w in successive low-to-high bits, 24 and 152 have the same syndrome (0,1,0), yet 25 and 153 give (1,1,1) and (0,1,1). The example lifts unchanged to 16 blocks and survives reversible output mixing. The 2,126-gate raw quartic fixture yields 16 certified pivots, every one using a variable multiplier, and the same 60^16 count (ten choices for y,z,a,b with yz=ab, three choices for u,w with uw=0, two choices for v, then unique x). A mixed negative variant adds guards 1+u,1+v,uv in the first block. These force u=v=1 and uv=0 simultaneously; mixing preserves their common zero set. The replay checks 256 embedded inputs and 128 accepted full assignments per degree. This extends the affine-syndrome route through a strict semantic example.

### Reproduction and deployment boundary

The companion replay note contains full source and a typed driver. On 300 seeded small systems, exhaustive enumeration reproduces the complete constant-direction space and the exact count. A second sample of 300 verifies both witness maps on 6,281 accepting assignments. The 798- and 911-gate cubic fixtures have 32 and 35 guards. Both produce ranks (21, then eleven 1s); the first leaves no constraints and 2^96 witnesses; the second leaves three inconsistent constraints (xy=xz=1, yz=0) and zero witnesses. With an extra four-variable quadratic, the 842-gate fixture reaches a 96-bit witness, accepts 6/16 patterns on its four relevant child bits, and gives 95 full registered witness round trips. The counts are consequences of exact reduction and residual enumeration. The separate typed evaluator parsed six padded gate lists into Circuit 128 4096, executed SigabaNeo.evalGates, accepted the dense, cubic, affine and quartic positive witnesses, and rejected the two named negative examples' test witnesses. A single rejected evaluation does not certify unsatisfiability; the proofs above supply that step.

For use in WE the issuer can send the message bit on publicly certified YES, send a fixed dummy on certified NO, and otherwise encrypt under a valid child backend, with a mode determined solely by the public instance. Dec computes the forward witness map. Reverse lifting ensures a false original statement always leads to a false child; transfer the backend's NO distributions for that child. Any backend with a different statement encoding needs bounded serialization and simulation; repeated images are not free bits of security. Random tests alone cannot justify revealing a message as a YES classification: a nonzero discrepancy can occur on a single input of 2^128. If a nonzero discrepancy has certified degree d, it has density at least 2^-d (induct on a variable in a highest monomial); independent tests miss it with probability at most (1−2^-d)^Q. This option would need an explicit soundness-error budget. The stated branch uses deterministic certificates.

For a deployable cutoff, fix a microstep counter on the actual compiler and reserve B_F of the allowed Enc/Dec and description budget before starting. Charge parsing, XOR recognition, creation of every atom/monomial/pair/derivative subset, every bitset row operation and storage movement, all projected circuit emission, and witness map evaluation on its own machine. Abort a proposed step before its allowance expires, discarding its candidate and dispatching to the secure original-instance fallback, or dispatch the last independently validated child if its serialization fits. Include the restart, routing and transcript costs in the cap. Degree cutoffs alone do not bound sparse support or cancellations. A rank-zero or incompressible valid instance may require the full fallback. No such general backend with a certified clock and no integrated worst-case Machine cost ≤2^100 (or the exploratory 2^32) is established here. The general Lean NAND-to-XAG compiler, complete sparse filtration checker, and certified integrated implementation remain open.
