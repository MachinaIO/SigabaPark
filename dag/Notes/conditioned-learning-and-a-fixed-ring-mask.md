### Several adaptive quarter phases

Fix a public low header L and condition ordinary labeled encryptions on L by the efficient sampler in the first note. Write the d remaining high bits as W. For any fixed mod-four linear form the high bit of that form on the complete header is affine in W: its carry from the low input bits is now a constant. Its low bit is already known. A decoder using a fixed witness, L and at most k such queries, followed by an arbitrary Boolean answer, defines a degree-at-most-k Boolean polynomial on W. Queries may be selected as an arbitrary function of earlier answers, the witness and L. To prove this, a query parity t splits the polynomial into (1+t)P0+tP1; induction on the decision-tree depth proves the degree claim after Boolean reduction. A witness which works for both bits can fix its internal randomness by averaging over the training experiment; equivalently couple the same decoder tape for all training samples, preserving the union bound. This is an upper bound on a reader class, not a computational assumption.

There are D=sum_{j=0}^{min(k,d)} binom(d,j) features. Draw an honest L, then generate N independent uniformly labeled samples conditional on it. Solve the binary linear system for a polynomial classifier over these D features. YES passes with probability at least 1−N epsilon if one witness recovers each message with error at most epsilon on the unconditioned honest experiment. On exactly private NO, fresh labels are independent fair bits even after fixing L, so the chance of feasibility is at most 2^(D−N).

On a computationally private NO, receive one unknown-message challenge, condition the same training experiment on its L, fit any polynomial, and use it to guess the challenge message; give a fair answer on failure. Conditional on L, the probability that any candidate of true accuracy at most 3/4 fits training is at most delta=2^D(3/4)^N. A successful good fit gives signed distinguishing gap at least 1/2, while a bad fit may contribute down to −1. Thus the training test's acceptance probability is at most 2 alpha+3 delta if the resulting one-challenge distinguisher has gap at most alpha. Taking N=4(D+128) makes delta≤2^-128. This yields a scalable SAT decision consequence for constant k and efficient conditional sampling; all issued samples, the feature size, and solving the system are costs of the test. Even for moderate k the concrete procedure can exceed any claimed adversary budget. Arbitrary high-header processing, opaque auxiliary packets lacking the required conditional sampler, or using a number of phases growing with security are outside this conclusion.

### A fixed Ring-LWE sample in a nonlinear-support all-circuit packet

Here is an actual reduction whose ideal full law hides every false registered NAND circuit. For a candidate full trace use 128 inputs, 4095 unpinned internal outputs, and 4096 additional bits w_g. The 8319-bit feature u contains all of them; the constant one and the pinned terminal are substituted. Split each gate into a quadratic test w_g=a_g b_g and an affine test w_g+z_g+1=0. Normalize unavailable references exactly as the registered evaluator. The set of simultaneous zeros is exactly the set of accepting traces. Write B_C for the 4096 augmented binary rows of the affine tests, including their constants. The binary packet has one anchor and one position per feature.

1. Sample 4096 independent selector bits and let q(u) be the selected sum of w_g+a_g b_g. Complete Boolean quadratic pivots over F2 to write q=c+v·u+sum_{j=1}^h L_j(u)M_j(u) with homogeneous linear L_j,M_j; absorb their constant terms into c,v. The number h is half the alternating polar rank. Only primary and unpinned output variables occur quadratically, so h_* = 2111 always bounds it. One can use a smaller publicly certified upper bound for a given circuit. For each pair choose independent a,b, add aL_j+bM_j to v and ab to c. The one-pair Walsh identity gives conditional phase mean 2^-h (-1)^q(u). Keep its sign with probability 2^(h-h_*), otherwise add an independent fair bit to c. Averaging selectors yields exactly 2^-h_* times the indicator of all quadratic tests at every u. This uses at most 4096+2h_*+1 fair bits, bounded without rejection. Direct quadratic Gauss pivot identities already appear in the referenced quadratic residual note; this use samples one selector, instead of enumerating the signed counts.

2. Independently draw one instance of the existing registered decision Ring-LWE law with N=512, eight samples, q=128, uniform secret and CBD(1) coefficient errors. Read bit 6 of each canonical body coefficient (the indicator for residues 64..127), obtaining 4096 bits p from the eight bodies. Add B_Cᵀp to the *whole augmented* binary row of step 1 and add the payload to the anchor. Include the eight ordinary ring headers A in the public packet. The ring bodies, secret, errors, selectors and pad bits are not separately released. The ring and number of samples are fixed in advance for all statements. In the true world this is the stated experiment. In the ideal world p is independent uniform conditional on A since exactly 64 of the 128 residues have bit 6 set. Hence the conditional odd-character profile is the product of the step-1 profile and 1[B_C(1,u)=0]. On every false circuit it vanishes identically, proving equality of all bits of the two ideal public transcripts including A. Supplying the source headers and this public transform to a ciphertext distinguisher gives a legitimate fixed challenge reduction; the two transitions (one per chosen payload) cost at most twice the single-challenge distinguishing bound. No challenge secret or error is required by this transform. The registry entry is SigabaNeo.Lattices.RingLWE.decision at index 1; a quantum reading additionally needs a quantitative quantum source assumption, and the registered machine framework does not certify that.

3. For every genuine witness extending to u, B_C(1,u)=0, so **every** real mask cancels pointwise and scalar TV is exactly 2^-h_*; no property of ideal ring bodies enters correctness. In particular nonlinear support may vary over the entire valid trace family, instead of having to match a publicly chosen support. For a certified h_*≤1, each read succeeds on either bit with probability at least 3/4. With 711 independent copies majority has failure ≤exp(−711/8)<2^-128. The copies must use independent full-source instances (or prove a joint reduction at an enlarged fixed sample count); sharing one 4096-bit pad need not preserve joint ideal cancellation. Independent instances cost up to 1422 single-source hybrids. These are advantage/independence statements, not verified 128-bit concrete strengths. On arbitrary circuits the weak baseline 2^-2111 does not meet the correctness target; even sampling a uniform 128-bit witness can give a larger 2^-128 flat signal. This construction establishes the full reduction with a surviving restricted positive branch, not a general WE submission. Replacing the ring mask by fresh uniform p also works in the transformed ideal construction: Ring-LWE here justifies correlated real pad bits in the presence of ordinary ring headers. It does not amplify the witness signal. Extracting the least significant bit instead would discard this computational distinction altogether: each CBD(1) error's parity is already an independent fair XOR, even conditional on the ring secret and headers. For the chosen bit 6, condition on one ring header being the zero polynomial. Then its selected body coefficient has bit 6 set only for error −1, with probability 1/4. For any NO circuit, honestly evaluate on any fixed primary input and retain the correct products, changing only the terminal output pin to one. This direction passes all quadratic checks, and its affine residual vector is exactly the last unit row. Conditional on that ring header being zero its odd phase has mean 2^(-h_*-1), demonstrating that the real joint NO laws need not coincide statistically. The exceptional event has probability 128^-512; it is a distinction of the mathematical laws, not a viable concrete security claim.

### Implementation inside an actual quarter header

For clarity the binary output can be encoded *without exposing hidden sampler coins* in a circuit-specific deterministic parity sampler M=I. Take L free front coordinates and m private tail coordinates, fixed H=[0 | I_m], c=2e_1. Set their low bits uniformly and independently. The tails supply one bit t=z_tail,1 and enough further independent randomness to run steps 1 and 2 at payload zero, obtaining native anchor U, other entries V and public ring headers A. In the front high bits put (U+t,V,A), and use zero high tail bits. This specifies f_C(z) in r=f_C(z). Form the ordinary Z4 header P=Hᵀs+z+2r and T=cᵀs+2payload with independent uniform s. The sufficient statistic J=T/2−P_tail,1 mod 2 equals payload+t. From J and the first front high bit one recovers payload+U; all other native fields are unchanged. Conversely P_tail, the front low bits, and J are *independent uniform auxiliaries* conditional on the native public packet at the chosen payload: sample them, restore the first front high bit from its native anchor and J, and restore T from J and P_tail,1. Thus a reduction that only sees A and ring bodies can generate the complete quarter transcript through this inverse bijection, even though it cannot reconstruct the hidden bits used for the ring secret and errors. No other public view is omitted.

For a native measure vector (1,u), one can also take the literal Z4 solution with these front bits, zero on the header-A slots, and tail vector 2e_1. Subtract the known carry from the public front low bits; the resulting high bit is exactly the native scalar. Every such solution has a physical entry 2. Consequently universal forbidden-frequency cancellation would erase the entire signal; the NO proof above works because its zeros are imposed on the correct circuit-specific profile. This is a fixed carrier for the constructed experiment rather than a claim that a preassigned graph-incidence H automatically realizes it.

With h_*=2111, L=8319+1+8·512·7=36992 front digits. An honest raw fair-coin ring sample uses 28672 header bits, 3584 secret bits and 8192 error bits; m=1+8319+40448=48768 tail digits suffice. The raw carrier takes 171522 bits including T before framing. It is deliberately explicit; the native binary packet is much smaller. Dense Gaussian elimination, ring arithmetic, circuit parsing, machine clocks and descriptions, and the actual reduction cost are additional. One small full-histogram test used t=xy with x=y=1 and either pin t=0 or t=1: ideal NO histograms agreed exactly, and YES gave 3/4 success for both payloads. Exhausting the auxiliary one-tail quarter embedding recovered every four-bit native packet with precisely uniform independent front-low/tail/J fields. No new hardness assumption is proposed.
