### What the one-challenge argument establishes

At the registered point take N=1024, q=16, 548 rank-one rows, a uniform coefficient secret and independent CBD(1) errors. RingLWE.decision delegates to the rank-one ModuleLWE word game. The distribution lemmas ringLWE_realLaw_eq and ringLWE_idealLaw_eq of SigabaNeoStd.Lattices.RingLWEInst identify those words as encoded negacyclic pairs. Each error coefficient is the difference of two fair bits. The same secret is used for all 548 rows. The matrix expansion in the encoding is deterministic; rotations of one header are correlated. A fresh independent header is available per ring row.

For a fixed NO statement and payload, sample the live keys, live headers, live errors, masks, and any independently issued public graph metadata as in the honest graph. The two graphs use separate key namespaces. Every start belongs to the live set, every live-source edge ends there, and the union contains both sides. The reduction can compute this set by graph reachability before reading the challenge. More generally it may use any affordable edge-closed overapproximation containing the starts and satisfying the terminal separation requirement. List dead-source rows injectively in the 548 slots. The slot assignment may depend on the public graph, which is independent of these coins. Ignored slots can use a dummy hidden source and be projected out.

Write t_v for the independent, private, uniform shift at each dead vertex. For a row u→v with both endpoints dead, use A=α−d and B=r+A t_u+d t_v, with d=4 or 1. With live target use A=α and the known d k_v in the correction. A terminal uses A=α and its bit encoded as the constant polynomial 0 or 8. In the real branch r=αs+e. For fixed s, the map (t,α)↦((s+t_v)_v,(α_i−d_i)_i) is a bijection on the full product space, and each computed B agrees pointwise with the honest expression. The same shift t_v must be reused when v recurs. This covers self-loops, cycles, parallel edges and shared children. In the ideal branch fix t and the independent live coins. The complete pair of header and body vectors is a triangular permutation of the uniform challenge pair. Its inverse first reconstructs α from the public headers and then subtracts the known corrections from the public bodies. Hence the complete dead row bank is independent uniform even conditional on those coins. Forgetting programmed headers would leave a gap in this step.

BoundaryFullView.registered_real and registered_ideal prove both word-law identities for every fixed Boolean test of the complete bank. The output PMF agrees for every such test, including tests of the header/body correlation. Its conditional version accepts arbitrary observation types, so the already sampled root keys, live rows and public routing data can be inserted in their original order. The proof integrates secret, independent errors and all private shifts, avoiding a per-vertex hybrid. The registered wordDist inverse is a mathematical map; a concrete reduction still needs an implementation. The rows alone suffice for the algebraic claim. Extra fields correlated with private coins need their joint law checked. Equal metadata marginals in both message worlds do not by themselves justify conditioning.

### Correctness, terminal masking and explicit reachability

Given a source key, coefficientwise rounding of 4z+e recovers z modulo 4. Subtract that residue from z+e' and round again to recover the high digit. The residues are cyclic modulo 16, each error is in [−1,1], and centers are four apart. This works for all 16 coefficient values including those adjacent to modular wraparound. Each edge recovers its exact destination key, so path length introduces no approximation. For a terminal the constant coefficient phases 0 and 8 have disjoint noise neighborhoods. Accessible root keys and public routing yield every reachable terminal output, without imposing a common-input constraint on independently chosen paths. Correctness of the intended decoder separately requires an authorized witness to give two paths with a shared class.

For independent bits indexed by class, disjoint reachable front/tail classes let one flip precisely the masks of the live tail classes between worlds. All live terminal records then agree under this single permutation; all dead records are already independent. The more general exact criterion is the image of a linear map. Put the entire live terminal vector in a finite abelian group and write f(p)+βd, where p is the uniform auxiliary mask vector. The two laws agree iff d lies in the image of f. Sufficiency translates p by a preimage of d. Necessity uses that d is in the support at β=1. BoundarySymbolicAudit.phase_iff proves both directions. Additional metadata is safe under the same proof if it is pointwise invariant under that translation; affine metadata can be appended as rows with target offset zero. For ordinary class equality, a reachable class appearing on both sides would require its one mask to shift by both zero and one.

BoundarySymbolicAudit.separating_cover proves an edge-closure criterion for a symbolic node proposal. The existence of root-containing edge-closed class-separating covers is equivalent to separation for actual graph reachability. For honest and false instances as promised, an explicit small graph compiler already decides the language by testing reachable-class intersection. Computing covers or finding symbolic paths must still fit the reduction's cost. An implicit description with exponentially many nodes or pooled prefixes that allow inconsistent repeated choices does not inherit the stated bound.

### Challenge and clock accounting

In the 42-layer, width-two parser, each prefix admits at least one clean word. A fault can be selected at each layer after a clean prefix, so all 42 typed fault terminals are reachable. The front root terminal is live. Only the tail clean terminal may be dead. At most one parser state per layer is dead. It has at most three distinct outgoing edges, each using two rows; this gives 253 challenged slots including the possible dead terminal. For the XOR update subclass, disjoint triples let a clean word change one symbol while fixing all the others. If every accepting-clean parity is excluded, each local clean update must be constant. There is then at most one clean destination per state, plus the fault destination, giving 169. BoundaryRowCounts verifies the unique-state injection, the clean-parity implication, and both numerical slot bounds. The incidence interpretation assumes the specified local routing and terminals. The original 548-row game can ignore unused slots. Re-evaluation of the estimator at m=548,253,169 gave H=233,297,335 respectively. Invoking the latter two uses different fixed parameter points. The public 548-row issuance cap and the number of challenge rows used are separate.

The raw 548 pairs occupy 4,489,216 compact bits, while the registered word frames the expanded mN-by-N matrix of residues and the mN bodies. The library bound is 20,692,480,001 input bits. Prog.decodeM with K=4, T=4,489,216 and this input bound has clock plus description at most 4,432,923,803,936,027,597,344,883,446,924,616, below 2^112. RingLWEWord.decodeW_encodeRing identifies the output as a success flag followed by compact header and body words. The estimator uses LWE dimension 1024 and 561152 coefficient samples; its H is the output of its finite attack model.

Let δ be the difference of acceptance probabilities between the real payload worlds. Testing a fair payload by matching the distinguisher's output gives real-versus-common-ideal gap of magnitude |δ|/2, hence squared decision gain δ²/4. Equivalently one of two fixed-bit tests has at least this squared gain. The 233-bit assumption would transfer 128 bits if the complete reduction costs at most 2^103 times the WE adversary cost. The native WE tuple frames the fixed parameters and the unary gate list ahead of the ciphertext. Each of 4096 gates has an encoding at least three bits long. The outer statement frame and the parameter frame give at least 24578 identical prefix bits. A machine stopping within that prefix has exactly identical output PMFs for any two ciphertext laws. For every nonzero-gap adversary the above entire parser bound is below 2^98 times its cost. BoundaryEncodingAudit proves this using the tape's clocked prefix locality. The arithmetic leaves over 31/32 of the multiplicative budget for other operations when the preparation and execution costs can actually be added. A bound for the compiled composition, including paying for its transition table, private sampling, live products, graph computation and invoking the adversary, remains necessary. Neither the hardness number nor the prefix calculation bounds the cost of the complete native encryption or decryption machine. A quantum conclusion requires a separate assumption for quantum distinguishers on classical samples and an efficient uniform simulation in that model.

### A symbolic extension with nonlinear payloads

For a vector of hidden base keys t, write t+s for adding the same ring element to every component. A symbolic target F can have nonlinear dependence on the individual keys while remaining affine along this common translation. Put d(t)=F(t+1)−F(t). If F(t+s)=F(t)+d(t)s for all t,s, program the header A=α−d(t) and body B=r+A t_u+F(t). For each t, a uniform header remains uniform. For the real branch the inverse to the joint key/header map first recovers t from the honest keys using the fixed s. The ideal map still permutes the complete header/body pair for each t. Conversely, with a raw source t_u+s and one untouched challenge body plus a secret-independent correction, a pointwise identity for every α,s forces precisely this diagonal-affinity condition, even if the emitted header and additive correction depend on α and t. The identities at s=0 and 1 determine the slope. For example F(k)=(k_v−k_w)k_x has d(t)=t_v−t_w. The raw square of a single key fails the criterion whenever 2 is nonzero, as seen at s=0,1,2.

A derived source G also works when G(t+s)=G(t)+a(t)s and a(t) is a unit. Use A=(α−d(t))a(t)⁻¹ and B=r+A G(t)+F(t). At fixed t the header affine map is invertible, and the same argument gives exact full-bank real and ideal PMFs for a family of arbitrary such sources and targets, even with private shift-dependent slopes. Being able to solve A a+d=α for every α forces a to be a unit. An eligible nonlinear source is G(k)=k_u+(k_v−k_w)², of slope one. For a pair of decoding rows the payloads are 4F and F and their slopes scale accordingly. A holder of G can therefore recover the prescribed ring value F. To use F itself as a subsequent source, its own slope needs appropriate justification. The symbolic lemma supplies a row transport mechanism; it does not establish a path authorization scheme for general Circuit-SAT. In particular realistic reachable outputs and any public metadata depending on base keys must still be simulatable jointly.

The independent replay checked the full cyclic two-row joint law at modulus four for every shift pair on ideal inputs and every fixed secret/error triple on real inputs. All 64 three-layer clean Boolean parity tables passed the constant-update test. A separate modulo-16 check validated the nonlinear source/target identity on 393216 tuples. These are controls for the formal arguments. The review validates a conditional reduction for the explicit graphs and gives a stronger symbolic-row criterion. It provides neither an attack on the assumption nor a proof of the full registered witness encryption game.
