## What must be encoded

For the Boolean F3 fiber sign, the value as a bit at any raw input x is beta if C(x)=1, and rho=beta XOR k otherwise, where k is the one semantic fair coin. Extra coins may randomize the public representation. An evaluator can compute C(x) and return rho on rejection. A complete implementation must protect the accepted value even if no accepting x exists. We examine a concrete noncanonical noisy representation. It never needs a group encoding, an equality oracle for algebra elements, or secret group operations performed by a witness. Byte equality and ordinary arithmetic on public numbers remain available to the observer. The evaluation is nonlinear rounding; adding two serialized packets need not produce a valid encoding of their semantic product.

Fix q=2^ell, Delta=q/2, public H of shape m by n and c of length m over Z/q. Choose uniform independent s in (Z/q)^m, independent fair bits e_i in the n coordinates, and fair k. Publish

    P = H^T s + e,       T = c^T s + Delta beta,       rho = beta XOR k.

For an accepting x, a sufficient witness map returns an integer lift lambda with H lambda=c mod q and sum of absolute values strictly below q/4. Decode T-lambda·P by nearest center 0 or Delta, which succeeds on every coin. All rejecting x evaluate to rho. This realizes both semantic branches on the promise below. Matrix choice depends on the public statement. All proofs here are unconditional distribution calculations; no ordinary-LWE claim is made for the structured H.

## Both full-view characterizations

The two public message distributions are exactly equal iff **every modular solution** of H lambda=c has some coordinate Delta, including the case of no solution. To see this, Fourier-transform the complete pair (P,T). The independent uniform rho factors out. A frequency (u,a) can differ between messages only for odd a. It vanishes on averaging s unless Hu+a c=0. The fair bits contribute factors (1+exp(2 pi i u_j/q))/2, zero precisely at u_j=Delta. Odd a is a unit; multiplying by -1/a produces lambda and preserves Delta. Conversely a solution without this coordinate gives a nonzero sensitive Fourier coefficient. This is a statement about every coefficient of the *full* public law, regardless of the rank of H.

Opposite message supports intersect iff there is a dual seed difference d with c·d=Delta and H^T d in {-1,0,1}^n mod q. Necessity follows by subtracting two ciphertext equations; each signed bit difference is also realizable by an explicit pair. Such a d of signed weight w yields overlap mass at least 2^-w: fix the signs of the w toggled errors on one orientation, shift the uniform seed, and switch exactly those bits. The injective matching of equally likely source tapes gives the bound even when recordings have many names. A decoder with two conditional errors at most epsilon therefore requires 2 epsilon >= 2^-w if this certificate occurs. For perfect correctness on every YES, NO equality supplies a polynomial-size certificate of falsity in this architecture, whereas no YES can have one. A scalable universal polynomial-time compiler with both exact properties would put UNSAT in NP. This implication does not exclude the registered negligible-error condition at a fixed parameter.

A convenient sufficient exact-NO coupling is H^T d equal to a single positive unit coordinate and c·d=Delta. Flip its error e_j, and move s by (2 e_j-1)d. The entire header agrees, T changes by exactly Delta, and the map is an involution. Simultaneously flipping the semantic coin preserves rho between opposite messages.

## A complete sign representation for path verification

For an undirected graph with different designated endpoints u,v, orient the edges arbitrarily. Use one ordinary column Delta(e_a-e_b) per edge (a,b), a fallback column e_u-e_v and target c=Delta(e_u-e_v). The target always lies in the public column span, including on false statements. For a valid path use its signed edge traversal counts and a zero fallback coefficient. A walk with at most seven transitions gives norm at most seven. At q=32 this meets the strict rounding margin. In a disconnected graph the indicator d of the component of u has zero pairings on every ordinary edge, pairing one on the fallback, and c·d=16. The preceding coupling proves exact equality for all bits in the public representation. Pruning or testing connectivity at issuance is unnecessary.

An eight-vertex graph can be represented by a registered NAND verifier: use the first 24 raw bits as eight three-bit vertex names, require the endpoints and check each adjacent pair against the public edge-or-stay table. Any reachable pair has a simple path of at most seven transitions; stays permit padding. A six-bit table needs at most 63 binary multiplexers with at most three NANDs each plus six reusable negations and constants. Seven tables, the two endpoint tests and the final ANDs fit well below 4096 gates. Padding with two self-NANDs preserves the output; an odd remaining length of at least three admits the identity via NOT(x), NAND(x,NOT(x)), NAND(NOT(x),1). This is a recognizable graph-verifier class and public reachability is efficiently decidable on it. With at most 28 ordinary columns the representation is at most 151 bits before framing, with at most 70 independent fair bits (eight five-bit seeds, at most 29 errors, one semantic coin). Every input, including a rejected walk, has the required sign. No end-to-end registered machine clock is asserted.

## A universal exact-NO compiler that fails even with complete access on YES

There is a direct reduction from **every** registered 128-input, 4096-gate NAND circuit to H lambda=c at q=8, with a solution avoiding 4 exactly when the statement is satisfiable. Start with 20608 purported Boolean coordinates: 128 inputs, 4096 output wires, and four local one-hot selectors per gate for the legal NAND rows. For each gate require its four selectors to sum to one, and its two input wires and output wire each to equal the sum of the selectors whose respective bit is one. Substitute the public constant for an unavailable parent, and reuse the same variable for a repeated wire. Pin the final wire to one. Over Boolean values and q=8, none of the one-hot sums wraps. A true ordinary witness uniquely extends to these coordinates; conversely they give the usual gate-by-gate trace.

For each original coordinate x append the four coordinates a_j,b_j at j=0,1 with rows a_j-2^j x=0 and a_j-b_j=2^j. The four monitored values are x,x-1,2x,2(x-1). They all avoid four precisely for x=0 or 1; the Boolean-guard Lean lemma checks every residue. The short dyadic version at arbitrary ell uses j=0,...,ell-2: every nonzero even residue has a dyadic multiple equal to Delta, applied to x or x-1 according to parity. No duplicated-coefficient simplification is used in the q=8 counts. There are 103040 columns and 98817 rows, with sparse, directly generated coefficients. Honest issuance consumes 399492 fair bits and publishes 309124 bits before framing. Perfect NO privacy of this candidate at all circuits follows from the complete Fourier criterion. Merely having a nonzero sensitive character on each YES gives much less than the required correctness.

In fact **even an unbounded decoder using every header bit and a genuine witness** cannot significantly distinguish the payloads for this compiler. In each of the n0=20608 gadgets the top defining row a_1-b_1=2 has a dedicated uniform mask t. Its two header coefficients are t plus a fixed term and e_plus, and -t plus a fixed term and e_minus; its anchor coefficient is q/4=2. Give the observer every other row mask, every other error, each ordinary integer sum e_plus+e_minus, and t+e_plus modulo q. The entire P is fixed by this augmented side information. Conditional on a mixed pair (sum one), e_plus remains an independent fair orientation because t was independent uniform. The number K of mixed pairs has distribution Binomial(n0,1/2). Thus the residual anchor noise, up to a known translate, is -(q/4) times Binomial(K,1/2). The affine rows that elsewhere refer to these columns do not change this calculation.

On the four-cycle the TV distance of a K-step binary walk from its translate by two is exactly 2^(-floor(K/2)). For masses p_j the two signed differences (p0-p2,p1-p3) have l1 norm equal to this TV. One step maps (a,b) to ((a-b)/2,(a+b)/2); two steps rotate and halve. Both initial parities K=0,1 have TV one. Averaging over the augmented data bounds the actual ciphertext distance by

    E[2^(-floor(K/2))] <= sqrt(2) ((1+1/sqrt(2))/2)^20608 < 2^-4707.

This upper bound applies to every circuit and is independent of how the receiver tries to correct the errors. Independent repetition has distance at most the number of packets times their single-packet distance. Even allowing one bit per repetition, to reach conditional error 2^-128 would require more than 2^4706 repetitions, exceeding the 2^100 cap. Shared masks, correlated non-product error distributions or a different compiler need a fresh proof for the whole law. A smaller mod-four graph-coloring route similarly admits local mask swaps along a matching; the contraction above handles the actual raw NAND compiler, not an externally unpriced graph reduction.

## Why a public header need not have a public conditional sampler

For fixed H, conditioning on P=h makes e uniform among binary vectors in h-im(H^T), since every allowed seed fiber has the same size. Finding or uniformly sampling these error vectors on a prespecified supported header can itself represent a nontrivial problem. For example encode the one-hot gate constraints as a modular affine system B e=r at q=8 without Boolean guards: sampled e is already binary. Add a flag t and binary slack constraints input_i+u_i=t and out=t+v. On Boolean values there is exactly one dummy solution at t=0, the actual all-zero-input execution, and precisely one solution per accepting assignment at t=1. Without Boolean requirements, a modular particular solution e0 is always easy: choose arbitrary wire values respecting the output condition and solve each gate's four selector equations; the four legal augmented rows are unimodular (for given x,y,z take the 11-selector 1-z, then subtract it from each parent value to get the 10 and 01 selectors). Fill the remaining slack and 00-selector by the stated equations. Generate im H^T=ker B over Z/8 by modular linear algebra (or Smith normal form), and set h=e0. The header is guaranteed supported. An exact sampler for its binary preimages returns t=1 with probability at least one half on a satisfiable input, and never on a false one. Repeating gives a one-sided randomized SAT test. This concerns adversarially prescribed supported headers and actual *preimages*. It does not prove hardness at a random honest header or equivalence to conditional body-only sampling. The complete positive path construction and the all-circuit failure above do not assume such a sampler.

The five formal nodes cover the q=8 Boolean guard, two-step signed norm, exact seed/error flip, odd-frequency half turn, and opposite support intersection. The whole-distribution arguments and construction-level cost counts are mathematical analyses. No new hardness game or generic post-quantum WE is claimed.
