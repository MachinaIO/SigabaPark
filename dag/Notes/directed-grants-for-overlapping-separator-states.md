### Public representation and honest algorithms

Represent a conjunction of finite local relations on a rooted tree. Every constrained wire's occurrences form a connected subtree, its local predicate appears in a bag containing its arguments and boundary conditions include the constant and accepting output. The state on an edge is the restriction to the parent/child intersection, which may differ on every edge. Use an empty parent separator at the root, with a single state. For each pair (bag v, its parent-separator state s) independently sample a uniform lambda-bit K[v,s]. For each locally allowed assignment publish one grant containing its parent-state key XOR one pad from each child's projected state key. With no child the grant is the plain key. Enumerate the same local row order at decryption. One occurrence of a child-state key means one distinct pad position identified by the public bag, row and child port; two identical projections in different rows are distinct usages. Identical *whole* signatures (output state and list of child states) may be deduplicated before numbering. At root publish the payload XOR its key's first bit, reserving the root key for this single terminal use. All other uses of a key as grant plaintext are separate from its outgoing pad positions.

A witness supplies all relevant row indices from its trace. From leaves upward, XOR each already recovered child pad into the corresponding grant; its parent key is recovered exactly for every random tape. Every satisfying witness works. A state is reachable iff some locally allowed grant producing it has *all* child states reachable: one direction restricts the subtree witness, the other pastes child witnesses onto the chosen bag tuple. The running-intersection condition guarantees two subtrees cannot disagree on a variable outside the parent bag. Therefore root reachability is exactly the original conjunction. This is a proof invariant; honest issuance enumerates local information and never computes subtree reachability. The signed marginal relaxation can satisfy the false tree with leaves x=y=1 and middle NAND(x,y)=1: signed weights -1,1,1 on 00,01,10 produce the formal desired leaf marginals. In the directed scheme each of those three rows has a missing child label and no accepting root can be derived.

### Exactly two fixed native rows per expansion

Set N=2048, q=64, lambda=6N=12288, and R=Z_q[X]/(X^N+1). Sample two independent, uniform public polynomials A0,A1 once per ciphertext (12N header bits). A lambda-bit seed splits into 3N pairs of fair bits. Map each pair to first bit minus second, forming independent CBD(1) coefficients of s,e0,e1 with N entries each. G_A(seed) serializes A0 s+e0 and A1 s+e1 in that fixed order, with six little-endian bits per coefficient. Each output half has lambda bits. The public-header/real-output joint law is exactly fixed decision Ring-LWE with m=2 ring samples, not two independent secrets. The ideal bodies become independent uniform halves even given A. The registered challenge uses its native integer/list encoding; a reduction must parse and reserialize that encoding. The release 640702122226 computation of RingLWE.H on precisely these parameters returned 762. This is a registry model value; no quantum security theorem or bit-level cost certificate is asserted by that computation.

For a state used f times, fix a balanced binary tree with f leaves in public occurrence order. Expand a seed at every internal node using G_A and give every occurrence its own leaf string. For f=1 use the state key directly and only once, f=0 has no outgoing expansion. The real issuer evaluates exactly max(f-1,0) source calls per state if materializing each once. The receiver can derive the leaf on its relevant path, requiring at most ceil(log2 f) calls. A state with multiple occurrences must not use its raw key as one pad plus further expansions from that key. This schedule realizes the finite list of domain-separated pads directly; no arbitrary-domain PRF oracle is needed. All keys, coefficients and tweaks have finite exact coin or deterministic formats. Total real source ring-row evaluations equal twice the number of expansion calls. Every call reuses the same public A; its seed and all internal coin-generating halves remain private except where the actual protocol exposes state keys.

### Whole-view NO reduction

For a fixed false instance compute reachability only in the reduction, traversing the finite published tables or using fixed-instance advice (charge that advice). Proceed from leaf states towards the root. As soon as pads of an unreachable state are made independent, every previously unsanitized row consuming one of those pads may be replaced by an independent uniform lambda-bit grant. A unique occurrence supplies a dedicated uniform pad, unused by any other grant. Conditional on all other columns, vector translation by the remaining masks and possibly repeated parent keys is a bijection (simultaneousPad). Thus sanitizing the complete collection preserves the current joint law, including all known labels and openable rows. If two unreachable children feed one row, sanitize it only once.

Consider an unreachable state s when its turn starts. Every row granting s has an unreachable child (missingChild), hence was already sanitized. There can be no leaf grant exposing s; a state with no grants needs no such replacements. No unsanitized public field other than its outgoing pad tree now needs its true seed. Starting with a hidden uniform seed, switch the tree's internal expansions top down. At a switched node use the real/ideal two-body Ring-LWE challenge as its two child seeds, without knowing the source seed. Its parent expansion, if any, was already switched. Draw siblings and unrelated labels independently and compute all visible known-key trees under the challenge header. At intermediate levels the incoming hidden seed need not be written anywhere: its sole role was as source for this node. Entire paths and all its leaf positions, including other rows and any already sanitized fields, are simulated consistently. This realizes the two adjacent games exactly. One-use states need no hardness hop; their unseen uniform seed itself serves as the only pad. The proof samples tagged state keys independently and splits once at each tree node, so implicit accidental bit-string collisions are handled by the real/ideal transitions; they are not an assumed collision-free invariant.

At the unreachable root every grant of K[root] has been sanitized. The terminal field is now an independent fair bit in either world. For h internal expansions associated to unreachable state keys, and gap Delta between the two full real payload laws, some fixed two-row challenge reduction has absolute acceptance gap at least Delta/(2h), by telescoping with random payload or two separate worlds. For h=0 the actual joint NO laws are exactly equal. Uniform h-branch hybrid indexing is unnecessary for the existential best-hop bound. If every embedded reduction costs at most K times the adversary in the registered step-plus-description metric, sufficient strength is 2^128·4h²K ≤ 2^762. This inequality concerns squared gain. Simulation includes advice or reachability, the decoder for the native challenge, other expansions and complete ciphertext generation; raw counts alone do not establish K. A quantum algorithm on classical ciphertexts makes no superposition queries to the source in this argument. Applying the reduction to it requires the corresponding quantum hardness of that same fixed sample problem. No new assumption has been introduced.
