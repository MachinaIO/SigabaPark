The complete 4,224-variable local switching dictionary fits below 30 million public ring-data bits in three tested parameterizations. One of them packs 12 prime-field coordinates per tag degree, processes all aggregate supports correctly, and gives strength 139 in the fixed ordinary Ring-LWE registry. Its issuer computes one inverse transform per CRT component of each ring sample. A direct implementation using the registered tape language still exceeds the 2^32 cost objective; we identify and count the particular kernels responsible. This contribution implements a local join for the existing goal, not a complete witness encryption. It succeeds [task 168](https://github.com/MachinaIO/SigabaPark/pull/168), whose binary-gadget scalar bodies already exceeded 2^32 bits.

### Mathematical interface

Let N=4224, M=3N=12672. Positive and negative variable i carry source exponents i and M−i for 1≤i≤N. The D=6N−3=25341 unary and legal product degrees are precisely the integers in [1,6N−2] except M. This includes equal-polarity repeated occurrences. With uniform nonzero hidden τ in the source field, T_d=τ^d. Uniform independent source messages m_e can be published as (a,c=m_e−aT_e) for independent a, or as c=m_e−T_e at fixed a=1. Source rows for a reused label must be memoized. With fixed a the ideal public rows are independent uniform. Equivalently draw c directly and define the underlying independent random label by m_e=c+T_e. Extra data giving the labels themselves changes the game.

For sets S,T of positive and negative IDs form the actual public polynomials f=Σ_(i∈S)(c_i+X^i), g=Σ_(j∈T)(d_j+X^(M−j)). In the actual product h=fg the coefficient h_M is exactly |S∩T|, as an element of the source ring. The Lean theorem actual_overlap proves this for arbitrary constants, finite support sets and every commutative coefficient ring, including the zero-variable edge case. In a field of characteristic >N or the dyadic source ring below this coefficient vanishes iff the sets are disjoint. Thus source rows and the checked coefficient can be linked by public recomputation of the same h. Scalar h_d for d≠M can be switched together when h_M=0. Equal-polarity individual products use their legal sum degree and the same bank. Repeated occurrences are permitted by reusing a record; the set-count test presupposes deduplicated support IDs. A route claiming IDs different from its actual hidden garbled labels still requires authentication. The same omission does not by itself track arbitrary three-factor products.

Let n be the ring degree, sk one shared fresh destination, a_b a uniform header in R_q=(Z/q)[X]/(X^n+1), and e_b independent coefficientwise CBD(1), supported on −1,0,1. Divide all logical slots in a fixed order into m blocks of n coefficients. Publish a_b and the body z_b=a_b sk+e_b+mask_b. A slot at (b,j) has a scalar header row U_t=a_{b,j−t} for t≤j and −a_{b,n+j−t} for t>j, including wrap at j=0. Its body is z_{b,j}. For arbitrary weights with repeated blocks or indices, summing weighted bodies minus the inner product of the weighted aggregate row with sk leaves exactly the weighted payloads plus errors. weighted_extraction uses Cslib.negacyclicMul and its actual rotMatrix as in the registry. Logical slots are distinct for simulation; repetitions during evaluation are simply public reuse.

One registered decision Ring-LWE instance supplies ALL m blocks, with the stipulated CBD or uniform secret below. The reduction samples τ and the label records, then adds the known correlated masks to the challenge bodies. Conditional on the headers, ideal bodies are jointly uniform with independent coefficients even when many logical slots come from the same polynomial. Extra arbitrary deterministic functions of the published records, including every correctly formed lookup row, may be returned. This gives a single challenge reduction with no factor for the number of slots; replacing independent destination layers is a different hybrid. The missing-power guessing check samples τ and compares a proposed scalar to τ^M without consulting SAT. The exact ideal baseline is the reciprocal power-map image size. Disjoint unmasked columns can supply a public residual tester as described in task 168. For n-dimensional secrets in any modulus, v uniform independent test bodies and B=1, its ideal false acceptance for any proposed secret is at most q^n(3/q)^v by a union bound conditional on all headers. To interpret a returned tag as a secret vector, use an explicit injective encoding and reject out-of-range candidates. The checker alone does not bind arbitrary witness-chosen rows to garbled gates. Quantum security requires the quantum version of the assumption on these classical challenge inputs. The registry, estimator, and formal Machines have classical semantics.

### Dyadic source and radix comparison

Use source modulus P=2^160, τ uniformly odd. Unit group C₂ × C_(2^158) and M=2^7·99 give an image of size 2^151 and exact ideal guessing probability 2^−151. For q=PΔ=2^w, radix 2^b, let a degree slot j carry Δ·2^(bj)·T_d. The public coefficient h_d reduced to [0,P) selects its digits. Multiplying each slot by that digit and adding the clear constant times Δ yields Δ times the true source value modulo q, with additive noise bounded by the sum of digits. Center then round to the nearest multiple of Δ modulo P. nearest_scaled formalizes integer rounding under arbitrary whole wraps, also for negative error. For a pair there are at most three nonconstant terms; repeated degrees are harmless. With five limbs of 32 bits, the 3·5·(2^32−1) radius is <2^47. With one 160-bit multiplier it is <2^167. Full dense aggregates need the sum over all used degrees, which can exceed the available margin in this table.

### Small coefficient fields for all supports

Use K=Fp[X]/F, degree r=12, and encode each T_d in r basis coordinates. Selection coefficient h_d∈K acts by an r×r matrix over Fp. For output coordinate l, multiply the d,j slot, carrying Δ(T_d)_j, by the canonical coefficient in [0,p−1] of the l,j matrix entry for multiplication by h_d. Add Δ times the clear coordinate of h_0. Exactly Dr logical slots are sufficient for all r outputs. Each output's absolute error is at most Dr(p−1); the weights are not multiplied into one another after switching. If q=pΔ and this radius is <Δ/2, modulo-p nearest decoding is exact for every support and every permitted CBD error, including repeated uses duly collected modulo p. Distinct slots can be combined in public computations without assuming additional Ring-LWE instances. With a=1, all 2N source records together use 2Nr·ceil(log₂ p) bits. The target decryption key is one ring element, and the evaluated ciphertext has r scalar samples (one public n-vector plus one body per output coordinate if materialized).

* For p=8191 take F=X^12+X+12. The finite-field Frobenius calculation finds gcd(F,X^(p^4)−X)=gcd(F,X^(p^6)−X)=1 and X^(p^12)=X mod F, a Rabin irreducibility check. Take Δ=2^33 and q=pΔ. For n=2048 and m=151, comprising 149 occupied or padded bank blocks and two reserved test blocks, the raw H,Z bank costs 28,450,816 bits. Sources add 1,317,888 bits. The aggregate error bound 2,490,513,480 is below 2^32. The power-map fiber size is 1152. Uniform secret and CBD(1) error give the pinned strengths 141 at (2048,151), 301 at (4096,77) and 663 at (8192,40); (512,596) gives 42. All are independent evaluations, not interpolated figures. The full-size replay generates all 151 actual negacyclic products by unsigned Kronecker substitution with enough bits between coefficients to avoid carries, independently checks one direct coefficient in each and decodes two legitimate aggregate products. A two-element overlap gives coefficient 2.
* For a transform-friendly exact scale take p=12289, F=X^12−11. Here 11 is primitive modulo p (p−1=2^12·3, both factor tests pass). The standard irreducible-binomial criterion applies: every prime divisor of 12 divides ord(11), the index of its subgroup is one, and p=1 mod 4. The other two CRT primes are 65537 and 114689. Thus Δ=7,516,372,993, q=92,368,707,710,977, and 2Dr(p−1)=7,473,364,992<Δ. Roots of exact order 4096 modulo the three primes are respectively 1331, 54449 and 6932. Degree n=2048, m=151, uniform secret and CBD(1) error give registered H=139. The binary challenge and the signed rotation definition are unchanged. A triple-residue coefficient costs 14+17+17=48 bits (a single modulus representative could use 47); the 151 header/body blocks occupy 29,687,808 bits, plus 1,419,264 bits for the source c rows. The local power-guess ideal probability is 1152/(12289^12−1). A tester uses 4096 reserved coordinates; its union bound applies to all adaptive attempts.

For each CRT prime P, write ρ for its specified primitive root and ω=ρ². With evaluations at ρ·ω^j the negacyclic transform is a bijection. Draw each public header and the uniform secret in the evaluation domain independently and uniformly. For each block multiply their evaluations pointwise, compute ONE inverse cyclic transform with root ω^−1, multiply the coefficient at i by n^−1 ρ^−i, then add noise and payload in the coefficient domain. The SAME ternary error at each logical coefficient is used in all three CRT components, as required by the registered sampler. The payload vanishes modulo both factors of Δ. Publish the header evaluations and coefficient bodies, charging their full representation. A challenge reduction given coefficient headers applies the forward transform before publishing them. A destination with a CBD secret could not sample it uniformly in this domain.

An explicit public switched header for output weights w_(b,i) can stay in the evaluation domain: at index j sum aHat_(b,j) times Σ_i w_(b,i) n^−1(ρ·ω^j)^−i over blocks. The weighted sum of coefficient bodies supplies V. This formula does not use the secret. The destination checks V−Σ_j U_j skHat_j and centered CRT reconstruction before rounding. Computing that header costs time: either directly expand every selected rotation or transform each populated weight vector for each output and combine pointwise; deferring it merely moves work. The full-size replay issued every block, independently checked one inverse coefficient in each of the 453 CRT transforms, and recovered a full 12-coordinate product with the displayed public header formula.

### Clocks, tables and sampling

The trusted machine has one fair coin and at most one output bit per step. For k working tapes and s states its description costs 6s·3^k·(4+4k+Nat.size(s)) bits. No tape/word count below is a certificate for the complete compiler.

We priced a concrete registered Tape.Prog inner kernel. It doubles the W-bit accumulator with carry, conditionally adds the multiplicand according to the top multiplier bit (the clear branch makes a same-width copy), then removes that bit. Each of its three column primitives carries the pinned bound 2W+3 ticks. The if test costs one. horner_column_clock checks the exact compiled body clock 3(2W+3)+1. With an initialized accumulator and a b-bit multiplier this implements Horner in b such rounds, modulo 2^W. Using W=2b keeps the full product; modular reduction costs extra. This is an actual registered compiler charge for one specified kernel, not an unavoidable cost for multiplication, and initialization, looping and all descriptions are additional. The arithmetic semantics uses the core bitsVal_add lemma. A compact loop still charges the body every iteration.

* The dyadic issuer enumerates 6N−2=25,342 powers when proceeding successively from 1 (it must pass the omitted degree); direct width-160 Horner multiplication therefore charges 3,933,078,400 ticks for powers alone. For n=8192, schoolbook CBD-secret convolution visiting each of n coefficients for every emitted body coefficient takes mn² ternary additions in a fixed-loop implementation. Even charging only w bit updates per add, 16·8192²·208 exceeds 2^37. Public output length is a much smaller term.
* For CRT setup, 151·2048 pointwise products in each of the three fields, when implemented by the stated raw integer kernel, charge 3,020,734,464 ticks before reduction. Each of 453 inverse transforms uses (n/2)log₂ n=11264 butterflies, before normalization, twiddles and serial data rearrangement. A literal schoolbook source-field update alone uses 25,342·12² scalar multiplications modulo 12289. With b=14 and W=28 the kernel charges 9,093,926,016 ticks before even reducing those products. This schedule exceeds 2^32 independently of the remaining transform costs or the small serialized output. The implementation in Python uses host integer arithmetic; its running time does not replace the multi-tape clock.
* Fixed-τ source multiplication admits a better table. Precompute 12·14 rows giving τ times each binary basis component as a 12-vector modulo p. A current power's bits select rows. Each integer component accumulator is <168(p−1)+1<2^21, so scanning all 168 rows and 12 components costs 42,336 bit positions per power with 21-bit padding. If one scans the accumulator forward then resets it between rows, the two passes visit 84,672 positions per power. Table reset, modular reductions, loading selected bits, generating the table and both machine descriptions remain. This schedule removes the full set of schoolbook scalar multiplications on the source side. It has no end-to-end clock certificate.
* An exact finite fair-coin clock cannot sample a uniform residue of an odd prime exactly. For the RNS option, take w+192 fair bits for a w-bit prime and reduce modulo that prime. Total variation per scalar is <2^−192. Fewer than 2^21 scalars, including 3n secret coordinates, 3mn headers and 12·2N source slots, contribute <2^−171 by coupling. For τ draw two complete field elements and keep the first nonzero or fall back to one; the double-zero exception adds |K|^−2 for exact component draws. Independent CBD(1) uses two bits per body coefficient before sharing the error across RNS limbs. A bounded implementation must count the extra bits, small-modulus reductions, source-power computation, conversion of the challenge's framed word, layout transpositions, tape repositioning, output and description. In the power-of-two setup header coins and the odd source unit are directly sampled at fixed length. Direct uniform sampling of the field c rows can be combined with defining the hidden random labels from them; garbled tables that also consume those labels are outside the present algorithm.

A successor needs a costed fast modular multiplication and transform layout on a small number of tapes, including all headers or their actual reconstruction. The present data and reductions certify a complete *local dictionary*, a substantive improvement over the previously documented body-only overflow. Proof of fully authenticated cross-gate consistency, the message-dependent terminal, a quantum full-view reduction and a sub-2^32 global Machine certificate remain open. The official goal's outer cap is separately 2^100.

For the dense public evaluator with 149 populated blocks, constructing all coefficient-domain weights and evaluating the dual-transform formula takes up to 149·12·3=5364 short cyclic transforms plus 10,985,472 pointwise products to combine transformed weights with headers, before twists. Final decryption of 12 materialized evaluation-domain rows needs another 73,728 modular products across the three primes. A single two-label gate has just three nonconstant source degrees; its 12-coordinate slots touch at most six populated blocks. Computing the coefficients h themselves costs work, including off-diagonal difference counts of the chosen sets; a literal enumeration visits |S||T| pairs. The Python replay used the direct public coefficient formula for one gate.

The trusted ModuleLWE.encodeSample serializes a rank-one ring header as a framed, expanded ordinary matrix. At m=151,n=2048 the body begins after at least 4mn²+2mn+1=2,533,978,113 input positions even when every natural-number entry encodes zero. More precisely its offset additionally includes eight times the sum of bitlengths of the expanded residue entries; uniformly sampled nonzero-width headers are much longer. A one-head reduction reading that body must pay traversal, separately from public encryption size and the earlier honest computation ledger. An estimator figure of 139 is not a 139-bit end-to-end guarantee after accounting for a particular concrete reduction.
