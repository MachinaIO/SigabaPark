For opened source sets A, C and J, authorize whenever some a in A, c in C and j in J satisfy a != j and c != j. The two indices a and c may coincide. Represent each index below 4224 by four base-nine digits, viewed as cubic coefficients over a field of nine elements. Use seven distinct field points. For any authorized triple, the product of its two polynomial differences from j is nonzero of degree at most six, hence at some point x both values differ from the value of j. The 63 terms are pairs (x, v). A or C is eligible on inequality to v, J on equality. Conversely any occupied term supplies an authorized triple. The Lean theorem wins_iff quantifies over arbitrary sets of sources in all three roles.

Each term receives independent uniform bits u and v and shares u, v, beta XOR u XOR v. Each eligible incidence receives an independent uniform pad. Publish the role share XOR its incidence pad and return only the pads belonging to an opened source. A satisfying triple unmasks three shares at a common term and recovers beta. For a losing union, choose one missing role per term. When flipping beta, flip that role's share and all its incidence pads at the term. The new independent bits and pads are obtained by an involution, all public corrections agree and all opened coordinates agree. The equivalence fibers gives identical counts for every complete public record and collection of open pads. Dividing by the common number of uniformly sampled coin strings gives exact equality of laws.

For a bounded classical adaptive reader, fix its independent coins and an entire transcript, including the initial public correction vector. If its queried sources form a losing union, choose the missing roles for this particular union. The involution preserves that outcome: replay queries from the initial state; identical public bits, query answers and internal state determine each next query. The inverse replays in the opposite direction. transcriptFibers proves equality of the raw transcript fiber sizes and hence of their probability masses. Summing transcripts gives equal probabilities of every losing terminal mask and of events within that mask. Conditioning on a specified mask is valid when that mask has positive probability. Independent reader coins may be averaged. A classical source opening can be expanded into a fixed sequence of coordinate reads, treating every opened source as part of the mask. Extra supplier outputs, validity flags or correlated side information require their own joint simulation. The identical classical record has identical diagonal density operators for a quantum receiver; coherent opening queries are outside the model.

Each A or C source has 56 eligible incidences, each J source seven. There are 502656 correction bits and the same number of source-pad bits. Adding three flags per index gives 1017984 bits, excluding framing, the supplier and all other encryption data. Independent coordinates and losing unions across the whole false execution are requirements on the supplier. These counts and the algebraic Lean specification do not certify the clock or description of a tape machine. This component supplies no full proof of capped Circuit-SAT witness encryption.
