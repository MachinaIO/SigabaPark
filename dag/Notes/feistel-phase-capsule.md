## A true cyclic programming instance

Use modulus q=2^l with l≥3. All designated feedback coordinates are distinct. Start with four standard GSW encryptions of binary plaintexts. In each choose one top coordinate of a column with corresponding gadget entry q/2. Label its raw, unshifted most significant bit k_j. After adding the plaintext gadget, the transmitted bit c_j is k_j XOR m_j with no carry ambiguity. Flatten all bits of all four GSW matrices exactly as in the outer layer of Abram, Malavolta and Roy, [Tree Encodings I](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/741), Section 4.1. For every index i the programmed CRS matrix is A_i=H_i+c_i Gbar. In each of the four selected matrices let z_j be the least significant bit of its entry in the first top row and the weight-one column for that row. Thus z_j=h_j XOR c_j, with h_j the corresponding unshifted bit of H.

Write z=(L,R) in two-bit halves. Fix a publicly computable function F on two-bit words with two-bit output. It may, for example, have NAND as its first coordinate. Let a be two simulator-known low key bits, let b be the payload, and write b·e₀ for the two-bit word (b,0). Put

    P(L,R) = (R XOR F(L), L),
    m = z XOR P(z) XOR (a XOR b·e₀, a).

These are four ordinary GSW plaintext bits. From the challenge and the unshifted inner tape, compute P(z)=h XOR k XOR (a XOR b·e₀,a); if this is (r₀,r₁), recover L=r₁ and R=r₀ XOR F(r₁). Now compute all native GSW plaintext shifts and outer CRS matrices, with no guesses. The two first-coordinate message bits from the left and right halves XOR to F(L)_0 XOR b. The choice of a nonlinear F does not change the one-inverse property.

For the real coupling condition on the key and the raw inner tape before adding plaintexts. For these fixed values, mapping a native uniformly random outer CRS A to H=A−Delta(c(F,z(A))) is bijective by the explicit inverse. The other coordinates of all the matrices are fixed translations once z is known. Thus conditioning on the raw tape leaves the simulated A uniformly distributed. The unmodified outer LWE response at H matches exactly the native body at A−c_i Gbar. For the ideal game additionally condition on the low key digits. Under the same bijection, A is independent of the uniform raw inner blocks. Conditional on A the entire inner GSW blocks are translations of these independent uniform blocks. Outer uniform response columns are never inspected while solving for z. The joint issued CRS, inner blocks and bodies therefore have the same ideal law for either b, even with nonlinear F. The construction handles this explicit feedback through four designated indicators, without establishing that an arbitrary CRS-dependent master factors through them. It incurs the cost of all published fields.

The general finite-statistic lemma in the Lean node also permits a separate uniform guess for a statistic with K values, with success probability 1/K: for each guess g, applying a permutation p_g before testing g=stat(output) leaves exactly one preimage per output after acceptance. Restricting guesses to a typical subset yields the native output conditioned on its statistic lying in the subset. The Feistel construction above uses its explicit inverse to avoid that loss.

## Ten retained columns

For the following application retain four complete inner top-gadget columns (one per programmed bit), the two complete inner bottom-gadget sentinel columns for m_L,0 and m_R,0, and four complete programmed outer weight-one columns with their responses. Sentinel means the column whose top gadget coordinates are zero and whose last coordinate is q/2. The remaining GSW coordinates do not affect any retained field: the single relevant bit in each outer matrix is programmed by one of the included top columns and the two sentinel columns have independent fresh raw coins. Thus a direct issuer needs 6+4 columns. This projection does not implement arbitrary later GSW circuit evaluation or the rest of the paper's LFE.Hash.

Write the two sentinel headers as B_0,B_1 and their responses as Y_0,Y_1. Their sum satisfies

    Y_0+Y_1 = h + e + (q/2)(F(L)_0 XOR b)   modulo q,
    h = ⟨s,B_0+B_1⟩,  e = e_0+e_1.

The equality uses q/2 times a bit and holds for the arithmetic sum of the two encoded bits. The public decryptor performs this specific homomorphic XOR using just the two sentinel columns. It does not need to multiply two GSW matrices.

Set q=8s' and transmit the value index j=floor((h+s')/(2s')) mod 4 only inside a witness capsule. The representative center is 2s'j. After opening j, test (Y_0+Y_1−2s'j+2s') mod q ≥4s', then XOR with F(L)_0. The centered phase error h−2s'floor((h+s')/(2s')) lies in [−s',s'−1]. If |e|≤s', the shifted zero-code interval is precisely contained in [0,4s'−1]. The one-code case is its antipode. This includes ties at both noise endpoints. The Lean node proves the integer bound, periodicity and exact decoder.

## A structured common NO simulation for the actual decoder

For an affine statement Mv=c over F₂ with n_w witness coordinates, sample two fresh independent binary mask vectors u_0,u_1, of length equal to the number of equations. For each bit j_i of the phase index output R_i=Mᵀu_i and τ_i=j_i XOR ⟨u_i,c⟩. A witness v gives j_i=τ_i XOR ⟨R_i,v⟩, enabling the actual sentinel decryption. The phase h need not be independent of any ciphertext field. The honest issuer knows s and the retained sentinel columns.

On NO there is a v₀ with Mᵀv₀=0 and ⟨v₀,c⟩=1. For either index bit, translating u_i by v₀ preserves R_i and flips its tag. Conditional on the *entire* key, transcript, errors and both phase bits, the two tags are jointly uniform independently of the two R_i. Thus a NO simulator can sample R_i from uniform masks and send two independent fair tags, without knowing the underlying t, the decrypted phase or either index bit. Reusing a mask for both bits would expose their XOR and invalidate this argument.

Now simulate the remaining actual ten-column front by the one-challenge low-digit hybrid above, with separate raw columns from the same source key for all six inner and four outer samples. The ideal front is common to both messages. This gives a common simulator for the whole view (front, both public linear masks and both tags), including arbitrary processing of the disclosed fields. The capsule simulation requires NO; the front simulation alone does not grant the phase to a valid witness.

The same two-bit phase index can be shared across a public disjunction of affine branches: issue one independent pair of masks for every branch and one front overall. On NO each branch is inconsistent, so joint hiding of all tags holds conditional on the same phase and on the entire front. A YES witness supplies a branch index and witness for that branch. If branches are selected by additional conditions, the NO criterion used here requires every *issued* branch to be inconsistent; an unreachable but solvable issued branch would open the capsule. One can also use M·phi(w)=c for a known surjective nonlinear witness permutation phi (for example a Feistel permutation on two 64-bit halves involving a public NAND circuit). Correctness uses v=phi(w); surjectivity ensures that every false statement in this family admits the affine dual vector. These families remain publicly decidable by linear algebra. A general NAND-to-capsule compiler is still missing.

## Concrete accounting and an exact projected packet

In a standard-uniform-header variant let the native dimension be 160, q=256, d=2, source modulus 128 and an independent binary low-digit mask for every secret coordinate. Draw 10 raw source columns and use zero source errors and independent uniform dithers from {0,1}, so the two sentinel errors sum to at most 2, below q/8. The fresh raw native header vectors really are uniform. In this fixed short sample regime no conjecture is needed for the hybrid: for H uniform over (Z/128)^(160 by 10), if H mod 2 has full column rank some 10 by 10 minor is odd, hence invertible over Z/128. Thus Hᵀt is jointly uniform conditional on H. For each nonzero binary vector v of length 10, Pr[Hv=0 mod 2]=2^(-160); a union bound shows TV(real source,ideal source)≤(2^10−1)2^(-160). The total difference of WE acceptance probabilities across payloads on NO is <2^(-149). This classical statistical estimate also bounds quantum postprocessing of the sampled classical view. It applies to the ten-column projection with that error law, not a large-sample hardness estimate. The serialized front is 10·161·8=12,880 fixed-width bits. For n_w=128 the one-branch capsule adds 258 bits, for a total of 13,138 raw payload bits. Framing, random sampling, hash/circuit parsing and machine transition counts are additional.

For comparison, literally serializing all four full GSW matrices and all associated outer CRS matrices/bodies increases size enormously. At dimension 512 and q=2048, word-column width is D=(512+1)·11=5643: the four GSW matrices contain 4D²=127,373,796 bits, the outer layer needs 4D³=718,770,330,828 scalar columns, and the total including the inner columns is 718,770,353,400. Explicitly writing outer headers and responses costs 4D⁴=4,056,020,976,862,404 bits. The short-sample rank argument does not apply there.

A separate fixed-header implementation gives exact statistical privacy and smaller wire cost. Set q=8 and use a uniform secret (s₀,...,s₁₀) over Z/8. Each of the ten base columns has header (x_j,e_j), with independent uniform x_j and fixed, distinct standard basis vectors e_j in the final ten coordinates. The two self bits a come from s₀ alone. All gadget additions used in the top headers and all CRS translations act on the first coordinate only. Sample the sentinel noises independently from {0,1} and {−1,0}; the other errors may be zero. Conditional on s₀ and the entire error tape, additively masking with independent s_j makes all ten raw bodies s₀ x_j+s_j+error_j jointly uniform, independently of x. Equivalently in the native order, condition on all x and s₀ and the noise: all ten final bodies are translations of independent s_j, including the outer corrections. The four top- and four outer-header coordinates acquire the common fair joint law by the Feistel bijection; the two sentinel first coordinates remain independent uniform. Thus the front is exactly bit-independent. On NO the preceding affine capsule replacements remain exact even with the body pads and the phase sampled from those very same s_j. On YES |e_0+e_1|≤1=q/8, so the center decoder is exact. The ten predetermined tails are part of the role specification. Sending the x-coordinate and the scalar body for each column costs 60 bits, or 318 raw bits with the 128-variable single-branch capsule. This is a standalone fixed-header packet with dedicated one-use mask coordinates, not a random-header standard GSW key. Sending more evaluations involving the same mask coordinates requires a new joint-law proof.

Independent checks matched 1,000 complete projected standard-header transcripts at dimension 160 between native issuance and inverse source programming, including actual recovered centers and payloads. Exhaustive checks compared the two-tag NO distributions for all four centers on an inconsistent two-equation instance, both permutations on all small raw coordinates, all 256 functions from two-bit words to two-bit words, and the mod-two rank bound on small complete matrix spaces. The registered role implementation, clock proof and full NAND statement transformation remain open. No new hardness assumption is introduced.
