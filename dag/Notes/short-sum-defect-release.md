### Fixed congruence

Use 4225 groups for the constant, 128 inputs and 4096 NAND outputs. Redirect unavailable parents to constant true as in native evaluation. With both occurrences of a repeated read counted, the fanouts d sum to 8192. A regular gate contributes four items, the last only its three accepting rows. This gives 16640 items. Set L=16384, K=2^4225, H=2^6546, Q=LKH=2^10785 and target t=4225+L(K−1). The radix product D0=∏(1+d) is at most H. The arithmetic lemma uses (d+1)3·2^d ≤ 4·3^d for every nonnegative integer d, then multiplies over the wires. A materialized dense row costs 179462400 bits.

The weight in group j is 1+L(2^j+KW) modulo Q. For each wire count producer output zero with weight d, and count each parent bit equal to one once, all times the wire's mixed-radix place. These contributions sum to W over that item. Subtract LK(D0−1) in the sole constant item. The target high digits d telescope to D0−1. A binary target solution has weight 4225 since 0≤mass≤16640 and mass≡4225 mod 16384. Each marker has multiplicity one: the marker sum uses 4225 powers of two and all 4225 low bits are one. Any carry would reduce their number. With this choice the raw high value lies in [0,2(D0−1)], so its centered value lies strictly between −H and H. Successive radices d+1 force every digit equal to d. Since a producer contributes either d or 0, every parent occurrence agrees with its claimed producer, also for repeated and defaulted parents. Induction across the gates reconstructs the native evaluation and the terminal choice enforces true. Conversely any accepting assignment chooses exactly its native rows. This preserves unused input choices. The Lean components verify the digit arithmetic and native trace uniqueness. The mapping from the flattened Python row generator to these components is specified here and replayed on all 256 one-input two-gate wirings. A single typed end-to-end compiler equivalence remains open.

### Signed solutions and centered gaps

For an integer coefficient vector c, let d(c)=Σ c_i(c_i−1)/2. Each term is a nonnegative integer; d(c)=0 exactly for binary vectors. Write m=4225. At squared norm ≤m+2T with T≤3966, a target solution has mass m. Indeed |mass|≤sq, and mass≡m mod L. The interval [−m−2T,m+2T] contains only one integer in this residue class, since 2m+2T<L. Then sq=m+2d(c), giving 0≤d(c)≤T. Thus radius T=0 enforces binary membership exactly, and every authorized query on a NO has positive defect.

In the full-size NO all gates read wire zero twice. Earlier gates ordinarily use the 11 row, of weight u+2v where u=1+L2^j and v=LK. Replacing it by −00+01+10 yields −u+(u+v)+(u+v)=u+2v. The terminal gate requires this replacement because its 11 row is absent. No gate output is ever read. All 8192 wire units cancel the constant-item translation. Every earlier replacement increases squared norm by two and leaves the target fixed. The Lean ledger and norm identities permit every earlier replacement mask. Each of the 128 unused inputs may freely select either value. In particular there are at least 2^128 binom(4095,d−1) distinct solutions of defect d, for every 1≤d≤4096. Replaying all 4096 prefix masks against the full materialized Python row checks the same congruence.

For finite p≥1, every coordinate of 2c−1 is odd; a nonbinary coordinate has magnitude at least three. The minimum possible p-th power on NO is at least 16639+3^p and the infinity norm is at least 3. The mandatory-only replacement has exactly one −1 and all other entries binary, so it attains both bounds. On YES the corresponding values are 16640 and 1. The finite-p lower bound is formalized for real p≥0. These cosets use circuit-dependent rows with every coefficient congruent to one modulo L.

### A nonlinear complete-table release

Limit queries to solutions of the public target congruence with squared norm at most m+2T and 0≤T≤3966. Over the field of 4099 elements put P(z)=1−∏_{j=1}^T(1−z/j). Encode a field message a by responding to each allowed c with a+rP(d(c)), using the same uniform field element r for every response. Binary solutions recover a, and every allowed query on a NO receives the same a+r. For any two messages a,b, shifting the coin to r+a−b preserves the entire indexed table. This is joint equality even for adaptively or coherently reading this already-defined classical table. The Lean statement uses the allowed subtype, the actual integer defect, its cast into the field and simultaneous equality for all indices. Its NO hypothesis is absence of binary allowed solutions. Interpolation at T distinct nonzero nodes requires degree at least T for any scalar polynomial that is zero at zero and one at every such node; P attains it. All T nodes occur on the fixture above. At T=1 this uses just one quadratic defect statistic, with exact honest recovery.

The model supplies only allowed responses. Exposing a polynomial description with its clear coefficients may expose the secret message; its value at zero is the message. A table with one response per allowed query already needs at least 2^128 entries at T=1, by the unused inputs of the NO fixture. The existence of identical table entries on NO does not give the issuer a succinct representation on arbitrary circuits. A public succinct issuing algorithm whose complete output is simulable under a fixed falsifiable challenge remains open.

### Correlated binary Fourier filtering

Fix k four-choice groups; an outside sign and every error sign may be arbitrarily correlated on finite coins. Average signed observations over each group's singleton or triple choices. If the honest average is at least α and all nonempty triple-pattern averages have magnitude at most η, then (3/2)^k α ≤ 1+[2^k−(3/2)^k]η. Indeed, in one group the quantity (3/2) times the average singleton sign minus (1/2) times the average triple sign has magnitude at most one on all 16 rows. Multiply these identities and average with the outside sign. The sum of the absolute coefficients is 2^k. Uniform pointwise requirements imply the averaged hypotheses. At k=128 and η=2^−128, α<2^−73. For η=0 the bound (2/3)^k is sharp: independently sample a six-outcome uniform coin in each group, flipping one sign on its first four outcomes and none on the last two. The singleton expectation is 2/3, the triple expectation zero. The local moments and the upper bound are formalized. The proof constrains common signed signal in this binary filter model. Circuit-aware correctness and the public distribution of an arbitrary WE issuer require separate analysis.

Initial falsification included complete enumeration of 256 small wirings (64 NO and 192 YES), signed vectors in dimensions one through six, all 16 local sign patterns and direct full-size signed-vector replay. No assumption or registered witness-encryption implementation is supplied here.
