### A stable submixture near the minimax face

Use the oriented signs A,B,C,D, paired AB and CD as in the companion note. If the minimum absolute bias is at least 1/2-delta then all four oriented biases lie within delta of 1/2. Set p_i=P(i negative). Each p_i belongs to [1/4-delta/2,1/4+delta/2]. Write d_1,d_2 for the probabilities both members of each pair are negative. Since each pair product is negative with probability 1/2, d_j≤delta/2. Let T and R be the indicators of an odd number of negatives in the two pairs. The triple zeros give P(i negative,R=1)=p_i/2 for every first-pair member and the symmetric identity for each second-pair member.

Let q_ij be the probability of precisely the two indicated cross-pair negatives; put t=sum q. Then t=(p_A+p_B)/2−2P(both first negative,R=1)≥1/4−d_1. The row sums are p_i/2 minus that common double-negative probability and similarly for columns. Define x=min(q_AC,q_BD), y=min(q_AD,q_BC). By subtracting row/column sums and using |a+b|+|a-b|=2max(|a|,|b|), x+y=t/2−max(|p_A−p_B|,|p_C−p_D|)/4 ≥1/8−delta/2. Exact singleton masses s_i satisfy s_i≥p_i/2−d_1−d_2≥1/8−5delta/4. The all-positive mass p0 is at least P(T=R=0)−d_1−d_2=t−d_1−d_2≥1/4−3delta/2.

Let c=min(x+y,min_i s_i,p0/2). Reduce x,y proportionally to sum c (if c=0 use zeros). The symmetric cross matrix with diagonal x' and off-diagonal y', four identical singleton masses c, and mass 2c at zero form a dominated subdistribution of total alpha=8c. When nonzero its normalization is exactly one of the classified optimal laws. Consequently alpha ≥ max(0,1−10delta). The residual after subtraction, when nonzero, is itself admissible by linearity of all six Fourier restrictions. For independent slots, reveal the optimal/residual choice and only the optimal erased flags. Conditional on this information every local law is still supported in Fourier space on legal addresses. Any difference of complete conditional laws needs a surviving odd global address and hence a satisfying trace. On a gate whose slots are all optimal that trace's row is available with probability at most one half. Grant every gate with any residual slot unconditional availability for this upper bound. Independence and a union bound give full TV ≤ min(1,#SAT times product_g(1−(product_i alpha_gi)/2)). For n=7 and delta=0.01 at every slot, the 4096-gate/128-input upper bound is below 2^-1487. This analysis leaves strongly asymmetric samplers outside its quantitative conclusion.

### Limited gate independence

For a fixed true trace, assume the plane configurations of different gates are t-wise independent, with the simultaneous swap invariance in each block. Each row-availability indicator has mean at most 1/2. Enlarge these indicators to unbiased bits using independent single-gate randomization that never removes a success. For 2k≤t their joint moments through degree 2k match the uniform law. The sum K of the Walsh characters of weights at most k, oriented towards the all-success string, satisfies E[K²]=D=sum_{j=0}^k binom(G,j), whereas its all-success value is D. Thus each trace survives with probability at most 1/D. The full flagged TV is at most #SAT/D. At G=4096, k=31 gives log2(D)>259, so 62-wise independent gate blocks still bound full TV below 2^-131 after the 128-input union bound. The condition is on gate-block joint laws. For efficient pseudorandom flags, existence of an odd admissible W address given the flags is testable by binary linear elimination. A generator computationally indistinguishable from the independent flag ensemble to that particular test can raise the survival probability only by its distinguishing advantage and simulation cost.

### A constructive use of correlated flags

Publicly choose at each gate one of its two parent pins and a Boolean prediction of its value from a shared public seed. Retain the two odd NAND rows having the predicted value on that pin. They form one of the four permitted planes. Hold this plane fixed at every position in the gate; choose independently fresh fair erased symbols in all slots. Reveal the flags together with the ordinary W-perp-padded packet. Conditional on any flags the complete output is uniform on an affine coset: its annihilator is precisely W intersected with the allowed local planes. Every false circuit has identical full laws conditionally and jointly for *arbitrary* distributions on flags independent of the payload. On a satisfied trace matching every prediction, fix any e with pi(e)=1 and assign codewords to each port by multiplying e by its trace bit, with anchor e. This yields an odd compatible W address (each local vector is either zero or the predicted trace row). The witness holder computes its binary dot product, subtracting known sign translations if used, to recover the exact bit.

For example predict all designated parent values by R plus public offsets, with one shared fair bit R. Each individual slot is an optimizer with perfectly correlated erased indices within that slot, since the two opposite planes occur equally often. On a circuit where a supplied witness's designated parent values match one of the two prediction patterns, one draw succeeds with probability at least 1/2. Issue both patterns with independent erased coins and pads, optionally randomly permuted to keep individual slot marginals fair, and select the matching one from the trace; recovery is exact. Every raw gate may point on its first pin to input 1 and on its second to any previous output or other source, giving full-length examples. More generally enumerate a public seed set of size M whose predictions cover the projected traces of interest. Both exact correctness *for every valid witness* and perfect NO privacy follow if every such trace is covered, or if one uses public linear elimination to find any compatible odd address whenever the instance is true. Direct witness readout costs one ambient dot per chosen packet after trace evaluation, with 4nG raw ciphertext bits per packet before descriptors. If the flags are fixed or deducible, an issuer can itself perform the same Gaussian feasibility test; polynomially enumerable exact coverage of all circuits would already give a corresponding SAT decision algorithm. No universal coverage or registered Turing machine cost has been established. Fresh and independently sampled pads across bank entries, and conditioning on the complete public flag list, justify the joint NO statement.
