**Terminal.** Encode the first 32 coordinates of E as canonical 9-bit integers in F257 and its last 24 as canonical 10-bit integers in F769. Denote the fixed 528-bit concatenation by X. There are eight quartic blocks in the first field and six in the second, each nonzero in the target law. The 24 moments S are computed over F769 of the entire 56-coordinate word, using integer representatives for the first 32 entries (not a field embedding). Given a classical auxiliary packet H together with S, the last fields are fresh uniform R of 528 bits and c=b xor <R,X>. The packet through H,S is drawn before R and b. The combined header A=(H,S) can have any correlation with X. A hypothesis about inverting X must reproduce the actual joint law of X and *all* the public fields. Giving a reduction real S as extra input when its assumed challenge never supplied S changes the problem.

**Bit-to-search.** For fixed (X,A) write d(r,c) for the probability that D outputs one on the indicated classical input. Keep a read-only copy of r, initialize its uniform superposition by 528 Hadamards, pick a fair independent classical c, and run a reversible purification of the local D. Apply a phase -1 when its output xor c is one, undo the purification, and apply 528 Hadamards to r. The workspace-clean amplitude at X is the real number
`a(X,A,c)=mean_r (-1)^(<r,X>+c) (1-2d(r,c))`.
Indeed conjugating the output phase by the purification gives the diagonal clean matrix element 1-2d. The last Hadamards supply the character of X. The sign from c can be applied with one extra Z or ignored for measuring a fixed branch; it is included in this signed-mean proof. The arithmetic theorem gives mean_c a = mean_r (d(r,1 xor <r,X>) - d(r,<r,X>)). Average over the joint classical law of (X,A) to obtain the signed acceptance gap Delta between bit worlds. A measurement returning X includes the clean component, so its probability is at least E[a^2] >= Delta^2. The weighted-square lemma applies even to correlated, nonuniform headers, with signed conditional biases of different signs. The oracle invoked coherently is the locally available circuit for D, including inverse computation and ancillas. The search challenger supplies one ordinary classical sample. Neither coherent access to challenge encryption nor a simulation of arbitrary pre-existing quantum side information enters the proof. [Adcock–Cleve](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0108095)

**Fixed input experiment for a positive construction.** Independently of any verifying circuit, sample a uniform x in F257^32, a uniform M in F257^(64 by 32), and 64 small errors eta (e.g. independent centered binomial from two fair bits). The input is the canonical encoding of M and t=Mx+eta; the search output is x. This is a fixed search-LWE *experiment for a conditional implication*, with neither a new registered assumption nor a hardness estimate. In particular we do not posit 128-bit hardness for these parameters. The **honest public view under review** adds the same M,t generated using its actual first 32 coordinates as secret. This changes the preheader relative to the independent-bank experiment of PR 185. It lets us study a genuinely correlated preheader, while the target support, 24 syndromes, 528-bit mask and bit-encoding rule match that terminal. Computational use of an existing registered LWE premise would require identifying its exact secret law, error law, encoding, parameter point, cost and strength.

Write the moment map as U canon(x)+V y, with V indexed by positions 33,...,56 and exponents 0,...,23. This square Vandermonde is invertible because the 24 evaluation points are distinct modulo 769. A reduction samples a fresh uniform S in F769^24 without knowing x and *defines for analysis only* the unique y=V^{-1}(S-U canon(x)). For each x, y is exactly uniform. Thus (x,y) are independent uniform coordinates before any nonzero-block restriction. With G the event that all 14 quartic blocks are nonzero, Pr(G)=(1-257^-4)^8(1-769^-4)^6, approximately 0.999999998149. Conditional on G the two collections of blocks have exactly the required independent uniform nonzero law. M and eta retain their independent laws; t remains correlated with x. This is a permutation of tail candidates, as formalized by syndrome_section. It does not compute the true syndrome of a previously fixed full target: the tail in this embedding is free to be planted. Recovering the first 288 bits of X recovers the entire fixed challenge secret. Neither the derived y nor G needs to be tested by the simulator.

**Complete gate materials for a polynomial NAND subfamily.** Let binary matrix C with 128 columns and rhs d specify Cw=d over F2. This verifier compiles into a NAND circuit: use NANDs to compute XORs and equality bits and then accumulate their conjunction, reserving two final NOTs after padding. A sufficient public budget is 1+4 nnz(C)+3 rows(C)+2<=4096. Keep the circuit generated by this compiler, or provide public C,d and check their correspondence by re-encoding. Its issued gate evaluation uses two rows per witness bit. Sample independent uniform 528-bit words r_j per equation and m_i per witness position, with m_0=0. Set k_i=(C^T r)_i and publish both rows
`T_i,0=m_i xor m_(i-1)`, `T_i,1=T_i,0 xor k_i`, plus `J=m_128 xor (d^T r) xor X`. The public packet consists of the fixed layout/C,d, M,t, both rows at all 128 positions, J, S, R and the terminal bit. No hidden gate table or leakage fields are presumed simulated. Any explicitly public deterministic formatting computed from these values may be included; its runtime and output still count. This packet is a different local issuer for the terminal, not PR 176's two-parent real gate algorithm.

A witness selects one row at every position. Their xor with J is X xor ((Cw+d)^T r), exactly X if it satisfies the equations; it then opens the parity bit. On NO, elementary binary linear algebra gives u with C^T u=0 and d^T u=1, since d lies outside the column space. Map each r_j to r_j xor u_j X while fixing all shared m_i. Every pair of gate rows is unchanged, and d^T r changes by X. This one involution moves the real residual J to its zero-payload form without changing any gate fields. It works after fixing arbitrary (x,y,M,t,S), since all these gate coins are fresh. Thus a search challenger input suffices to simulate the two rows at all gates and J by using the zero word in place of the unknown X. The simulator need not find u, know eta, or individually replace correlated rows by independent random bits. More generally a joint translation must meet every retained field equation simultaneously, as expressed by residual_fiber. For example, publishing the additional scalar d^T r on the same coins reveals X from the zero rows and J, even though that scalar and the gate pads each look locally uniform when d is nonzero. A claimed simulator must re-audit each extra field.

Run Fourier decoding on this simulator even on samples where G fails. Conditional on G its measured success is at least the squared gap Delta_G^2 of the *entire honest NO packet* with nonzero blocks. Outside G success is nonnegative, so unconditioned secret-recovery probability is at least Pr(G) Delta_G^2. Using the gap of the unconditional mixture instead would permit sign cancellation. Comparing the two distributions by total variation before squaring needlessly charges the bad-block mass additively. This avoids a factor 769^24 for guessing S precisely because the fixed search target is the head, the last 24 field coordinates are independent free completion variables, and recovering the head suffices. It does not justify the same shortcut for a fixed challenge requiring recovery of a full unknown tail with an already prescribed correlation.

**Cost and finite sampling.** Generating gate materials costs 528*(rows(C)+128) fair bits, plus additions of 528-bit words across nnz(C) incidences, generation of both rows and the final rhs sum. At 128 witness bits there are 257 gate words, 135696 bits. The 24 syndromes, R and terminal bit add 769 bits, and 64*(32+1)*9=19008 bits encode M,t. Their sum is 155473 bits before the explicit public circuit and framing. These bit counts do not certify the complete machine clock or table description. The simulator can sample S by at most L independent 10-bit trials per coordinate, keeping the first value below 769 and aborting if none exists. Success probability p_S=(1-(255/1024)^L)^24; conditional on success S is exactly uniform, independently of (M,x,eta). For L=80 the failure bound is below 24*4^-80 using at most 19200 fair bits. Aborts can return an arbitrary guess, so the unconditional bound is at least p_S Pr(G) Delta_G^2. Any separately bounded implementations of ideal challenge or honest samplers need their own accounting.

One recovery uses D's purified gate cost G_D once forward and once backward, 1056 Hadamards, at most two output/control phase gates, sampling c and S, reversible formatting, register initialization, storage and the final measurement/unpack. Input preparation, any nonunitary channel dilation, and program description costs belong in the wrapper model; 2G_D+1058 alone is not the total. With retained probability p, target search bound win<=C_red/2^h and cost(A)=C_A, a sufficient transfer condition is 2^k C_red<=2^h p C_A. It then follows that 2^k Delta_G^2<=C_A. For fixed absolute distinguishing gap 2^-128 the inversion threshold is roughly p*2^-256 at the larger decoder cost; that is a probability, not a statement that the fixed search instance has 256 bits of hardness. The Lean costed transfer is conditional on these resource inequalities; the trusted core uses classical machines.

**Checks and boundary.** Exact finite checks covered all 256 deterministic two-bit distinguishers and four hidden words each, plus eight fractional three-bit examples. Elimination over F769 independently verified rank 24 for columns 33 through 56. For C with three row masks 011,110,101, all 64 one-bit gate-mask choices per message were enumerated. The NO rhs 001 gave identical 64-point joint histograms, while the YES rhs 000 gave distinct histograms as expected. The linear-system subfamily is publicly decidable: it verifies a complete correlated-header construction, not a construction for arbitrary NAND circuit-SAT. Extending it to a general hidden gate supplier that reconstructs E on true instances and admits this full-view simulation on false ones, with a sufficiently strong fixed assumption and capped costs, remains open.
