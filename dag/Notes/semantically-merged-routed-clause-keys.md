## From rectangular switches to literal charts

For the NAND of parents x,y with output z put t=1+z in F2. Use switches s,r with t+x=s(x+y) and t=r(1+x+y). For equal parents the first equation forces t=x; for unequal parents the second forces t=0. Conversely take s=x,r=t whenever t=xy. Each selector changes the augmented matrix by one outer product supported on its own equation row. Normalize predecessor references to the registered constant one when necessary. Introduce primary fixing switches and a final unit row. This gives 8320 one-bit switches, 8321 rows, 4224 unknowns and one affine constant column. The first row of each gate together with primary fixing rows forms a fixed unit triangular coefficient minor at every setting.

With just the first switches fixed, each t is one chosen parent, and the gate output is its complement. Routing all 4096 gates therefore produces signed primary literals or constants. Existence of r says the chosen parent is no greater than the other; this is a binary clause, including units or tautologies after simplification. The final output is another unit. Thus every single schedule accepts a 2-CNF relation on the *original* primary variables. A witness computes its own consistent routing by the s=x rule. Squaring a rectangular pencil by dropping checks loses this conclusion; with rows (1,0),(0,s),(0,1+s), different square minors can vanish at different settings while no augmented rank drop occurs. For a square invertible base and rank-one updates U diag(s)Vᵀ, the determinant lemma reduces singularity to I+diag(s)VᵀD_0^-1U. An acyclic directed support has all principal minors of this last matrix equal to one; a shortest directed cycle isolates a cyclic permutation submatrix whose shifted all-ones vector is killed. This square test cannot decide general rectangular consistency.

For a fixed schedule the integer sum of its violated oriented implications and terminal violation is between zero and 4097. Over F4099 it is therefore zero exactly at valid pairs. The feature vector (1,x_i,x_i x_j for i<j) has 8257 entries, independent of schedule. A general key that treats arbitrary linear combinations of the 8257-dimensional schedules as new genuine schedules has no justification from this predicate. We checked the compiler identity on all schedules and assignments in 69 small netlists and sampled 30 full-size traces, the square test on 600 finite pencils, and the energy pairing on 156 full-size schedule/assignment choices.

## Merge exact unions without enumerating all schedules

For two binary-clause systems A,B on n signed Boolean primaries let J be the conjunction of every unit and binary clause entailed by both A and B, also allowing the empty clause. Test entailment of one clause by adjoining the at most two complementary units and running 2-SAT. J is the smallest 2-CNF superset of A union B. To test equality query whether J together with the negation of one clause of A and one of B is satisfiable for any such pair. Each negation adds at most two units. An accepting model witnesses that no single 2-CNF can express this union. If no pair is possible, J equals the union. The equivalence, including empty conjunctions, is formalized as unionGuard. If the union is empty, the explicit empty clause represents it. There are O(n²) possible literal clauses and at most O(|A||B|) further 2-SAT queries. Exhaustive construction of all 4,170 distinct 2-CNF relations on four bits and 5,000 sampled pairs confirmed the merge criterion.

Here is a uniform restricted issuer. Represent both output polarities of a wire by lists of 2-CNF components. Primary literals and constants initialize the lists. For NAND, output one is the union of the two input-zero lists; output zero is the cross-product of the two input-one lists, conjoining the paired systems. Shared input variables retain their identities even at fanout or tied parents. Drop empty or contained components and repeatedly perform the exact pairwise merges above. Keep canonical sorted clauses. This gives a public compiler that does not need to visit the 2^4096 schedules. With a fixed budget B for each polarity per gate, reject this fast mode if it does not find a representation within the budget; an unrecognized circuit still requires a separate correct fallback. A failed greedy merge ordering need not mean no B-sized representation exists. Before simplification a gate has at most B² or 2B candidates. For each merge attempt, a crude canonical bound uses O(n^4) calls to linear-time implication-graph 2-SAT. The number of repetitions, the maximal intermediate list size, and the serialized formula all need charging. One may also define two-input merging directly on a computed schedule tree; exactness uses only the primary relations. This replaces a union of many routing histories by a genuine relation on the witness.

## A 129-bit key for each retained component

For each final component j with implication graph G_j, and each literal l among the 256 signed primaries, include the guard l(x) if G_j has a path from l to its complement. A satisfying assignment to the component zeros every such guard. If G_j is inconsistent, there is a literal mutually reachable with its complement, so the two included guards sum to the constant one over F2. Treat an explicit empty clause as a contradictory pair on any one of the 128 primaries. Independently sample fair r_{j,l} and emit the 129 standard Boolean-affine coefficients of

    P_j(x)=payload + sum_{included l} r_{j,l} l(x).

On a valid witness choose a containing component by the public formulas and evaluate its row. Recovery is exact, so this mode has zero correctness error on recognized circuits, including the 2^-128 target. If an implementation discards all empty components then its final list is simply empty on NO, and the empty ciphertext is the common law. Alternatively retain unsatisfiable components; in every such row toggling the chosen pair of complementary coefficients is a bijection adding one to the scalar polynomial identically. Independent toggles jointly couple *all* emitted coefficient bytes for either payload. This latter proof allows issuance even when empty components were not pruned and shows explicitly which joint sampler is safe. If the graph/compiler is transmitted, it is deterministic from the public circuit. At B output components the raw key charge is 129B bits plus framing and at most 256B fair bits; reconstructing the covering list and finding the witness's row are part of actual costs. Computing mutual reachability and implementing the registered machines are additional obligations. Directly deciding satisfiability of each explicit 2-CNF is also possible. The issuer yields a mathematical bounded-class construction, not a general cryptographic advantage over that decision procedure.

A fixed schedule's unsatisfiability even gives a low-degree static identity without preprocessing its transitive closure. If a and its negation are mutually reachable, pick two simple paths of length at most 255. For endpoints u,v set I(u,v)=u(1-v). For every intermediate Boolean literal h, I(u,v)=(1-v)I(u,h)+uI(h,v). Balanced splitting gives a sum of violated edge polynomials with coefficients of degree at most eight along each path. At the two opposite endpoints I(a,1-a)+I(1-a,a)=1. This is a degree bound in the *primaries* for an individual graph. It does not provide one low-degree or short common certificate in the schedule bits.

## Why separated clear masks do not complete the issuer

One fixed false instance has a public schedule slice with energy E(x,s)=1+popcount(x AND s). Generate zero by NAND(true,true), use a comparison of x_i and zero selected with s_i, then compare zero with the preceding result choosing zero. The first comparison incurs x_i s_i and the second is always legal. Conjoin the resulting ones, pad preserving one, then negate for a terminal violation of one. This uses a connected 4096-gate netlist with all primaries present. If a public evaluator returns P=b+E F in F4099 with rank(F)≤k in the x/s matrix across each complete draw, query the rectangle indexed by a Boolean support down-set of size 2k+1. The entrywise inverse D=1/E there has full rank: its subset-incidence factorization has diagonal entries (-1)^|T|/(1+|T|) by binomial inversion, all nonzero. Dividing (P-b') entrywise by E gives rank at most k for the correct bit and strictly greater than k for the other. This distinguishes every sample in (2k+1)^2 public evaluations when that many supports exist. The finite replay checked both messages for ranks one through eleven. In particular a separable clear monomial tag is not a substitute for joint hidden issuance. This conclusion does not cover outputs retained under encryption or high-rank masks with short descriptions. No new hardness assumption is used.
