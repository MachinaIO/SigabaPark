Use binary characters chi_v(z)=(-1)^(v dot z), Fourier coefficients E_uniform f(z)chi_v(z), and total variation equal to half the L1 distance. All raw masks come from the same distribution mu on four binary entries per gate slot, allowing arbitrary joint dependence. Its transform must vanish whenever even one local frequency (a,x,y,z) violates xy=a(a+z). The encryptor adds a uniform W-perpendicular pad and a shift multiplying the common anchor functional by the payload bit. W encodes the public wiring: each port is a word of a binary linear code E, equally labelled ports share only a linear secret pi, all anchors have equal secret, and the constant one and last output take that secret. A fixed linear functional L reconstructs pi(x)pi(y) from coordinate products of x,y. Thus a globally supported frequency with odd anchor specifies an actual true trace, by applying L at every gate. This includes the length-one scalar code. NO laws coincide by Fourier inversion.

For every 128-bit string a, put all 128 gates NAND(x_i,x_i) first. Select one literal from each pair, positive when a_i=1; a serial AND of the 128 selected literals takes 254 NAND gates. Pad to 4096 with 3714 unused NAND(1,1) gates before the final gate, renumbering references. The unique witness is a and all suffix gate rows at that witness are constant across the family. Any two distinct witnesses differ at a shared prefix gate between rows (1,0,0,1) and (1,1,1,0), whose sum (0,1,1,1) is forbidden. For the scalar code the odd part of the full density on the wiring quotient has only one frequency v_a with coefficient delta_a=E_mu chi_v_a. Direct inversion gives TV_a=|delta_a|. These characters are orthogonal in L2(mu), so summing TV_a squared gives at most 1.

For coded ports take the sign of the difference between the two full laws, with zero at ties. This is bounded by one, invariant under W-perp and odd under the message-shift translation. Its expectation under the base law is TV_a: the shifted expectation is its negative, and their difference is twice TV. Let H be the subspace of address words with zero secret at every port; it is independent of wiring and contained in W. Project the test onto the coset v+H of lifts of the unique accepting semantic trace. This projection averages translates by h in H-perp weighted by chi_v(h), so has sup norm at most one (signedContraction). Expectation under mu is preserved because all other odd wired frequencies have incorrect semantics somewhere, hence zero coefficient. For a≠b every pair of frequencies from the two projected tests has an invalid semantic sum at a differing prefix gate. If all code coordinates there were homogeneous-legal, multiplication reconstruction would make their semantic sum legal. Thus the product of the two tests has expectation zero. L2(mu) norm is at most one. Discard norm-zero terms (their means vanish) and normalize the rest; apply Bessel to the unit constant function to get sum_a TV_a²≤1 (weightedBessel). Consequently some instance has TV≤2^-64, independent of the computation available to the receiver. Conversely this is a necessary compiler-dependence result about one noise law and its support restriction; statement-independent hardness experiments do not require a statement-independent honest sampler.

For a menu of K source laws fixed before choosing the target, the squared sum is at most K even if the statement uses a mixture: TV≤the maximum over menu entries. For public statement-dependent linear routing, the argument survives if after pullback the projected cross-frequencies still have a sum forbidden by the common source, for example identical gatewise pullbacks on the distinguishing prefix preserving the forbidden set, and each statement's projection/odd-support argument holds. General circuit-dependent routing can instead align opposite prefix rows and need not obey this condition. Independent copies of the same selected packet increase TV by at most their number R. These bounds concern honest marginals or complete public menus/flags with the same selection law in both worlds; publishing hidden conditionings of a noise source requires a new joint proof.

The scalar upper bound is attained simultaneously. The trace family is v_a=v_0+sum_i a_i r_i where the r_i are independent and v_0 is independent modulo their span. From a uniform raw mask choose a correction bit so v_0 dot z equals the sum of 64 disjoint binary products of the readings r_i dot z. The correction direction annihilates every r_i and pairs to one with v_0. This distribution has exactly one nonconstant affine Fourier chart (the v_a), each coefficient of magnitude 2^-64 by the four-term Walsh transform of a bit product. All those raw addresses are locally legal, even at instances to which they do not wire. Direct enumeration on two inputs and four gates gives full TV=1/2 at each of four targets and squared sum exactly one. An adaptive issuer can align the literal polarities on this particular family and program the known trace exactly. This certifies sharpness of the shared-law statement, not general correctness.
