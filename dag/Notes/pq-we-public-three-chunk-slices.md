### Shares for an entire fixed false statement

Suppose the public predicate factors as u(x) B(z) v(y) over F₂ for three *independent* witness chunks. Row vectors have width p and column vectors width q. The statement, all feature maps and the current public mode are independent of β. The adversary gets every transmitted record at once. Correctness only requires returning β on a satisfying witness.

If the middle matrix is the identity and p=q, sample a uniform matrix A and issue each row uA and each column (A+βI)v. The two publicly computed cross products sum to βuv. For a fixed false statement U V=0 (capital letters stack all allowed endpoints). Let π project onto the span of the columns. Translating A by π changes neither the left records (Uπ=0) nor the right records after flipping β (πV=V). This permutation of the uniform coin space gives perfect equality of transcript laws under arbitrarily many, even quantum, subsequent evaluations. If more row/coefficient or column/coefficient records of the same form are emitted, use their **actual** row and column spans: privacy requires all their cross products to vanish. Necessity follows by forming any disclosed pairing, including a pairing that does not correspond to an allowed raw witness. Public coefficients may therefore be disclosed on the basis of an attainable-span certificate. A free extra vector is unsafe merely because its slot is not selected in honest decoding.

Let S be the linear span of the attainable middle matrices and B₁,…,B_s an actual basis. Use independent uniform p×q masks A_l, publish uA_l and (A_l+βB_l)v for every disclosed endpoint and l, and open by adding the two cross products per selected basis coefficient of B(z). On NO each u B_l v vanishes because a basis element is a linear combination of attainable middle matrices. The simultaneous tape translation is A_l↦A_l+B_lπ. The same π acts on the column span of **all** disclosed records, with an independent tape for each l. More generally, the Lean shift criterion says translations exist exactly when all disclosed pairings u B_l v vanish. It covers extra slices outside S only if their additional zero pairings are independently justified. Sharing the same A across slices can expose the bit through differences of columns. For example, take row zero, column one and scalar slices zero and one: on NO the difference between the two column shares equals β when a single mask is reused.

For R disclosed row vectors and M disclosed columns, the literal fields occupy s(Rq+Mp) bits and the setup reads spq coins. With a public factorization B_l=P_l Q_l of rank r_l, use independent square masks on uP_l and Q_l v. Total fields become (R+M)∑r_l and coins ∑r_l². Computing factorizations, recovering slice coordinates and all public local features costs time. A chosen basis minimizing total rank may be found by ordering all nonzero elements of S by matrix rank and using linear independence as the greedy matroid test; this can enumerate 2^s elements. No minimization is needed for security.

### When one mask suffices across all middle values

For square d×d middle matrices put A=the unital algebra generated by S and C=its commutant. Sample T uniformly from C by taking a random linear combination of a fixed basis. Issue uT and (T+βI)v. For each M_z evaluate (uT)M_z v + uM_z((T+βI)v). Since T commutes with every M_z this equals βuM_z v. The records have d(R+M) bits and need dim(C) coins. A basis computation solves linear homogeneous constraints TM_z=M_zT, or only the s basis equations, in d² unknowns. Simple elimination costs O(s d⁶) scalar operations and O(s d⁴) bits for the unreduced system. This conservative count includes no raw-circuit parsing or feature extraction.

Write X for the column span and Z for the common row kernel. On NO, SX⊆Z. The two framed transcript laws are the image of a uniform linear map plus one of two offsets; their supports coincide or are disjoint. They coincide exactly when there is P∈C with P|X=id and im(P)⊆Z. Indeed these two equations give the required translation T↦T+P. Conversely, if a view in one support also appears in the other, the difference of its two preimages is such a P.

A universal criterion identifies **exactly which fixed middle spaces** work for *every* pair of endpoint spaces. Both conditions below are necessary and sufficient in finite dimension.

1. For every vector v, S v=A v. Equivalently SX=AX for every subspace X by taking sums of cyclic images.
2. Every A-invariant subspace W has an A-invariant complement, equivalently the natural A-module is semisimple.

For necessity choose Z=SX. A commuting P fixing X fixes AX, so AX⊆im(P)⊆SX. Next take X=Z=W invariant. An admissible P has image W and is identity on W; its kernel is an invariant complement. Every ambient subspace Z can be specified as a common row kernel by duality. For sufficiency let W=AX=SX⊆Z and choose an invariant complementary subspace. Projection onto W along that complement commutes with A and gives the simultaneous shift. The Lean theorem proves the reach/retraction equivalence under explicit closure and generator-fixing hypotheses; the preceding paragraph instantiates those hypotheses by ordinary module facts. Semisimplicity of the full matrix algebra as an abstract ring alone would not replace a check on the actual slice space. It is enough that S is cyclically complete; S need not equal A.

A proper example in dimension two has S₀=span(I,C,E₁₁), where C has rows 01 and 11. The operators I,C generate the four-element field, so S₀v is the whole plane for v≠0. Including E₁₁ generates all of Mat₂(F₂); S₀ has dimension three and its commutant consists just of zero and identity. Take S=S₀⊕S₀ on two disjoint two-dimensional blocks for a nontrivial NO example with nonzero row and column features. Its two independent scalar block masks suffice. All 67 binary matrix subspaces in dimension two and all 25 pairs of endpoint subspaces were checked by enumeration. Thirteen of the 67 satisfy universal privacy; the two-condition test agreed on every case.

The literal two-interface telescope with independent T₁,T₂ would issue u(T₁+βI), all bridges T₁M_z+M_zT₂, and T₂v. On a false instance the change of variables would have to satisfy uP₁=u, P₂v=0, P₁M_z=M_zP₂. Take u=e₁ᵀ,v=e₂, and M_z in {I,E₂₁}. Both triple products vanish. Identity forces P₁=P₂, and the endpoint equations force rows (1,0),(p,0) for P₁, which fails to commute with E₂₁. Exhausting all 16 potential common shifts gives zero solutions. The binary affine supports are therefore disjoint. The independent basis-slice construction above remains available here.

### Even-parity diagonal selectors

There are positive cases outside the universal commutant criterion. Suppose the linear span of the attainable diagonals on d≥2 coordinates is precisely the even-weight code. For a false circuit, all pairs of attainable endpoint supports intersect in either the empty or the entire block (the orthogonal complement of that code). Sample independent uniform bits a_i,b and put c=β+b. Publish for each endpoint u the d bits u_i a_i and one bit c·[u is nonconstant]. Publish for v the bits v_i(a_i+b) and c·[v is nonconstant]. An accepting triple has a common 1 coordinate and at least one nonconstant endpoint: an all-one overlap pairs to zero with every allowed even selector. Recover b from that coordinate and c from a nonconstant endpoint.

On NO, if all intersections are empty, each coordinate has an identically zero left or right endpoint. Flip b, keep c fixed, and flip a_i on coordinates having a reachable right 1. This preserves every public record after changing β. Otherwise some pair has full intersection: both sides contain a full endpoint, and hence every endpoint is empty or full. Keep b and all a_i unchanged while flipping c. The extra flag components all vanish. These are bijections on the complete uniform tape. The Boolean support dichotomy is Lean-verified. This costs (d+1)(R+M) bits and d+1 coins. For d=3 it ties the (R+M)·2(d−1) minimum basis-rank size; for d>3 it improves that count (an even nonzero diagonal has rank at least two, and the even code has a weight-two basis). An exhaustive comparison for d=3, two unordered endpoint slots per side, checked 1,296 inputs and 159 NO pairs of complete distributions.

For local predicates of the endpoint chunks one can eliminate enumeration of their assignments. Encrypt a_i under the smaller statement u_i(x)=1 and a_i+b under v_i(y)=1. Add two local encryptions of c under the respective nonconstancy predicates. All 2d+2 encryptions use fresh coins. An accepting witness decrypts three. In the empty-intersection case each coordinate has at least one false endpoint predicate; the same mask translation changes at most one false-side plaintext per coordinate and no true-side plaintext. In the constant case both nonconstancy statements are false and just those two encryptions switch. A hybrid therefore costs at most d or two ordinary false-statement changes respectively. A fixed-statement reduction need not encode an entire table of false sides. For a coordinate pair with local laws L₀,L₁ and R₀,R₁, the signed bit-zero minus bit-one distribution is (L₀−L₁)⊗(R₀−R₁)/2. A wrapper can insert its challenge on either side, encrypt a uniform bit on the other side, and complement its output when that sampled bit is one; the signed acceptance gap is identical on either choice. Apply the same identity at the outer split into b and c. Telescope the d coordinate-pair changes or the two flag changes in canonical order. In the disjoint case at least one of the two possible local challenges per coordinate is false, and in the constant case both flag challenges are false. Thus among 2d+2 canonical wrappers there exists a valid false-statement wrapper with gap at least the original absolute gap divided by max(d,2). Each wrapper is generated from the public instance, a side and an index; the argument does not provide a single efficient algorithm selecting the successful false wrapper. Charge the full simulation of companion encryptions, index/instance description and squared-gap loss. This conditional route needs an actual small WE for the smaller predicates and quantitative reduction, or direct endpoint SAT algorithms. If all endpoint reachability flags are cheap, their Cartesian-product structure already decides SAT: for each possible pair of differing overlap coordinates, check the corresponding left and right local bit patterns, including the zero side of a failed product. Distinct graph-selector components can use independent splits of β and local coins; an accepting witness opens a component contributing odd parity. Independent blocks require their full direct sum of middle selector spaces, not just surjectivity of each projection separately.

### Sparse public coefficients, a gate-list control, and actual costs

If a feature map has a sparse squarefree ANF f(x)=∑_{T⊆supp(x)}f_T, the span of its attained values equals the span of its true coefficient vectors. Evaluation proves one inclusion; subtract proper-subset contributions from f(1_T) to recover f_T inductively. This works with vector or matrix values. The Lean proof establishes the equivalence for every target subspace. Merge equal monomial supports modulo two before extracting middle directions. Selecting independently listed coordinates from a larger ambient matrix space can violate NO privacy. By applying this fact separately to each disjoint chunk, a basis of its coefficients can replace an exponential endpoint list. The decoder computes basis coordinates for its public u,v,M and combines records linearly. If monomial lists are available, the image bases and matrices U_* B_l V_* can also be computed publicly. Testing whether all of those matrices vanish decides SAT outright. A single true tensor entry implies some true raw witness by linearity and attainment of the spans. Direct decision then sends one bit β AND SAT, with perfect NO privacy. This is often less work than generating the masks.

For g raw NAND gates and a cap H on intermediate ANF support, an exact capped ANF pass replaces a gate by 1+fg in the squarefree algebra (monomial multiplication is bit-mask union). A gate can form H² products before cancellation. Sorting and parity-counting canonical 128-bit masks takes O(128 H² log(H+1)) indexed bit/comparison work per gate apart from operand lookup. Count peak candidate storage O(128 H²) bits, or stream candidates through a slower dictionary; do not invoke this polynomial bound after the cap has been exceeded. When starting with sparse local maps of lengths h_u,h_m,h_v, reading/writing dense coefficient vectors already costs O(h_u p+h_m pq+h_v q) bits besides supports; eliminate h vectors in width D by O(hD²) scalar XOR/test operations for a straightforward streaming basis. Full arbitrary NAND lists can create exponentially many monomials. Even OR of 128 bits has all nonempty monomials in its unreduced ANF, although it has a trivial direct solver and small alternate representations.

A registered-size control has 40 left, 40 middle and 48 right bits. The left feature uses the conjunctions of disjoint groups of 20 bits, occupying the first two-dimensional block. Six middle coefficients are conjunctions of the six residue classes modulo six within the middle chunk. They independently span S₀⊕S₀. The right feature uses two conjunctions on disjoint groups of 24 bits, in the other block (NO) or first block (YES). Compile each conjunction by two NANDs per binary AND, XOR by its four-NAND macro, and the eight blockwise products u_iM_ijv_j by two successive ANDs; sum those products by XOR. These canonical connector and chunk-local macros are checkable on the raw indices and can be recovered by rebuilding the fixed template. The raw builder used 313 gates in both cases, then inserted unused NAND(true,true) gates immediately before the final gate to reach 4,096. All 1,024 feature words are attainable. The registered NAND evaluator on their 128-bit preimages agreed in both cases: zero accepting feature words for NO and 288 for YES. With four distinct attainable vectors on each side the full masks need 32 bits (4×8) or, after coefficient basis compression to two vectors per side, 16 bits. There are two setup coins. For the four-vector lists the complete NO frames had four atoms on each side with identical multiplicities; the YES supports for β=0/1 had two atoms each and were disjoint. Separately, exhaustive two-slice tests with independent masks covered 256 small inputs, 148 NO, comparing complete two-slice joint frames. Direct span tests readily decide these controls.

The registered Dec receives (statement, witness, ciphertext) and the fixed parameters. The statement-dependent mode, local programs, basis order and address convention must be deterministic from φ or serialized with headers; this control can use fixed template matching. Both machines must handle the encoded unary gate indices and self/forward reads (default true) and pay sequential access to arrays and ciphertext. The library's gate encoding bound is 4096·(6·4224+3)=103,821,312 bits before the outer argument frames. Input parsing, writing the shares, transforming coefficients, matrix arithmetic, lookup and all basis recomputation on the Dec side are separate costs. The registered objective adds worst-case times of Enc and Dec and both transition-table description lengths. No compiled machine, general sub-2^32 clock, or universal 2^100 construction follows from these bit counts. A public proof of the factorization for arbitrary raw circuits and a bounded fallback when recognition fails are open.
