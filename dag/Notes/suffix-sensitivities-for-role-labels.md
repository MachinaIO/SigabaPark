### A universal identity with local frontiers

Number the gates in a public topological order after replacing forward/unavailable reads by the true constant. For an arbitrary consistent right assignment v, hold its primary bits fixed, supply its first i claimed gate outputs, and evaluate the remaining suffix honestly. Denote the final bit by A_i(v). Thus A_0=C(primary), A_g=1 under the final pin, and this definition does not assume the intermediate wires are correct. For a gate with supplied output z_i and parents a_i,b_i let e_i=z_i-(1-a_i b_i). Run the suffix twice after fixing the i-th output to one or zero and the earlier values to their supplied bits. Let d_i be the first output minus the second, in {-1,0,1}. A Boolean argument has just two possible values, so the following holds over the integers and hence modulo both 3 and 4:

    A_i - A_(i-1) = e_i d_i,           sum_i e_i d_i = 1-C(primary).

If the suffix does not use the changed gate output, its d_i is zero (except at the final output). To compute d_i one needs only those primary/prior wire values still read by the suffix besides the current gate's output. For each such wire choose a fixed occurrence in the raw address. Its bit is available to the left evaluator too, and on the right it equals the well-defined wire value. This choice and the topological order may depend on the public statement but not the payload. The final gate has d_g=1.

Use these live bits as the gate's context in the companion issuer. The certificate coefficient for a row/context is e_i d_i(context). Every valid left gate row has e_i=0, regardless of the supplied context. On a NO, the sum over right rows is one for *every* consistent trace. This proves exact equality of the full public key distributions on any circuit represented with maximum live boundary w. The raw left and right table bounds are respectively 4g 2^w and 1+g(2^(w+3)-1) coefficients. Issuance uses all three truth-row bits plus that gate's own context when constructing the right polynomial. The coefficients d_i need not be computed by the actual issuer: the existence of the shift proves equality for its independent uniform table masks. Lookup, compilation and honest clocks are separate; the bound is exponential in w without a uniform small-width schedule. The certificate is an additive sensitivity identity, not a partition by the first wrong gate. A public checker can still decide the bounded-width language by standard frontier computation.

A more compact conceptual context is the trit d_i itself, computable on the selected raw representative by two suffix evaluations, with total naive O(g²) NAND work per address. The left table then has only 12g trits. On right inputs the resulting 24g features f_(i,a,d) are indicators that the gate has a specified row and the calculated trit equals d. They can be highly nonlinear in the primary and intermediate bits, so collecting monomials can be expensive. A sparse list of factorized program coefficients does not automatically erase linear dependencies that exist only on legal input assignments.

There is a precise reduction of the remaining representation question. Let lambda_(i,a,d)=e_i(a)d. Choose any slot with a unit lambda (one can take an invalid row and d=1 in the full table). Write the uniform masks r=r'+t lambda with r' zero at that slot and t independent uniform; divide its original coordinate by the unit to obtain t. All left-valid slots have lambda zero. The right response decomposes into

    <r',f(v)> + (payload+t) - t*C(primary(v)).

Issuing all coefficients of r' is now harmless because they are independent of t and payload. Only the last term requires a representation whose public bytes hide t when the original circuit is identically zero. For instance, a canonical table of coefficients of t*C over just the 128 primary Booleans works information-theoretically, but may require 2^128 entries or that many operations to compile. An efficient such canonicalizer could also issue payload*C directly. This reduction from 24g feature functions on all consistent trace wires to one original primary-input function does not solve arbitrary witness encryption.

Canonicalizing by a list of actual right evaluations has a stronger requirement. On augmented feature vectors (1,f(v)) the linear functional 1-<lambda,f(v)> equals C(primary(v)). If a bank of sampled rows comes only from nonsatisfying primary inputs then all its linear combinations lie in the kernel of that functional. It cannot represent the augmented row for any true witness, where the value is one. An issuer that successfully spans that row must therefore already have sampled a satisfying primary input, which a public evaluator could recover by checking its samples. This applies even if completion was required with probability only 1-epsilon for an individual witness. Virtual generators are not necessarily actual rows. However multiplicative evaluation into commuting idempotent matrices on a vector space of dimension k splits into at most k nonzero joint Boolean eigenspaces by repeated image/kernel decomposition: each tuple of eigenvalues defines an actual assignment. A multiplicative evaluation detecting the nonzero primary function still identifies a satisfying joint eigenspace, with costs for its representation and decomposition. General linear but nonmultiplicative functionals are outside this last observation.

### Why a bounded number of raw context wires cannot suffice in the asymptotic trit model

Use the odd-vertex Tseitin parity systems on bounded-degree expander graphs from Theorem 10 of Buss, Grigoriev, Impagliazzo and Pitassi. Each edge carries a sign variable y_e and each vertex v requires the incident product to equal -1. Over F3 the equations y_e²=1 and 1+product_(e incident to v) y_e=0 have no polynomial-calculus refutation of degree below epsilon*n/8 for their n-vertex graphs with expansion epsilon. The following argument transfers their theorem to the specific local mask issuer. [Authors' journal paper, section 6](https://mathweb.ucsd.edu/~sbuss/ResearchWeb/lineargaps/journalfinal.pdf)

Build constant-size NAND parity/check gadgets for each vertex (size depending on maximum degree r) and a NAND aggregation tree accepting just when all checks pass. This is an asymptotic family with O(nr) primary/gate resources at fixed r, not a numerical lower bound at the registered 128-input size. Suppose the F3 issuer had a certificate with at most t context *raw wire bits* at each gate. Substitute edge signs for the primary bits by the affine sign-to-Boolean conversion. In each vertex gadget choose its actual intermediate wire values but force its local check output to one; evaluate the whole aggregation tree on ones. This gives a well-defined right assignment including the final pin on every sign vector. Every substituted wire value is a Boolean function of at most r signs and hence a multilinear polynomial of degree at most r. All non-final vertex gates and all aggregation gates are locally legal, so contribute zero. Only the last gate of each vertex contributes a polynomial P_v. Because each table depends on at most t+3 bits, its substituted multilinear degree is at most r(t+3), and P_v vanishes whenever that vertex's parity passes.

On the sign cube, P_v equals P_v*(1+product_v)/2. A full-key certificate sums the P_v to one. Multiply the vertex equations by the monomials of P_v/2 and sum; reducing powers with y_e²-1 uses no increase in degree. The sign-cube multilinear normal form of the result is the constant one (the multilinear representation on a binary cube over F3 is unique). This produces a polynomial-calculus refutation of degree at most r(t+4), so necessarily t ≥ epsilon*n/(8r)-4 on this family. The proof allows the chosen raw context wires to be arbitrarily far away and accounts for the auxiliary NAND gate variables of the encoding. It is an inference from the cited theorem for this F3 construction only. The three-valued nonlinear suffix contexts have no bounded-raw-degree hypothesis, and Z/4, different program representations, preprocessing and general witness encryption are outside this lower bound.

The exact finite replay checked the telescoping sum on 13,192 pinned consistent assignments over two- and four-gate circuits including forward references. A separate full-coefficient comparison verified the three-gate one-context repair over both Z/3 and Z/4. For the single-gate density experiment over all 64 binary 3-by-2 headers, left-image hit counts 0,1,2,3,4 occurred respectively 18,33,9,3,1 times, with the predicted mean and variance.

### A concrete 128-input NO needs at least six raw context bits and admits seven

Start z_0=NAND(true,true). For i=1,...,128 compute u_i=NAND(z_(i-1),x_i), z_i=NAND(u_i,u_i), so the output z_128 is always zero. Pin it to one in the right opening. These are 257 NAND gates; insert 3839 ignored constant NANDs before the active chain and renumber for exactly 4096. Assign those dummy gates their correct values throughout. To lower-bound the number of extra raw wire bits in a single table per gate, substitute x_i=z_i and u_i=1-z_i with z_0=0 and z_128=1 into the allegedly universal right identity. This substitution need not be a legal *left* trace. Every second NAND now is valid. The first NAND of each pair is invalid only on its single pattern (0,1,0) when z_(i-1)=0 and z_i=1. Every extra raw context becomes a constant, one Boolean variable z_j, or its complement. A certificate with at most t context bits would therefore imply the multilinear identity

    1 = sum_i P_i(z) z_i (1-z_(i-1)),            degree(P_i) ≤ t.

This implication uses neither positivity of coefficients nor locality of the extra bits. Work in any nonzero commutative coefficient ring, including Z/3 and Z/4, in the free module of multilinear Boolean monomials indexed by subsets of the 127 internal z's; uniqueness of the Boolean multilinear expansion follows from triangular subset evaluation with unit diagonal. At degree d=t+2 build an undirected graph of subsets of size at most d. Allow a toggle of j if j+1 is present, or of 127 with no prerequisite. Multiplication of an interior generator by a multilinear monomial yields zero or a difference of two adjacent monomials in this graph. The last generator 1-z_127 toggles 127; the first generator z_1 leaves a monomial containing 1. Hence if the connected component of the empty set contains no monomial with 1, its indicator defines a linear functional taking value one on the constant and zero on every term on the right. This rules out the putative identity.

For reproducibility the following complete finite traversal numbers bit positions in reverse, so the prerequisite-free toggle is the low bit and reaching z_1 would produce bit length 127.

    seen = {0}
    todo = [0]
    for state in todo:
        candidates = ((state << 1) | 1) & ((1 << 127) - 1)
        while candidates:
            p = candidates & -candidates
            candidates -= p
            target = state ^ p
            if target.bit_count() <= d and target not in seen:
                seen.add(target)
                todo.append(target)

At d=4,5,6 the numbers of reached states were 373, 15193, 1626196; the largest bit lengths were 15,31,63. Thus at d=6 the functional exists, and t≤4 cannot suffice on this *registered-size* NO, even if every gate may choose different distant context wires. This is a finite exhaustive computation separate from the Lean statements and from the asymptotic field result.

A matching-style constructive upper bound is eight extra bits. On raw consistent values let I_ab=(1-z_a)z_b. For any midpoint c,

    I_ab = z_b I_ac + (1-z_a) I_cb.

Split the interval [0,128] into halves recursively. Every consecutive factor I_(i-1,i) receives just one product of at most seven ancestor endpoint bits or their complements. With arbitrary right variables (without the lower-bound substitution), put e1=u_i-(1-z_(i-1)x_i) and e2=z_i-(1-u_i). Then I_(i-1,i)=(1-z_(i-1))(e1+e2). The first residual uses its own parent bit; the second may use that bit as an eighth context. Both residuals vanish whenever their own NAND row is valid (for the second gate define the value as zero on unequal-parent rows not arising on the right). The initial-gate residual is z_0 on its actual pinned-parent pattern. Adding it to the decomposition of I_0,128 gives one because z_128=1. These shifts yield identical complete key distributions. These directly checked and constructed bounds can be tightened by tracking the endpoints.

For t=5 use the component of the empty set at degree d=7. Source terms P_1 z_1 have degree at most six, one below d. There cannot be a configuration containing the farthest site 127 with at most six pebbles in this component: with a seventh free pebble one could reach site 128 on the infinite line. This contradicts the maximum reachable site 2^7-1 for the unrestricted reversible line game under the same toggle rule. The component may contain a seven-element monomial containing z_1, but no source term can have that size. Thus the component indicator still kills every generator product, showing that at least six extra raw context bits are necessary. The finite d=6 enumeration above did not enumerate d=7. [Li, Tromp and Vitányi, Corollary 2](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~paulv/papers/pebbles.pdf)

For the improved upper bound replace only the starting value *in the interval recursion* by a virtual zero while leaving the actual source wire z_0 free. Both outer endpoints are now fixed, so both midpoint coefficients at the first split equal one. Each leaf has at most six remaining ancestor factors. For i>1 use the previous two residuals with at most one additional raw context bit. At the first leaf the virtual implication is z_1 itself. The identity z_1=e1+e2+z_0 x_1 holds with the actual source value, so place the six-factor leaf multiplier on the two first-stage residuals and its product with x_1 on the invalid source-gate row. Every gate needs at most seven extra bits. Thus the optimum maximum raw context count for this instance is either six or seven under the stated table format. With the ignored dummy-gate masks set publicly to zero, a coarse seven-context bound for the 257 active gates is 131584 left trits and 262912 right trits, at most 788992 bits at two bits per symbol. This excludes the hash header, serialization of indices, parsing, evaluator clocks and random mask generation.

For comparison to the one-unknown-parent hyperedge in PR 231, false input ensures at least one local nonzero residual and therefore one unavailable slope for a fixed right trace. That property alone does not yield a common linear functional of the publicly released key bytes for every right input. The sensitivity coefficients d_i(v) specify which linear combination works on each trace. A canonical or otherwise proven private serialization of these variable choices remains necessary before they can serve as a compact global key.
