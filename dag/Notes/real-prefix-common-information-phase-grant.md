## Capsule at the registered decision law

Take RingLWE.decision with N=1024, q=2^19, m=514, uniform secret and bounded(1) errors in the negacyclic ring. RingPrefixArithmetic.params has the registry's parameter type. Evaluating the installed registry's RingLWE.H for this exact tuple gives 163. This is an estimator output; no concrete advantage follows without charging the reduction. The core realLaw_bind_wordDist and idealLaw_bind_wordDist identify the encoded challenges with ring-level testing. For independent challenge rows (Aᵢ,Yᵢ) with the one shared secret, discard all but two. On a unit pivot A₀ set a=−A₁/A₀, y=Y₁+aY₀. On real inputs y=a e₀+e₁, including when the original secret is zero; a is uniform independently of errors. On ideal inputs (A₁,Y₁)↦(a,y) is bijective for every fixed pivot and body. The cancellation and both uniform bijections are formalized. Modulo two, X^1024+1=(X+1)^1024; reduction mod 2 reflects units since (2) is nilpotent. A₀ is therefore a unit exactly when A₀(1) is odd, with probability 1/2. Output an independent decision on abort. One pivot transfers half the normal-form gap; k independent candidates and one reserved final row retain 1−2^(-k), provided pivot selection only inspects their headers and the implementation pays to test and invert. No secret inversion is used by the honest issuer.

The issuer samples uniform a and independent ternary polynomials t,e and sets Y=at+e. Choose I uniformly among the first m₀≤1024 coefficients, and take x=Y_I. The public header contains a,I,Y strictly before I, j=1_{x mod 8∈{0,1,6,7}}, and a 16-bit nonzero mask r (replace the all-zero draw by the first basis vector). Put V=floor((x+4j)/8) modulo 65536 and K=⟨r,V⟩ over F₂. Send b XOR K. Anyone with the secret can use (at)_I since the shift leaves a one-unit safety margin, including wraparound. The issuer knows K directly from the body, so the grants below never need t. In the ideal body each upper word is uniform even given the prefix and j, by adding multiples of 8. Nonzero r makes K unbiased conditioned on the full header. This establishes the ideal hybrid on one challenge. The original eight-row form uses eight uniform-secret rows, chooses among their 8192 coefficients and costs 311308 raw bits at maximum.

## Why fewer prefix coefficients suffice

For each position let P contain a and all previous Y, j be a deterministic function of Y at the position, and V the reconciled word. V is a function of Y and is recovered from (t,P,j) everywhere on the real support. Therefore H(V|P,j)=I(t;V|P,j)≤I(t;Y|P,j)≤I(t;Y|P). The last inequality here uses that j is a function of Y, via the chain rule; conditional mutual information need not decrease under arbitrary extra conditioning. Summing telescopes to at most H(t). PrefixCommonInformation.one and total prove the one-step bound and the entire history inequality for arbitrary finite variables, retaining arbitrary correlations with a. The public index picks the average term. The same proof applies to the uniform-secret eight-row form, giving H(V|header before r)≤19/8, with no extra noise-entropy term.

For the ternary single row, H(t)≤1024 log₂3. If c denotes the expected probability of equality of two independent posterior words, c≥2^(−1024 log₂3/m₀). This follows by Jensen on each posterior and on the distribution of headers; posterior proves the average inequality for finite PMFs. Before replacing the zero hash mask the expected agreement is (1+c)/2: equal words always agree, and for a nonzero difference toggling a mask coordinate in its support pairs agreements with disagreements. Repairing the zero mask changes agreement by at most 2^-16. An independent approximate conditional zero-body sampler of average bit TV η on the *real* header changes real agreement by at most η. The agreement in the uniform world is exactly 1/2 for any sampler even if its behavior changes on those headers. PrefixSamplerTest proves the finite weighted hash and perturbation inequalities. Thus the test gap at the normalized challenge is ≥c/2−2^-16−η. The reduction computes K from its challenge and samples its comparison independently, without a conditioned-key oracle.

## Witness grants with a simulator for the complete transcript

On an undirected public graph with terminals s,t, use independent uniform vertex bits d_v. For each edge publish d_u XOR d_v and send K XOR d_s XOR d_t. A witness walk XORs its edge labels and recovers exactly K, hence b. A NO certificate for the proof is the component of s: toggling all its private bits leaves every edge correction unchanged and toggles the final mask. The issuer never computes this component. Given any joint law of the real or ideal prefix, phase and masked message, an efficient simulator samples the grant for zero using fresh vertex bits. PrefixWalkGrant.joint_sim is equality of the whole resulting law. A serialization may store each undirected edge once, using |E|+1 extra bits and |V| private coins for this layer. Directed reachability alone fails, e.g. s→a←t gives the same cancellations despite the lack of a directed path from s to t.

For a short grant on large implicit state sets, encode a state x by a public bijection e into a finite F₂-vector space X. Public generator map L:W→X acts by x↦e⁻¹(e(x)+Lw). The accepting witness w transports the given x to y. Set Δ=e(y)−e(x), draw a uniform dual k and publish (k∘L,K+k(Δ)) in canonical basis coordinates. Each accepting w opens K by subtracting (k∘L)(w). On NO, Δ is outside range L, hence there exists v annihilating L and evaluating to one at Δ. Translate k by K v to identify the complete grant with a grant of zero. ConjugatePhaseGrant proves existence, law equality for any field, the nonlinear transporter equivalence, and joint simulation even with arbitrary correlated public fields. One example is the invertible shear e(u,z)=(u,z+f(u)) for any public NAND function f; a generator adding w to u updates z by f(u)−f(u+w). For 127 generator bits only 128 raw grant bits are needed even when there are 2^128 possible states. For X of dimension d, sampling costs d fair bits. Computing all k∘L entries for g generators by dense products costs at most dg bit products plus the corresponding XORs, with another dot product for Δ. The witness uses a single g-bit dot product. The public linear system after applying e decides the endpoint language; allowing general f on specified endpoints does not encode existential NAND satisfiability.

A single capsule phase may serve several alternative accepting routes. Sample an independent k for each. Theorem shared proves that on NO the *joint vector of all routes* is generated by grants of zero, even after including the entire masked payload in the public front. Any one successful witness opens the shared K. In particular specialize a 4096-NAND circuit by guessing one input at each residual nonlinear gate. For each guessed branch, require its guessed inputs to match the affine wire expressions and require output one. With the branch fixed these checks give L_v w=δ_v on the *same* 128 primary witness bits. A satisfying assignment computes its own branch. On NO all branches are inconsistent. This implements the promised grant without embedding the actual secret or invoking a general WE primitive. With k guessed controls the layer costs at most 129·2^k bits, compared with 258·2^k for two separate quarter-bit grants. With a 512-coordinate prefix the total is 1,082,159,624 raw bits at k=23 and 2,164,290,056 at k=24. The latter exceeds 2^32 bits after ordinary 2n+1 framing. On YES the witness guarantee covers any satisfying assignment. It does not impose soundness on attempted openings with a wrong witness when a valid one exists. Some circuits require many guessed branches, and cheap linear solving is also available per leaf. The construction establishes a real conditional-sampling separation on this specified relation and circuit subclass; general capped WE and native machine clocks remain open.

On an actual YES statement the grant and any valid witness deterministically compute the correct zero body on every positive-mass augmented header, a perfect conditional sampler given that witness. On an actual NO statement, a sampler for the augmented real header can be simulated on a bare header by appending a fresh independent grant of zero before calling it. This preserves its average conditional TV bound by convexity, as the phase posterior given the grant equals that given the bare header. Thus the same challenge decision gap holds for a sampler for the augmented law, charged for producing the simulator's grants. A direct distinguisher between complete message views is handled by replacing the lattice challenge by its ideal law, whose phase is independent fair even after simulated grants. The YES grant's conditional law depends on the secret only through the actual K, using fresh coins. Separate payloads reusing K would expose their XOR; the shared construction routes a single payload.

For the coarse two-bit variant, each x has helper j determined by its residue modulo D=2^17, with shift jD/2 putting its remainder in [D/4,3D/4). RingPrefixArithmetic.coarse_exact proves simultaneous correctness for every signed displacement of magnitude at most 32768. Choose a random bit position in all 2048 upper bits and publish previous bits and all helpers. Rounding each transmitted a coefficient to B=32 gives deterministic tolerance 16383 beyond dot-product rounding and the unit error. This costs 17419 raw bits. At B=128 it costs 15371 bits: per-coordinate Hoeffding for independent centered trits bounds dot-product reconstruction failure by 2 exp(−32767²/(2·1024·64²))<2^-183. The coarse entropy bound for the selected bit is log₂(3)/2 (plus binary entropy of the prediction error in the probabilistic case), giving conditional agreement ≥1/√3 in the exact case. An issuer that computes K on its *original full row* and uses one of the exact grants above recovers K from the witness even if the rounded dot-product predictor errs. Such a failure probability still matters when using that predictor to bound real conditional entropy. These are mathematical estimates, beyond the formal arithmetic margin and lengths. A 76-attempt bounded sampler per ideal trit (reject one two-bit symbol and fall back to zero) introduces joint distance at most 2048·4^-76=2^-141; charge it per affected draw and transport conditional laws through joint bounds rather than asserting pointwise posterior closeness.

The following replay checks two alternative inconsistent affine branches with one witness coefficient each. It also demonstrates why the independent coin requirement matters: when their masks are reused the two supports become disjoint. The final loop checks both reconciliation endpoint radii at all 524288 field residues.

```python
from collections import Counter
from itertools import product
ip = lambda x,y: (x & y).bit_count() % 2
for h in (0,1):
    full, reused = Counter(), Counter()
    for k,l in product(range(4), repeat=2):
        g = (ip(k,1), h^ip(k,2), ip(l,2), h^ip(l,1))
        full[g] += 1
        if k == l:
            reused[g] += 1
    if h == 0:
        ref, bad = full, reused
    else:
        assert full == ref and set(bad).isdisjoint(reused)

for x in range(1 << 19):
    for d in (8, 131072):
        h = int(x%d < d//4 or x%d >= 3*d//4)
        at = lambda y: ((y+h*(d//2)) % (1<<19))//d
        margin = 1 if d == 8 else 32768
        assert at(x-margin) == at(x) == at(x+margin)
```
