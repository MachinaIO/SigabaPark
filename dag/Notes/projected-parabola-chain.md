For a public linear map M of the four NAND row weights, a lane publishes the pair (Mr,M(r²+b1)) for four independent field elements r and a share b. These are the full two columns, so every linear combination a recipient can form is present. The Lean histogram counts all seeds of each complete output. Its two shifted laws have statistical distance below one precisely when every coordinate is nonconstant on the kernel of M, for odd finite fields with more than four elements. The proof avoids at most four scalar values to construct a kernel vector v with no zero coordinates. Taking s=(1−v²)/(2v) coordinatewise gives identical headers on the seeds s+v and s in the two worlds. Conversely a coordinate fixed on the kernel is determined by Mr, and it also determines the corresponding squared coordinate plus the share from the second public column. For a normalized affine projection S, a coordinate functional in the row span is a vertex eᵢ in S: its coordinate sum is one, so a linear combination of normalized points representing it is already affine.

The complete independent gate and lane headers with bits XORed to the message are parity mixtures. Their signed difference factors and their exact statistical distance is the product of the individual distances. This is xor_distance, with repeated composition in packet_distance. A vertex appearing in every local projection exposes every share, even when those vertices require different global tableaux. A rank-three four-column matrix whose kernel is a full-support line can have overlap at most (p−1)/p³: on each nonzero kernel displacement the difference of squared columns imposes three independent linear equations on a uniform seed. Hence strict overlap alone does not certify a useful concrete hiding bound.

The scalar comparison uses i.i.d. coefficient noise with law μ on the same cyclic group in both circuits. The signed gap-two representative adds one independent sample and the reflection of another to the honest G-fold sum. In Fourier coordinates the magnitude of the change is |μ̂|ᴳ(1−|μ̂|²). Parseval and Cauchy–Schwarz bound the distance by √(q−1)/(G+2) times (G/(G+2))^(G/2), including after any common independent terminal noise. For 4096 terms modulo 257 this is below 0.001437. The numerical verifier checked 2475 small distributions. This comparison only concerns the scalar decoded statistic.

In odd characteristic the signed three-row residual is −2(a−c)(b−c), independent of the uniform first coordinate after an invertible linear substitution. Its full signed-row header is a uniform plane of weight 1−1/p plus a uniform shifted parabola of weight 1/p. Between bits this gives distance 1/p. The full-rank-two non-singleton example has row line (1,−x,x−1,1). Its two intercepts and slopes give all responses. If c−b is nonzero, the slopes reveal b,c, and the remaining random coordinates reduce to a+d and (a−d)²/2 plus the share. The two translated square laws have overlap (p−1)/(2p): pairs with t²−u² equal to a nonzero constant are in bijection with p−1 nonzero values of t−u. Each overlapping output counts twice its minimum fiber size because one squared fiber has size two and the other has size one or two. For zero slope the signed-row calculation gives distance 1/p. The complete line distance is (p²+1)/(2p²), verified by exact enumeration for p=3,5,7,11. These closed-form counts and the scalar Fourier bound are paper derivations and finite checks, while the general full-view support criterion and XOR identity are Lean proofs.

The 4096-gate obstruction includes one unused gate and the chain g₀=NOT x, gⱼ=NAND(x,gⱼ₋₁) for j=1 through 4092, followed by the two NANDs computing x AND g₄₀₉₂. The registered evalGates output is always false. Set x=true, flip the output of any selected internal gate once and propagate its flipped suffix. Now the output is true and every other Boolean NAND equation holds. The changed gate has normalized affine rows (2−a−b−c,b+c−1,a+c−1,1−c) for its Boolean parent and child values a,b,c, preserving all three wire margins. Duplicate-parent NOT gates still have genuine rows. Thus each group missing any internal gate has a simultaneous all-unit restriction in some globally feasible affine tableau. A grouping scheme exposing its share from such a restriction loses all parity hiding if each group omits at least one chain gate. This concerns the original affine relaxation and independent group headers that admit these local readouts. Stronger synchronized constraints across the groups can alter the feasible set.

For p=257, 129 lanes improve the displayed rank-two line below the 128-bit target. A fixed-clock fair-bit machine cannot generate an exactly uniform element of an odd field. Reducing a uniform 160-bit integer modulo 257 gives an exact mixture of the field-uniform law with an exceptional atom at zero of weight 2⁻¹⁶⁰ because 2¹⁶⁰ is one modulo 257. Replacing all 2113536 draws in a fixed packet changes each message law by at most 2113536·2⁻¹⁶⁰ by coupling. Twice this allowance plus the 129th power of the line distance is below 0.502·2⁻¹²⁸. Four rows and two nine-bit columns per lane yield at most 38043648 public payload bits. The field draws consume 338165760 fair bits plus the independent XOR share splits.
