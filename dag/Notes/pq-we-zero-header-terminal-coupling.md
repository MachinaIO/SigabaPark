## Selective single-path word law

For each coordinate i let P0,P1 be bits from the two physical control keys on a word-specific address. Different slots, rows, words, coordinates, gate ports and cryptographic roles have distinct, unambiguously encoded domains. The semantic normal result is y and the encryptor's override word is v. Let h=1 when physical control 0 selects the normal branch. Use the same slope G for the whole word. Choose pi_i=v_i XOR P0_i XOR P1_i, S_i=(if h then pi_i else v_i) XOR P0_i, and put g_i(u)=S_i XOR (u AND G) into the normal label of physical color u. Its independent outbound PRF key part is sampled separately. The semantic-to-physical orientation is u_i=y_i XOR pi_i. Send C=h XOR G. The receiver on physical row p evaluates g_i XOR (u_i AND (C XOR p)) XOR Pp_i. This equals y_i if p XOR h is true, and v_i otherwise. Taking G=h gives C=0 identically and needs no public header field.

For any fixed p,h,G,y,v and active pad word a, let r be the inactive pad word. The map from r to the whole selected color word u=y XOR v XOR a XOR r is an involution, including when y or v have repeated coordinates. For each r, the *pair of words* (u,g) is identically (u, out XOR (u AND (C XOR p)) XOR a). Thus for uniform r, u is uniform over words and g is exactly the public deterministic reconstruction. With random independent G, C is uniform for fixed h; one first samples C, then u. With G=h, use C=0 and the same coupling, even though the hidden slope is shared across coordinates. One can condition on the active control key and its entire function, treating a as determined. Further conditioning on ordinary active key parts independently indexed by physical color also preserves the law. Conditioning on upstream observations of r, revealing both g values, or requesting another normal opening requires a different argument. The Lean companion gives the bijective coupling and decoder equality for arbitrary index type; the equal probabilities follow by finite uniform counting. This avoids assuming word independence merely from marginal uniformity.

## Embedding conditions

For static inputs, first determine the active *semantic* path, without computing any programmed physical colors. Select the control orientation independently of the pad answers and sample ordinary outbound base keys independently at both physical colors. Use the independent-key four-row garbling in which a masked cell is the XOR of a payload and independent per-port PRF pads under the physical parent keys on public distinct addresses; distinguish both ports even for repeated parents. Replace pads from inactive wire keys in upstream topological order. Before substituting an unknown inactive key for the next challenge, every incoming row that would deliver its key bits must already have an independent, exclusive random pad and can be generated uniformly; the unique active row delivers the other key. For an input key, its unpublished alternative must be simulatable from the input-seed/transfer hybrid. Compute active rows as encryptions of their true active payload under both known active pads. Treat these rows as data correlated with their delivered payload and reproduce them in the simulator. For cells with at least one hidden ideal pad, sample the masked strings uniformly, preserving consistency on replay.

At the inactive control challenge, query the oracle for the whole role-separated terminal word in addition to all needed outbound gate-row pads. Both real PRF answers and random answers suffice for the program that sets pi and g; upstream active rows that produce carrier data can be built using those answers, known input labels, and known active pads. The unchanged unexposed control key must never be passed as data to a domain, serialized into a clear record, or derived from a revealed seed. If addresses themselves contain a challenge key or the inactive control key doubles as a carrier base key or an opened PRF seed, the outlined oracle embedding is unavailable. Physical indices are public labels, not encodings of the hidden key. Control generation and its active orientation must not depend on the programmed normal colors. In particular do not make a challenge out of a base seed selected using its own challenge answers. For other hidden ordinary keys, the physical inactive index may depend on the two known or simulated control pad words; its ordinary base key still has the independent required law. Use independent control and ordinary key material. Costs and security losses count each actual hidden-key replacement at the running time and query count of the enclosing reduction, plus collision and transfer losses. These are conditions on a proposed complete circuit, not a proof for arbitrary BKS key queries, shared non-independent master keys, coherent oracle access, or registered WE.

## Returned wires and accounting

[Brakerski, Komargodski and Segev, Figure 6](https://eprint.iacr.org/2015/158.pdf) returns the supplied v_i's on its exceptional branch, and serializes fresh lower-arity encryptions with PRF-derived coins on its ordinary branch. Figure 6 specifies algorithms, not a bit-level compiler or a final-gate netlist. A final bit output by an independent ordinary gate is eligible if both output records can have their orientation/payload programmed; replace those records in its producer cells. A nonce copied from its coin string, a public shape constant, a directly copied selected input label with fixed color, or the same output wire aliased twice cannot automatically supply such two fresh records. For example, two aliases force the XOR of their active physical colors to zero; uniform independent ideal pads instead give probability one half. Constant propagation or explicit reuse when both whole branch functions agree is a separate optimization. Free-XOR imposes an orientation relation on XOR-derived outputs and needs a different proof. If a normal output feeds other gates, preserve its independent full-strength outbound keys, program the semantic orientation consistently in *all* consuming tables, and charge the added field in all existing producer cells. Acyclic dataflow still needs control-independent domain generation. Choosing an unused bit of a nominal k-bit key to hold g leaves only k-1 unconstrained bits; use a proved (k-1)-bit family at actual composed security, or enlarge the label.

A fresh two-input four-row terminal producer emitting just (u,g) costs eight masked payload bits even without ordinary outbound keys; a unary two-row identity costs four. For an already required four-row producer with independently writable color and a genuine spare bit the incremental terminal field costs zero. If one extra bit is appended, the same producer adds four bits. Its original cells remain present even when surgery has zero incremental size. Garbler pad computation costs two terminal word streams (2L one-bit oracle evaluations if called per coordinate), receiver one stream (L such calls); batching must use an appropriately long PRF output and its real running time. At least one genuine normal producer incurs its own payload: for example one two-input four-row producer emitting a k-bit outbound key plus (u,g) serializes 4(k+2) bits, in addition to any other previously charged operations. With k=128 this alone is 520 bits. Charging that output once per 2^13 final entries is 4,259,840 bits. Even adding the old optimistic five front fields of width 23 at 64 bits each gives 2,688,614,400 bits before remaining producers, primitive operations, or machine clocks. These numbers are selected partial ledger terms.

If disjoint additional unary producers are required for at least f times the returned word length at every application, each costs at least four bits; serial output expansion has factor at least 4f per application, or 8f for disjoint binary four-row producers. A nontrivial bound on a repeated chain needs 4f>1 (respectively 8f>1); for k applications multiply the actual layer factors, k=2^(r-1)-1 in the literal fold. For f≥3/4 (respectively f≥3/8) this alone restores the ternary floor. This accounts only disjoint physical bits; when an otherwise-required producer is counted for its full cost it cannot be counted again as incremental. The source supplies no count f for an instantiated FE encoder. No full cost below either cap, compiled WE, registered PRF security reduction, or complete enclosing adaptive FE hybrid has been established.
