## Publish one sign and one linear functional

Use binary coordinates. A complete candidate trace w contains the 128 primary bits and first 4095 output bits. Substitute constant one for the constant wire, invalid predecessor reads, and the required terminal output. For each gate define the Boolean quadratic residual e_g(w)=1+z_g+a_g b_g, with repeated inputs reduced by x²=x. A trace is a valid opening exactly when all residuals vanish. In particular every Boolean vector w gives a meaningfully defined frequency without an additional Booleanity check.

Internally select s uniformly over the binary residual indices. Write q_s=sum_g s_g e_g. Complete one quadratic pivot at a time to obtain

    q_s(w)=c+v_0·w+sum_{j=1}^h L_j(w) M_j(w),

where L_j,M_j are linear and h is half the rank of the alternating polar form. At a cross term xy+xA+yB+W (A,B affine on the other variables), complete (x+B)(y+A)+W+AB. Separate all constant and linear pieces and continue on the remaining coordinates. This yields the claimed rank count by splitting an independent symplectic plane at every step. This step makes the earlier public quadratic-counting pivot useful for encryption without enumerating the 2^4096 selector masks.

Choose independent fair bits a_j,b_j. Set v=v_0+sum_j(a_j L_j+b_j M_j) and set U=c+sum_j a_j b_j. For every w the signed expectation is 2^-h (-1)^q_s(w), because the one-plane average of (-1)^(ab+aL+bM) is (-1)^(LM)/2. Choose any efficiently certified h_*≥h for every selector. With probability 2^{h-h_*} retain the sign U; otherwise add an independent fair bit to it. This is a bounded fair-coin experiment. Emit only (U+payload,v); keep s, the success flag and the individual draws private. Averaging over the uniform selector gives the exact complete odd-character profile

    E[(-1)^(U+v·w)]=2^-h_* · 1[all e_g(w)=0].

On a NO every anchored frequency vanishes, so the whole public distribution equals its payload translate. Every genuine witness yields scalar TV exactly 2^-h_* and a sign decoder correct on both messages with probability (1+2^-h_*)/2. With a unique accepting complete assignment Fourier inversion implies equality of the scalar and whole-packet variations. No cryptographic premise is needed. The sampler sends 4224 binary symbols including the anchor in the uncompressed general case. It needs 4096 selector bits, 2h local pivot bits, up to h_*−h thinning bits and one independently fair fallback bit; the private computations include forming and eliminating an at most 4223-square binary polar matrix. A Gaussian elimination bound in elementary bit operations is polynomial, but the registered tape machine, clock and description size are unproved.

## Rank and wiring

Always h_*≤2111 is available since there are 4223 free bits. A better public bound is the maximum matching size of the graph whose vertices are free variables and whose edges are the distinct nonlinear parent pairs, dropping affine equations. Indeed the polar is an alternating matrix supported on this graph. For completeness, elimination on a nonzero two-by-two principal pivot finds a nonsingular principal submatrix of size equal to the rank, by induction on the Schur complement and the block determinant formula. In characteristic two the determinant of that submatrix is the sum over perfect matchings of the squared edge-weight products. In the permutation expansion any term containing a cycle of length at least three cancels with the term obtained by reversing the first such cycle; fixed points vanish on the diagonal. Thus a rank-2h matrix requires a matching of size h. A computed vertex cover is another elementary upper bound on h since a star contributes polar rank at most two. If all legal samples have h≤1 with a certified uniform envelope h_*≤1, scalar recovery meets 3/4. In bounded-matching cases a maximal matching also gives a small vertex cover: branch on its at most twice-as-many endpoints and solve the remaining affine equations. Thus the restricted good decoder does not establish a new reduction for hard general instances.

It is important to compare with a simpler all-circuit reference. Evaluate a uniformly sampled 128-bit input x. Sample v independently uniformly. If the evaluation accepts with trace w(x), set U=v·w(x); otherwise choose U independently fair. Orthogonality gives exactly 2^-128 at every accepting trace and zero elsewhere. Thus the unstructured 2^-2111 rank envelope by itself is a weaker universal guarantee. The Walsh method improves the reference on structured low-rank circuits. For publicly recognized quadratic constraints directly in the 128 primary bits, without free intermediate wires, h_*≤64 always suffices; verifying exact correspondence of those constraints to the original circuit is necessary.

## Pay for active stars instead of the worst rank

Assign each nonlinear gate to one of its two parent variables and group the selector bits by this chosen center. Let m be the number of selectors in these nonempty groups and d_j their group sizes. The remaining affine selectors can still be independent fair bits. For each group give its all-zero selector pattern integer weight one and every other pattern weight two. Its total is W_j=2^{d_j+1}−1. Let H be the least power of two at least product W_j. A uniform index below H either falls in surplus (emit an independent fair anchor and any v), or unpacks into group-pattern digits and internal offsets. For a fixed selector s the offsets form 2^a positions with a the number of active stars. Its polar is a sum of a star polars, each of rank at most two, even when multiple gates have the same parents. Thus h≤a.

Choose the h pivot pairs as above. For the first 2^h offsets keep their predicted sign, giving an effective factor 2^-h, and for all other offsets draw a fair anchor. The selector's signed contribution after summing its offsets has magnitude 1/H exactly. Averaging over the independent affine selectors leaves flat amplitude 2^m/H on precisely the satisfying traces. All constants and sampling masks are public algorithms. The values of all private selector digits stay hidden. Mixed-radix unranking, pivot evaluation, and public wire serialization must all be paid for; the dyadic H avoids an unbounded rejection loop.

In the connected AND-of-128 example with 3842 unary padding gates, the 127 nonlinear parent pairs are disjoint. Affine padding and the second NAND in each AND contribute no polar rank. Thus there are 127 singleton stars, H the next power of two above 3^127, and 2^127/H=2^-75. This is the exact whole-packet TV on that unique-witness instance. The bounded sampler improves both 2^-128 random witness checking and 2^-1182 from matching adjacent raw NAND blocks, while remaining far from the requisite 1/2. Choosing a public cover C for some edges and applying singleton tilting to t remaining selectors gives another bound 2^{t-|C|-ceil(t log2 3)} using the same offset argument with an independent 2^{-|C|} factor. Taking the preferable certified construction per public circuit preserves exact NO equality.

An exhaustive check on 150 small quadratic systems reconstructed every anchored coefficient, including signs, under the uniform-selector sampler. For 180 further graph and star partitions with at most eight edges, enumeration of every selector pattern checked r≤2a, norm product W_j, and the identical signed prefactor for each pattern. No standard or new hardness assumption is used. Constant correctness on general registered circuits, a better signed sampling envelope beyond these certified decompositions, and a registered machine implementation remain open.

### Statistical version of the public-syndrome comparison

For comparison, in the public linear syndrome construction from the first note a feasible rank-r congruence system gives E_h |phi_h|²=2^-r. Conditional scalar variation is at least |phi_h|, which is at least |phi_h|². Therefore even statistical NO error strictly below 1/8 excludes every feasible rank-at-most-three candidate. Given a feasible frequency, the conditional scalar comparison is efficient: solve Hr=h for one representative and evaluate the phase on one representative of each coset of U in K. There are exactly 2^r such cosets. All U-shifts preserve the phase on every fiber by the defining congruences. The resulting four integer counts give the optimal test, and their likelihood decision attains the stated variation. This also rules out a candidate under computational NO privacy when the frequency and public linear algebra fit the adversary budget; the scalable assertion does not certify the concrete registered adversary cost. In an independent finite enumeration 1,788 choices of anchored frequency and rank threshold under 72 seeded small D,K instances had exactly the same answer from the congruences as from exhaustive ciphertext histograms (1,010 positives). This computational comparison is specific to the small syndrome quotient and does not apply to every nonzero high-rank bilinear frequency.
