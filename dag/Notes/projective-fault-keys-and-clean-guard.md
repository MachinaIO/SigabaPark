## Two trace domains and their full descriptions

Take p=4099, R=F_p[X]/(X^N+1), N a power of two, and at most G=4096 Boolean NAND gates. Use one left opening for locally legal gate triples and one right opening for a wiring-consistent trace with the final output pinned to one. Missing inputs take the registered constant. A genuine witness generates both openings with the same triples by ordinary evaluation. Every purported right opening specifies all primary bits and all free gate values, so it can be checked even when the statement is false. On a common legal opening every fault indicator j_i=(z_i+x_i y_i-1)^2 vanishes. On a right opening these are bits, and c=sum j_i in F_p is zero exactly when the opening is clean. The cubic Boolean expansion is 1-z_i-x_i y_i+2x_i y_i z_i. False instances contain no clean right opening. These interfaces neither expose a sampler for valid witnesses nor assume that the two openings chosen by an adversary agree.

Write h(f) for the constant coefficient of a ring element. To issue a bit beta, choose independent uniform A,t,T_1,...,T_G in R, one ring error with independent CBD(1) coefficients, and let e be its constant coefficient. Define W=h(A t)+e+2049 beta in F_p. The complete left key contains t; its value on every legal opening is t. The complete right key contains A, all G field residues

    V_i = h(A(t+T_i)) + e + 2049 beta,

and a guard array F specified below. Both keys and the statement publicly fix all evaluators, bases and auxiliary maps. There is no other random tape in the keys. On a right opening with c nonzero, return A and the scalar sum_i (j_i/c) V_i. This is exactly h(A K)+e+2049 beta for the latent ring key K=t+sum_i (j_i/c)T_i. Only the clean left token is issued; K at a faulty opening is not handed out separately. All nonzero-count inputs work simultaneously, with the original scalar error even when some residues j_i/c have large centered representatives. On a clean opening the right evaluator returns A and an evaluation of F equal to W. With t the honest decryptor then subtracts h(A t) and rounds the residue to the closer of 0 and 2049 on the cycle of length 4099. The two radius-one neighborhoods are disjoint. This is deterministic for every valid witness and every permitted encryption coin string under the guarded construction below. Standalone higher-rank separated expansions of the reciprocals are unnecessary for this joint key format.

## The only exceptional measurement

For c nonzero the normalized feature (1,j/c) is the homogeneous vector (c,j) scaled by a unit. At c=0 the homogeneous vector vanishes; the normalized convention inserts exactly g0=(1,0,...,0). Accordingly the span over all right openings equals the homogeneous span plus the line of g0 precisely when a clean opening occurs (otherwise only the homogeneous span). In the ambient feature space all homogeneous rows satisfy first coordinate equals the sum of the others, while g0 does not. The homogeneous part itself admits complete span computation without deciding validity: after wiring substitution its coordinates are Boolean polynomials of degree at most three. Values at raw traces of Hamming weight at most three span every vector value, by taking finite differences for its square-free coefficients and evaluating its multilinear expansion. This is a polynomial bound with a large cubic factor. It does not handle the exceptional direction. Any public efficient algorithm returning an exact full normalized span uniformly for all statements gives a SAT test by this one linear functional. This is a computational implication, not a lower bound asserting a complexity separation. The Lean annihilator theorem covers arbitrary homogeneous families satisfying the zero condition.

## A complete clean guard and its converse

Choose a public finite coordinate dictionary with a linear measurement ell_w in its dual for each right opening. Fix a public vector g representing 1 (ell_w(g)=1), and let a public matrix M represent functions vanishing at every clean opening (ell_w M=0 there). Its columns may be signed constraint polynomials or valid factored functions. The issuer samples a uniform field column r and emits the **collected coefficient vector** F=g W+M r. The evaluator uses ell_w(F). Emitting the separated secret coefficients or a program containing W literally would change the transcript. No decision about reachability is part of this issuer.

On a NO instance with g in im M, the entire F is a uniform image sample independent of W, A, t, the common error and every V_i. For the full-row comparison use one ordinary RingLWE.decision sample (alpha,b) with uniform ring secret s, CBD(1) error, m=1 at the same public modulus and N for every statement. Set A=alpha, sample t,t_i independently and uniformly, set V_i=h(alpha(t+t_i))+h(b)+2049 beta, and sample F=M r independently. Conditional on s the conceptual T_i=t_i+s are jointly independent uniform. Thus the real challenge produces the exact joint issuer for this NO, including all correlations and all repeated headers. The ideal challenge gives one common uniform scalar h(b), whose shift by 2049 beta does not change the joint distribution. The two real message worlds have distinguishing gap at most twice the maximum one-challenge gap (plus bounded-sampling errors when implemented). There is no G-fold hybrid and no statement-dependent challenge law. The proof of the whole transcript is on paper. The Lean nodes verify the weighted aggregation, annihilator, translation criterion and dual extraction.

Conversely if g lies outside im M, a public dual f annihilating the columns with f(g)=1 recovers W=f(F) for every key and all mask coins. Subtracting h(A t) and rounding recovers beta even on a false statement. Gaussian elimination computes f from the public matrix. Hence an uncovered NO defeats this precise key format independently of Ring-LWE hardness. A small public M covering every NO while its columns vanish on all clean openings, and allowing the required unit measurement on YES, would decide general SAT by testing membership of g in im M. Arbitrary depth of circuit evaluation in a public dictionary does not exempt its finite coefficient matrix from this condition.

## Exact direct sampling of the projected bodies

For A nonzero, T to h(A T) is surjective onto F_p even when A is a zero divisor. A nonzero coefficient of A paired with the complementary signed monomial in this negacyclic basis gives a nonzero scalar. Conditional on such A the V_i are therefore **independent uniform field elements**, independent of W and t. At A=0 all V_i=W. This gives a smaller exact direct honest sampler: draw A and t, compute h(A t) by one signed dot product of length N, draw e, then draw G uniform scalars if A is nonzero or replicate W otherwise. Sample guard-mask coins and form F. No individual T_i or ring products for the G slots are needed. For covered NOs the complete two-key statistical distance is at most p^(-N), by conditioning on A. This argument even supplies statistical privacy in this restricted class without relying on the lattice estimate. The reduction above separately explains how the correlated headers and noise arise under the fixed ordinary assumption. Direct evaluation of registered RingLWE.H with m=1, q=4099, uniform secret and CBD(1) error gives 418, 801, 1569 for N=512,1024,2048. These are estimator outputs for that one-row input, not certified end-to-end machine strength.

## A connected capped pair with globally irredundant constraints

Use 4093 successive self-NAND gates z_i=NOT(z_(i-1)), with z_0=x one of the 128 input bits and y=z_4093. Finish with a=NAND(x,y), b=NAND(y,x), out=NAND(a,b) pinned to one. Every gate leads to the output; unused primary slots are legal. This is false since y=1-x. Every one of the 4096 gate checks is indispensable: fix x=1; a single flipped NOT evaluation followed by an honest suffix makes y=1 and admits all terminal checks. For a failure at a or b follow the honest chain (y=0), set just that terminal input to zero and the other to one. For a failure at out take a=b=1. Thus no proper subset of the individual checks refutes the trace. The positive sibling changes just b to NAND(y,y), accepting x=0. The algorithms use the same layout on both.

Take columns d_i=z_(i-1)+z_i-1 in F_p, zero on an honest chain, and indicators of the locally inconsistent four-bit patterns (x,y,a,b) for the three final checks. Canonical evaluation emits the 4092 interior coefficients r_i+r_(i+1) and a table on the 16 boundary patterns, with values W-sum r_i+r_1 x+r_4093 y plus the independent pad on each locally inconsistent pattern. This represents exactly W+sum r_i d_i plus tail pads. On the negative sibling the alternating sum of d_i is S=x+y-1. The only locally consistent boundary pattern is (1,1,0,0), and there S=1. Thus 1=S+(1-S), where the latter function belongs to the span of the local bad-pattern indicators. This is the required full-range certificate, even though every gate has a singleton-failure opening. At a clean positive sibling opening all columns vanish, and evaluation gives W. The full construction supports exponentially many raw trace guesses with no trace enumeration. This promised family also has an easy public decision algorithm.

At N=1024, fixed-width packing uses 13(2N+4096+4092+16)=133276 raw bits for both keys together. A direct implementation needs one N-term dot product, O(G) field operations for coefficient and tail generation, plus statement parsing, sampling and packing. Decryption verifies the trace and evaluates at most G indicators plus a linear guard. With at most 150 independent 13-bit rejection trials per uniform residue, defaulting to zero on failure, each draw differs from exact uniform by less than 2^-150. There are at most 10253 draws (two ring vectors, G projected bodies, 4093 chain masks and at most 16 tail masks). The joint error is below 2^-136 per actual encryption; uniform defaults preserve YES correctness. CBD(1) for the one scalar costs two bits. These field-operation and raw-size counts do not certify the registered Turing-machine clock or an overall quantum cost. No new hardness assumption is proposed.

A second public guard uses only eight boundary patterns (t,n,v) when the last three gates are n=NAND(t,t), v=NAND(t,n), out=NAND(v,v). The local pattern set is empty at out=1, so three gates suffice to cover the false statement; replacing the middle gate by v=NAND(n,n) gives an accepting instance whenever the upstream cone can produce t=0. The shared row and all G fault slots then cost 13(2N+4096+8)=79976 bits at N=1024. The two examples distinguish local inconsistency from a globally indispensable chain.

## Limits and related routes

For an actual 4096-gate connected false instance the scalar reciprocal can still have exact x-versus-y separation rank 2^64. Use 64 NAND pair tests on disjoint halves of the 128 primary inputs, fold and pad them into t, and finish with the three-gate inconsistent tail just described. Pin the test outputs to one, evaluate later gates honestly other than the pinned final output. The count is 1+popcount(x AND y). For each subset S the multilinear coefficient of 1/(1+popcount) is (-1)^|S|/(1+|S|), by binomial inversion. The evaluation matrix across left and right halves is the subset-inclusion matrix times this nonsingular diagonal times its transpose, hence has full rank. This excludes that exact separated scalar format, even though the shared projected row and an eight-cell guard suffice on the same false fixture. Earlier unit-slope state keys and PR 231 program independently headed edges; the repeated-header and shared-noise law here is different. The telescoping contexts of PR 232 require actual restricted coefficient descriptions. Our chain realizes that requirement by collecting interior and boundary terms. The two-trace NAND energy controls arithmetic zero testing but does not supply these guard coefficients for arbitrary statements. Neither the two keys nor the dictionary characterization close the general capped WE obligation.
