Index the 128 input bits and 4096 gate outputs by i=1,...,4224. Give the true constant index 0. During the native sequential scan alias each unavailable pin (including a reference to the current gate or beyond) to the true constant. Over F2 put X00=1, X0i=Xi0, X0,out=1 and X0z+Xab=1 per NAND z. A rank-one solution is ab^T and the anchor identity proved in Lean forces Xij=Xi0 X0j. Thus it equals (1,u)(1,u)^T and the gate equations give the unique evaluation trace of its 128 input bits. Conversely every accepting witness produces exactly this solution, including on repeated parents. Rank zero is excluded by the anchor. There are 8322 sparse rows and 17850625 matrix bits. At most 16642 incidences suffice for accumulation (two for each equality and gate, one each for the two pins). A witness reader computes the 4096 gates and can stream a dense matrix to take its inner product; it must pay to read each issued dense bit. All dimensions are algebraic counts, without a multi-tape clock proof.

Let H encode these rows with rhs d, and let (E,e) be arbitrary errors independent of uniform r. Publish C=H^T r+E,S=d·r+e+beta. Even Fourier characters are independent of beta. The odd Fourier character indexed by X is zero unless HX=d; otherwise it is (-1)^beta times the error response at X. Thus only values on this affine slice matter. The usual E=uv^T with independent fair factors and e=0 has response 2^-rank(X), including unauthorized openings. For a legal full-size false fixture use dummy constant-zero NANDs, then a zero wire from NAND(true,true), w=NAND(zero,zero), and terminal NAND(w,w). Take t equal to the honest anchored trace except set terminal to one. X=tt^T+e_w e_w^T has rank two, satisfies all rows and is cheap to describe. Its parity distinguishes the two false ciphertext laws with gap 1/4. Directly folding repeated pins into linear terms changes this fixture's relaxation.

For a universal error law with response c on ALL nonzero rank-one m-by-n matrices, zero on ranks at least two and free response a at zero, Fourier inversion gives signed mass 2^-mn(a+cK(E)), K=2^(m+n-rank(E))-2^m-2^n+1, obtained by summing signs over nonzero pairs of factors. Its variation must be at most 1; conversely any such signed mass can be realized with a sign bit by adding balanced mass. Thus inverse optimal gain equals min_t E|K-t|. For n>m the full-rank atom is a majority by the union bound (2^m-1)/2^n, and the inverse is 2^m-1. For squares m>=2, rank deficiency at least two has probability below 1/6 by counting two-dimensional kernel subspaces, while full-rank mass p=product_(i=1)^m(1-2^-i)<1/2. Thus 1 is a median of K and E|K-1|=2^(m+1)p-1. An unsigned bounded-fair sampler picks x in F2^m, on x=0 uses uniform E, otherwise samples independent uniform columns in x-perp using a pivot; gain 2^-m, exactly zero for rank at least two. Exact normalized square signed sampling may need unbounded rejection for nondyadic probabilities. A universal gain this small cannot meet the capped goal by repeating scalar openings (order c^-2 for constant advantage). The affine-slice interpretation allows arbitrary off-slice coefficients or differently signed witness characters; these bounds do not exclude such repairs.

Chatterjee, Mu and Vasudevan, *Public-Key Encryption from the Minimum Rank Problem*, [primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.03752), construct PKE for a random planted MinRank distribution. Here a NO instance supplies a prescribed circuit-dependent coset with no rank-one representative, while opening must work from every legitimate accepting witness on YES. The homogeneous constraint dual contains the concrete rank-one E00. Under uniform choice of a k-dimensional constraint dual in m-by-n matrix space, its expected count of rank-one elements is (2^m-1)(2^n-1)(2^k-1)/(2^(mn)-1); k<=8322, m=n=4225. Left/right invertible transformations, transposition and row recombination preserve that count. Their orbit is statistically far from the uniform dual in these dimensions. This by itself gives no efficient test when transforms are hidden. It proves no distributional bridge via randomizing these coordinates alone. A PKE-based replacement needs a reduction jointly sampling the arbitrary NO public view from its fixed challenge, a witness-to-opening map on YES, and explicit parameters and clocks. No MinRank assumption is introduced here.
