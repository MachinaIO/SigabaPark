## What the baseline guarantees

Work in G=(Z/4)^m with a distinguished anchor coordinate. A payload b adds 2b to the anchor. An odd-anchor Fourier mode changes sign under this shift; all even-anchor modes are unchanged. By inversion, equality of all odd-anchor coefficients to zero is necessary and sufficient for exact NO privacy. For a chosen odd y and the scalar y·F with first coefficient phi, the TV of its shifted laws is |Re phi|+|Im phi|. This follows by pairing residues 0 with 2 and 1 with 3. Under a common public header, take the conditional scalar variation and then its expectation. These arguments apply to all measurements on the joint classical transcript when the full laws agree.

For reference, take one coin column for each of the two checks v and v−y_0 per free wire, and a column 2y_0 plus the sum of three Hamming mismatches against each of the four forbidden Boolean rows per NAND. Each mismatch is v or y_0−v. Independently choose fair coin bits r and publish F=D[r]+2b e_0, with [r] the integer lift. After normalizing y_0 to one, non-Boolean wires and violated gates hit a coefficient 2, making that mode vanish. At a valid trace the number h of odd coefficients gives |phi|=2^(-h/2). For a true circuit with a unique complete accepting assignment the *entire* packet gap equals the witnessed scalar gap because only one conjugate pair of odd modes survives. For the 254-gate AND of 128 inputs followed by 3842 unary inversions the count is 12415, giving TV exactly 2^-6207. The next note gives a better correlated constructor. Here we ask if leaking linear information about the coins gives useful correctness.

## A linear helper, with the full joint transcript charged

Fix D and an arbitrary circuit-dependent public binary matrix H, let K=ker H, and reveal Hr in addition to F. The original coins r are independent uniform bits. Fix a frequency y, put t_i=y·d_i modulo four, O={i:t_i odd}, B={i:t_i=2}. For an achievable syndrome h denote phi_h=E[i^(sum t_i r_i) | Hr=h]. Set U={v in K: v_i=0 on O}. Then exactly

    E_h |phi_h|² = 0                              if some v in U has odd B-overlap,
                 = 2^(-rank(proj_O K))             otherwise.

Indeed expand the squared conditional mean as two independent uniform points of a fiber, equivalently r uniform on all coins and v uniform on K, the other point r xor v. The ratio of phases is i^(sum t_i v_i) times (-1)^(sum_{i in O} r_i v_i). Averaging r eliminates every v outside U. On U the first factor is the linear sign of B-overlap. A nontrivial sign has mean zero and a trivial one has mean one. This gives the claimed identity and the *all*-syndrome vanishing criterion, since every syndrome in the image has positive probability. The formal cosetEnergy lemma proves the general finite reindexing in additive fiber coordinates; the orthogonality step and its consequences here are mathematical proofs in this note. Independent exhaustive histograms for 512 seeded matrices and phase lists at n=1..8 were compared with the formula, including zero/full-dimensional kernels and degenerate phases.

For a legal trace of the baseline B is empty. The best syndrome-dependent scalar TV is at most sqrt(2)*2^(-rank(proj_O K)/2) by Cauchy–Schwarz. More generally this upper bound applies at every witness even when B is nonempty, with zero in the obstructed case. A gap at least 1/2 forces rank at most three. This is the threshold for equal-prior success 3/4, hence necessary if the decoder succeeds with that probability on each payload. rankThreshold formally checks the arithmetic implication.

## A uniform decision procedure even with dirty modes on YES

Let g_i be the coordinate functional v↦v_i in K*, computed by Gaussian elimination. For constant R enumerate every linearly independent list of at most R of the g_i, including the empty list. For its span T let U=T-annihilator in K. Use the following linear congruences in y over Z/4, with integer lifts on the binary vectors:

1. y_0=1; require y·d_i odd at each selected basis index.
2. Require y·d_i even at every coordinate with g_i outside T.
3. For each binary basis element v of U require y·D[v]=0 modulo four.

Parity prescriptions are linear over Z/4 after multiplication by two. The first two items say precisely that T is the span of those g_i with t_i odd. In particular U is exactly the subspace used in the energy identity. On U, odd columns are never touched; Q_y(v)=y·D[v] is an additive even phase, so checking its vanishing on the basis suffices. Thus a feasible system exists iff some normalized odd frequency with projected rank at most R has positive conditional energy. Frequencies with anchor three are conjugates and need no additional search. The systems can be solved by elementary unit pivots over Z/4 followed by binary elimination on the remaining doubled equations, retaining witnesses for consistent systems. The enumeration uses at most sum_{j=0}^R binom(N,j) linear systems for N coin positions.

If every NO has equal full (Hr,F) laws and every YES has at least one anchored scalar with syndrome-assisted TV at least 1/2, this decides SAT with R=3 in polynomial time for scalable parameters. It requires neither sound Boolean support on YES nor access to a decryption oracle. It applies even if H,D are efficiently chosen as functions of the circuit. With a randomized public setup the pointwise or averaged setup guarantees must be accounted for; the deterministic theorem fixes H,D. Under the additional condition that only true trace frequencies survive on YES, a solution is an actual trace, and its input wires give a witness. No general bounded-machine lower bound follows. The algorithm does not cover nonlinear helpers, arbitrary binary codes on the original column coins, or a decoder using more than this scalar and syndrome.

For a little more structural detail, at a genuinely Boolean trace y the stronger absence of all y+2z for nonzero z with z_0=0 implies D(U) modulo two spans y's full orthogonal hyperplane. Adding 2z preserves O, and tests whether z annihilates D(U). Every D(U) row is already orthogonal to y; if its span were too small there would be a nonzero annihilator with anchor zero. In particular at the correct T just the support-parity equations on its exterior and the anchor already determine y modulo two: any solution annihilates D(U). This stronger conclusion uses elimination of invalid characters on YES.

## A genuinely shared bilinear random mask

Here is a separate result for two shared random banks x,z over F2, possibly reused across all ciphertext coordinates. Suppose the public packet is binary-valued, its anchored bit is toggled by the payload, and at frequency y its sign is (-1)^q with q=xᵀM_y z+a_yᵀx+b_yᵀz+c_y. All coefficients are affine functions of the normalized y. Averaging first over x requires M_y z=a_y. Averaging over the kernel subsequently requires b_y in im M_yᵀ. With both conditions satisfied the magnitude is exactly 2^-rank(M_y), with sign (-1)^(b_yᵀz_0+c_y) for any solution z_0. A binary projected bit's payload TV equals this magnitude. Any element in a rank-r column span is a sum of at most r columns; this follows from choosing a column basis. The analogous result holds for the transpose. Hence a YES with rank at most r supplies two satisfying vectors with Hamming weight at most r. Enumerate every such pair (x,z) and solve M_y z=a_y, M_yᵀ x=b_y, y_0=1 as ordinary binary linear equations in y. A feasible solution of *any* rank already has nonzero character, impossible on every perfectly private NO. A successful marginal with TV≥1/2 guarantees rank≤1, giving only one support index or zero on each side to guess. This remains true with arbitrary shared bilinear masks and arbitrary efficient circuit-dependent pencils in the stated format. General quadratic masks with terms inside both banks, biased coins, and joint nonlinear decryption remain open.

## Optimal local correlated guard on independent legal frequencies

Suppose the entire odd Fourier support lies in ±y_j (1≤j≤k), with jointly independent uniform Z/4 evaluations y_j·u for uniform group element u. Let t_j denote the optimal scalar TV at each. Write p for the density of F with respect to uniform and f=(p−p_shift)/2. Fourier inversion gives f as the sum of the first harmonics in these k independent coordinates. Also E|f|≤1. Each term has the law of a fair sign times an independent equally chosen magnitude 2|Re phi_j| or 2|Im phi_j|. Conditional Jensen over the magnitudes gives E_sign |sum_j sign_j t_j| ≤ 1. Permutation averaging and another Jensen step give

    (sum_j t_j / k) E|sum_j sign_j| ≤ 1.

This is sharp. Set all t_j=t=1/E|sum sign_j| and let f=t sum_j sign(y_j·u), where sign is positive on residues 0,1, negative on 2,3. Density |f|+f is normalized; the shift negates f. Each marginal has exactly TV=t and the complete packet and its shift have disjoint support. Thus collective decoding can be much stronger than looking at a prescribed coordinate. An independent public random choice of conditional clean laws also obeys the averaged inequality. Without the clean support requirement the lemma does not apply.

The four valid NAND rows, augmented by anchor one, form a Z/4 basis (their integer determinant has magnitude one). Here E|sum of four signs|=3/2, so maximal worst-row scalar TV equals 2/3. For an exact bounded *dyadic* sampler take c=5/8 in place of 2/3 and add a 1/16 uniform density offset to |f|+f. In the independent four-coordinate chart the integer weights relative to a common denominator 4096 are 1+20 max(sum sign_j,0). Sample twelve fair bits and unrank the 256 fixed cells; transform to an original gate packet by the inverse unit matrix over Z/4. The gate has no illegal anchored coefficient because |f| is shift-even and f contains only single legal harmonics. The four marginal rows are all [1664,1664,384,384]/4096, giving each legitimate row success 13/16. All packet variation is 15/16, giving 31/32 success for its full comparison. The definition and dyadicGate theorem check the exact total, marginal and translated absolute masses; 170 toggles the high bit of all radix-four digits. The 2/3 mathematical extremizer has a non-dyadic normalizer, so the twelve-bit sampler does not attain that optimum.

This supplies a working local gate but independent per-gate convolution multiplies witnessed Fourier coefficients. In a circuit with 64 disjoint unaries-free NANDs on 128 unconstrained primary bits, followed by a constant-one output and harmless padding, choose the worst legal row for each. Its projection has TV at most sqrt(2)(2/3)^64 even if locally chosen optimal correlated clean distributions differ gate by gate; other independent factors can only shrink it. A decoder with all ciphertext coordinates is outside that conclusion. Replacing the independent draws by one global bounded draw is explored in the companion note.
