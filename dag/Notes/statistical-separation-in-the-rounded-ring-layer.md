Use the rounded source in the companion note: p is bit 6 of each of 4096 canonical body coefficients of an eight-sample rank-one Ring-LWE instance over (Z/128)[X]/(X^512+1). The following result concerns this actual high bit, not coefficient parity. It gives constant *information-theoretic* separation on at least one 4096-gate NO instance, while the fixed-decision reduction continues to give computational privacy subject to the registered premise.

For any public A put C_A={topbit(A s): s any ring element}, a subset of the 4096-bit cube of size at most 128^512=2^3584. Topbit takes the indicators of [64,127]. Except with probability 2^-512 a given random ring header has at least one odd coefficient. Conditional on such a header, any given coefficient of its product with the independent uniform secret is uniform modulo 128: its linear form in the secret coefficients has an odd coefficient, including the possible negacyclic sign. An independent CBD(1) increment flips bit 6 with probability exactly 1/128, from residues 0,63,64,127 with a specified sign, each of probability 1/4 for that sign. By linearity of expectation, no independence between flips required, real p has mean distance at most 32+4096·2^-512 from its own word topbit(A s). Therefore distance to C_A is at most 64 with probability at least 1−(32+4096·2^-512)/65 by the integer version of Markov. In the ideal law the chance is at most 2^3584 sum_{j=0}^64 binom(4096,j)/2^4096 < 2^-40. The integer Hamming-ball numerator has 472 bits. Searching this codebook can require enumerating 2^3584 candidates. This is not an efficient distinguisher at the claimed hardness level.

### Transport to the complete ciphertext of an actual false statement

Fix any d of odd weight on the 4096 gates with final bit one. Put precisely the selected gates on a chain of self-NANDs starting from the registered constant true; put a self-NAND of true at all other gates, which may be unused. Odd chain length gives final false, independent of all 128 inputs. This is a registered 4096-gate NO circuit. Every product is a square of a Boolean variable or a constant. Use the certified choice h_*=0 in the actual construction, which makes the quadratic-part phase exact.

Given a proposed trace satisfying the product checks w_g=a_g, set r_g=1+z_g+w_g. As the 4095 unpinned internal output bits vary, the possible affine-residual words are *exactly* {r:d·r=1}. Summing the equations on the chain proves inclusion. Conversely for any choice of the first 4095 residuals, the triangular equations give a unique sequence of internal outputs; the pinned last equation has exactly the value prescribed by d·r=1. One can choose the irrelevant primary inputs arbitrarily and reconstruct every product bit. Consequently on this NO instance the public augmented binary row can be evaluated as beta+p·r at *every* point of that affine hyperplane. This is (p+beta d)·r there. Those points span the full binary space because the level is one and d is nonzero. Evaluating on an affine basis including its base point recovers p+beta d uniquely by linear algebra, with no secret advice. The full quarter header gives the same native row and public A by its stated bijection.

Now average d uniformly over odd words with last bit one, independently of the ring source. For beta=0 the reconstructed vector is p and the real codebook-neighborhood test accepts with probability greater than 0.507. For beta=1, conditional on A,p, the reconstructed vector p+d is uniform on a coset of a codimension-two space. Its chance of lying in the neighborhood is at most four times the uniform probability and is less than 2^-38. Thus some fixed NO choice of d gives complete real ciphertext TV greater than 1/2. The argument constructs an ensemble, proves existence of a large-gap fixed member, and explicitly prices the exponential membership test; it makes no assertion about a particular hard coded d. All 127 eligible choices of d through eight gates were exhaustively checked against their full reachable residual sets and the affine reconstruction. The source Hamming-ball computation used exact integers. This demonstrates why equality of the *ideal* NO laws and a fixed computational reduction, rather than equality of *real* NO laws, are the relevant conclusions here.

### Initial pricing

Direct execution of the official concrete RingLWE.H at N=512, m=8, q=128, CBD(1) error, uniform secret returned 177. This is the registry's estimator value in the loaded revision and not a proof of quantum hardness. At h_*=1 odd majority of r independent unbiased trial outcomes with worst success 3/4 has error sum_{i=0}^{floor(r/2)} binom(r,i)3^i/4^r. The first odd r making this at most 2^-128 is 593 (base-two logarithm of error approximately −128.207). Independent selectors and phase draws suffice for correctness; the ring mask cancels for every such witness at every sample. At the deliberately uncompressed worst carrier length 171522 bits per trial this gives 101712546 raw bits. With independent eight-sample sources and two transitions per trial, the loss in the registered squared-advantage measure is 2 log2(1186) plus log2 K for an actual reduction-to-adversary cost ratio K. The first term is about 20.424 bits, so K≤2^28 would retain at least 128 bits; K is not certified. Sampling all other trial packets, parsing and arithmetic matter. Uniformly small h_* on all registered NANDs, native machines, a concrete cost proof and end-to-end registered WE security remain open.

For the amplified version one may use a *common* ring secret for 16 distinct eight-sample blocks inside each group, and independent secrets across groups. Native phase selectors, pivot bits and thinning coins are still independent across trials. Correctness is unchanged even in the real correlated group because every authorized phase cancels every ring pad pointwise. The ideal fixed Ring-LWE source provides independent uniform bodies conditional on all 128 group headers. Pad the last of the 38 groups and discard excess blocks. Two transitions per group suffice, with an ordinary fixed decision instance having m=128 samples, not one m=8 response copied 16 times. The quarter sampler for a whole batch can put all source and phase coins in a single hidden tail, use one fair tail bit t and publish all native anchors masked by the same t. Its J=payload+t unmasks all of them to their native payload-shifted values and is independently uniform conditional on that batch. Distinct ring headers and the data/serialization costs for every visible trial remain charged.

At fixed N=512, q=128, uniform secret and CBD(1) the core evaluation gave (sample count, H) = (8,177), (64,177), (128,177), (256,169), (512,138), (1024,115), (2048,103), (4744,76). For groups of sixteen the squared-advantage loss from 76 transitions is 2 log2(76)≈12.496 bits, leaving about 36.50 bits for log2 K if targeting 128. Sharing one secret across all 593 trials instead gives 4744 samples and H=76, insufficient even before other losses. These values are source accounting, not a measured or proved bound on K. Reusing the very same pad across trials is a distinct proposal: uniformity of that one pad does not imply independence of all resulting packets. One sufficient special case for such reuse is a global affine identity a·Q(u)+d·B_C(1,u)=1 in every u, where Q contains all the product residuals. Translating every selector s_j by a and the one shared ideal pad p by d then adds exactly the shared anchor shift in every row. Adding an affine function to q leaves its polar pivots and thinning distribution unchanged and translates its emitted row by the affine coefficients, including on a fair fallback. This certificate is checked by Boolean polynomial coefficient comparison and is not guaranteed on arbitrary NO instances.

A more economical batch uses every bit of distinct ideal body coefficients rather than discarding six of seven. At q=128 the entire seven-bit representation of an ideal coefficient consists of independent fair bits, conditional on all source headers. An m=128, N=512 instance thus supplies 458752 ideal pad bits: exactly 112 disjoint 4096-bit trial pads. Assign successive bit planes of eight-body groups to different trial indices, retaining a bit-6 trial as a projection of the full batch. In the real source the seven planes can be highly correlated and the bottom one is already uniform; none of those dependencies changes any valid witness's native phase. Use six independent fixed source instances (padding the sixth) and 12 game transitions to handle all 593 trials, leaving 177−2 log2(12)−128≈41.83 bits for log2 K in the registered metric. The bundled quarter carrier at h_*=1 now has 7686272 front digits (593 native 8320-bit rows plus six sets of 128 ring headers) and 5991156 tail digits (one mask, 593·4099 native-sampler bits, and six ordinary full-source tapes). Its length is 27354858 bits plus external framing. A whole-batch m=678 source is just large enough if using all body bits but the evaluated H there is 126, insufficient for 128. Grouping changes the honest source design and counts: these bounds are for the indicated joint sampler, not independent eight-sample sources.
